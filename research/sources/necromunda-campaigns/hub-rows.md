## Target: `## 📈 Campaign, progression & snowball control`

| Take | Game | Mechanic | Type | Description | Full write-up |
|:--:|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚙️ | **Necromunda** | Reputation Boons — a continuous, bracketed reward ladder that self-revokes | Campaign | Six Reputation brackets each grant a Boon per alignment, auto-revoked the instant Reputation drops below threshold — no separate downgrade rule needed. | [[Necromunda Campaigns#Reputation Boons — a continuous, bracketed reward ladder that self-revokes]] |
| ⭐ | **Necromunda** | House Patronage — underdog banding evolved into a spendable currency | Campaign | The N23 core rulebook's replacement for scaling "extra gang tactics": the rating gap converts into a capped, itemised currency spent on temporary-only benefits — updates [[Necromunda and Mordheim#Underdog banding]], doesn't replace it. | [[Necromunda Campaigns#House Patronage — underdog banding evolved into a spendable currency]] |
| ⚙️ | **Necromunda** | The 3× kill-ratio threshold — gating territory control behind a decisive win | Campaign | A battle only flips Territory control if the winner's OOA count is ≥3× the loser's — independently converged on by two campaign systems four years apart. Decouples "won the battle" from "the map moved." | [[Necromunda Campaigns#The 3× kill-ratio threshold — gating territory control behind a decisive win]] |
| ⚙️ | **Necromunda** | The Exploration-point race — the campaign advances on pooled player action, not the calendar | Campaign | Underhells' three phases are "Variable" length — the *table's pooled* Exploration points (not any one player's) trigger the phase change at 3× player count, with a spend-vs-save tension on the currency that's about to evaporate. | [[Necromunda Campaigns#The Exploration-point race — the campaign advances on pooled player action, not the calendar]] |
| ⭐ | **Necromunda** | Splinter gangs — the one persistence mechanic every book keeps | Campaign | Reprinted nearly verbatim in three books across seven years: one Champion becomes the new Leader, keeps gear/Advances/injuries, pays full price — the most stable single idea in the whole product line. | [[Necromunda Campaigns#Splinter gangs — the one persistence mechanic every book keeps]] |
| 📎 | **Necromunda** | Twelve dials on one chassis — the official campaign variants | Campaign | Twelve named variants, each a short stated delta against the same Dominion chassis (never a rewrite) — the "publish deltas" discipline applied at the whole-campaign level, not just to advancement costs. | [[Necromunda Campaigns#Twelve dials on one chassis — the official campaign variants]] |
| ⚙️ | **Necromunda** | Semi-Perpetual Campaigns — a Triumph-to-legacy-bonus table | Campaign | Each of the five Dominion Triumphs converts to one small, specific, capped starting bonus in the *next* campaign for carried-over fighters — a legacy system with a hard ceiling. | [[Necromunda Campaigns#Semi-Perpetual Campaigns — a Triumph-to-legacy-bonus table]] |
| 📎 | **Necromunda** | Campaign end conditions, compared across the line | Campaign | Four genuinely different end shapes across eight systems: fixed-calendar Triumphs, a scored meta-verdict layered on top, a forced sudden-death Showdown, and a pooled-threshold ending with no calendar at all. | [[Necromunda Campaigns#Campaign end conditions, compared across the line]] |
