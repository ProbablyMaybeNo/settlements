PLAYING SOLO!
CHOOSE THE FORCE
Before a game, we recommend that you build an enemy force to an appropriate Resources limit. This force can be 
built from any of the Spectre Operations Force Builders. 
CHOOSE THE SCENARIO
The next step is to choose the Scenario you want to play. We have found that these games work best with the NPE 
Force as a defender, but feel free to experiment with them in different roles. 
If you are playing a 2 player game with an NPE, decide before starting what role they could play in your scenario. 
They could be a local security force that happens to be in the area, a rival militia, or even a local criminal organisation.
SET POI AND AOP
After you have set up your table and all relevant terrain, designate a number of areas as PoI and AoP as described in 
Spectre Operations. These are vital in allowing the NPE rules to function early on in the game.
DEPLOY
Deploy the NPE within or up to 4” outside of the edge of the PoI (The Force can be split across multiple PoI). A single 
Element may deploy within the confines of each AoP. NPEs should start the game as Unaware or Uneasy.
You are now ready to start your game!
These rules are intended to allow players to play solo, cooperatively, or as competitors against an NPE (Non Player 
Element) enemy. 
Using NPEs in your games can add some unique challenges whether you choose to face a few Elements guarding an 
Objective or an entire force!

PLAYING WITH NPES
NPEs will act and react by using a series of tables to dictate their behaviour. At the start of each of the NPE turns, 
a player will roll a dice and consult the relevant table for each Element on the NPE Force. This should be done for 
each Element, one at a time. 
Alert Elements will always act first, then Uneasy and finally Unaware.
AWARE ELEMENTS.
Aware Elements will behave slightly differently than Unaware Elements. Their behaviour is first dictated by if they 
have an enemy Element in LoS. If they do, they are likely to try to Engage them. If they do not, they will likely 
manoeuvre into positions, or take up defensive positions.

REACTIONS
NPE are able to make Reactions as normal, although it is not guaranteed they will. Reactions will, in general, only be 
made on the role of 6+, giving some unpredictability. 
RETURN FIRE:
If an NPE is the Target of an Engagement Action, on the roll of a 6+ on a D10, it will make a Return Fire Reaction, if it is 
able to.
SNAP SHOT:
If an Enemy Element could be the subject of a Snap Shot Reaction, on the roll of a 6+ on a D10, the NPE will make that 
reaction, if it is able.
SUPPRESSION AND PINNED NPE
If an NPE is Suppressed or Pinned, that Element will make a Rally Action before rolling for any other Actions.
