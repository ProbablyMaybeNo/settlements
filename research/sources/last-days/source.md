## Core rulebook — General Game Concepts (p.10)

> "Tests are when a Character needs to achieve something using one of its characteristics.
> Any Test will have a number and a characteristic associated with it
> (Intelligence/8)... If this sum equals or exceeds the Test number then they have
> succeeded... Unless specifically noted otherwise for a Test, ties are considered a
> success for the person with the higher characteristic. If this is still a draw, it goes
> to the Character that did not initiate the Test (the defender)."

## Core rulebook — Shooting Phase, "Lucky 7s" (p.52)

> "Once the final modified Firearms skill of the shooter has been determined, roll 1D6
> and add your Firearms skill. This is a Firearms/7 Test. If the total of the die roll and
> your Firearms skill is 7 or higher, you have scored a hit!"

## Core rulebook — The Menace Phase (p.39-40)

> "During this step of the Menace Phase, each model with a Noise Token must roll a die
> (1D6) and add the number of Noise Tokens they have from the previous turn. Any model
> that achieves a total of 7 or higher will draw a zombie to the battlefield... If a model
> ever has 6 or more Noise Tokens on it during the Menace Phase, it will automatically
> summon one zombie, and then roll again adding any Noise Tokens it has over 6 to that
> dice roll."

> "Unlike Noise Tokens, Ammo Tokens are only removed once a gun is reloaded." (p.53)

## Core rulebook — Zombie Activation / Knockback (p.44)

> "Every time a zombie is hit by a Ranged Attack (not Close Quarters) and survives, it
> receives a number of Knockback Counters equal to a Firearm's Knockback characteristic.
> Each Knockback Counter on a zombie subtracts 1 Action Point from their total for the
> round... Discard all Knockback Tokens a Zombie has at the end of its activation."

## Core rulebook — Shoot Them in the Head! (p.61)

> "Instead of making a Damage roll against a model with this attribute when they are hit
> by Shooting or Close Quarters Combat, roll a die (1D6). On a roll of 5 or 6 the model has
> been hit in the head and the brain has been destroyed. It takes 1 Damage Point... on a
> roll of 1 to 4 the model with this special rule will accumulate a number of Knockback
> tokens equal to the Knockback statistic of the weapon being fired."

## Core rulebook — Zombie AI / Moving Zombies (p.60)

> "Zombies will always move towards the nearest non-zombie model to which they have Line
> of Sight... If there are no living models in Line of Sight, then the zombie will move
> towards the closest model that Tested for a Noise Token during that Menace Phase
> (regardless of Line of Sight)... If the closest non-zombie model cannot be reached,
> because another zombie (or zombies) is already engaging it and in the way, the zombie
> must move towards the next closest model that is unengaged (and to which it has Line of
> Sight) instead. If no such model exists, it behaves exactly as if it had no Line of Sight
> at all."

## Core rulebook — Horror and Courage, "Sticky" (p.61)

> "If the Horror causing model's total is greater than that of the model it is engaging,
> it will panic as it grapples with the undead and may not use Action Points to break off
> for the remainder of the turn... This makes zombies 'Sticky'."

## Core rulebook — Breaking Point (p.57)

> "During any turn that a Group took at least one casualty, it must Test to see if the
> fighters have reached their Breaking Point... roll a die (1D6) and add the number of
> casualties the group has taken. If that number is less than or equal to the combined
> Courage characteristic of the Group's Leader and the remaining number of friendly
> fighters still on the table, then they will continue to fight."

## Core rulebook — Attributes: Noisy X, Stalker (p.71-72)

> "STALKER — Moving quickly and quietly is this something that comes naturally to this
> Character. They do not generate Noise Tokens when they run." (p.71)

> "NOISY X — A character, weapon, or piece of equipment with this attribute generates +X
> additional Noise Tokens whenever it is used." (p.72)

## Core rulebook — Refuge Perk trigger text used for the "touches an ordinary battle" audit (p.80-82)

> "ARMOURY — If a Character works the Armoury, during the next Encounter their Group may
> re-roll 3 failed Ammo Rolls."

> "FORTIFIED WINDOWS — During a Zombie Attack the Group may add +1 to the Attack Roll."
> (the abstracted, off-table campaign-turn roll — never an on-table Encounter)

> "RADIO ROOM — If a Character is assigned to work this Perk they may make an
> Intelligence/8 Test. If passed, you may modify the Scenario roll by +1 or -1 after it is
> made."

## Core rulebook — Encounters Table (p.98)

> table below." (1 Bushwhack / 2 Home Defense / 3 Massacre Site / 4 Rescue / 5 Scavengers

## Seasons supplement — Character Conditions (p.12)

> "There are four aspects to track, each of which has two levels... When the first box is
> checked the character is considered to be Suffering. If both boxes are checked off the
> condition is Critical. Should they ever be at the worst level of any two Conditions
> (both boxes checked on each Condition) they will die!"

## Seasons — Cold / heating formula (p.41)

> "To determine how many Rooms must be heated, add the total number of Starting Perks and
> Empty Spaces a Refuge begins with. This is the total number of Rooms that must be heated
> and each one will consume one Fuel."

## Seasons — Solo Play targeting AI (p.70-71)

> "Selfish Characters — They must target and shoot the nearest zombie to themselves...
> Selfless Characters — They may target and shoot any zombie in Line of Sight unless there
> is a zombie closer to another Character in the Group... in which case they will attempt
> to aid that Character instead... Trained Characters — These Characters may freely
> choose their targets... Neutral Characters — These Characters may freely choose their
> targets."

## Seasons — End Phase Event Table, solo escalation (p.71)

> "Roll 1D6 and add the current turn number while consulting the End Phase Event Table.
> The lower the score, the more things continue as normal. The higher the score, the more
> agitated the zombies become."
