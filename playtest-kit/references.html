2 Cover & Line of Sight
Cover = a penalty to the shooter's roll
Open 0 Light −1 Heavy −2 Hidden −3 Blocked no shot
Judging cover Most of the body behind solid material = Heavy. Partly screened (railing, bush) = Light. Cannot see it = no shot.
Height A shooter 2"+ above the target ignores Light cover at the target's level.
Cover only affects the hit, never the Injury roll.

4 Conditions
No stacking (re-applying refreshes) · one token each · a new bad condition adds +1 Stress (except Pinned/Shaken) · ongoing ones resolve in the End Phase.
ConditionEffect
PinnedNo Move/Charge/Sprint; spend your Move to clear it. May still Shoot / Interact.
Shaken1+ Stress: −1 to all rolls. Always on, no test.
DownProne, dying. Heavy cover vs shots; a melee attack auto-finishes it. Needs Stabilize.
ProneFlat (not hurt). Heavy cover vs shots; can't Shoot/Charge; standing uses the whole turn.
Hidden−3 to be hit. Lost when you move, shoot or are revealed.
SuppressedLike Pinned and can't React until the Pin clears.
GrappledHeld within 1". Only attack the grappler or roll STR to break free.
SnaredTrapped. No moving; Action + STR 7+ to break out.
Off-BalanceNo Sprint/Charge. Clears end of your next turn.
Hobbled−2" Move. Clears end of your next turn.
ProvokedFirst attack on anyone but the source is at −1.
FireEach End Phase, Injury at +1, ignoring Armor. Action to put out.
BleedEach End Phase −1 WND unless treated (Action + INT 7+).
Poison−1 to all rolls. Each End Phase roll STR 7+ to shake it.

7 Hazards & Falling
Dangerous areas A model that ends its move inside one suffers it (forced in = at once).
Fire → Fire Acid → Poison Ice → AGI 7+ or Off-Balance Wires → Shocked Deep water → Swim (AGI) Smoke → Blind
Falling Under 3": nothing. 3"+: land Prone. 6"+: Prone and an Injury roll at +1 Damage per full 2" fallen, ignoring Armor. A voluntary drop may test AGI 7+ to land clean.

10 Building a Weapon
1 · Class sets base damage, range, and minimum rank (Light Melee +1 · One-Hand +2 · Heavy Melee +3 · Sidearm 8"/+2 · Standard Ranged 18"/+3 · Heavy Ranged 24"/+3).
2 · Characteristics (cost points + a slot): Brutal +1 Dmg · Accurate +1 hit if still · Long Range +6" · Bleeding/Toxic/Incendiary add a condition · Blast hits an area.
3 · Drawbacks (refund points): Short Range · Unstable (breaks on a nat 1) · Cumbersome · Limited (one use).
