Scenario · Board
Take a Hold — first-pass, simplified: hold the centre. No twist, no hack-to-claim yet — just bodies in the area.
A Take a Hold  Control
Premise Three objectives sit on the centreline. Stand on them, keep the enemy off. Most points after Round 6 wins.
Objectives 3 markers on the centreline — one central, one 12" to each flank. Each sits on top of, or inside, a building — not both (pick when you place it).
Holding You hold a marker with a standing model within 3" and no enemy within 3".
Contested — models from both sides within 3" → nobody holds it. Down / Out / Broken can't hold; Shaken hold normally (at −1).
Scoring Each End Phase, Rounds 2–6: score 1 VP per marker held. No scoring in Round 1. A grab lost by the End Phase scores nothing. Ceiling 15 VP.
Victory Most VP after Round 6. A bottled or wiped crew stops scoring — see Bottling / Concession on the Rules Reference.
Parked for later The Twist table and the hack-to-claim hook are both cut from this first pass — we'll add them once the plain version has been played.
B Board Setup
1 · Density — the most important dial Divide the 3'×3' into nine 12"×12" squares. Place at least one large building/ruin per square — aim 9–12 buildings (12 is the balance ceiling from the sim). Then add 9+ scatter pieces (cars, crates, low walls) until no clean firing lane crosses the board.
2 · Objectives Put the 3 markers on the centreline — centre, and 12" to each flank — each on top of or inside a building.
3 · Deployment Each player gets a 6"-deep band along their own board edge (opposite sides). The two zones sit 24" apart. Deploy all models inside your band.
4 · Access Every rooftop / upper floor needs at least one no-test route (stairs or ladder). Mark Dangerous areas and name the hazard.

Cover & Line of Sight
To-hit modifier Open 0Light −1Heavy −2Hidden −3Blocked no shot
True LOS (model's eye) Bulk behind solid material = Heavy or Blocked; partial obscure by light material = Light. Height: a shooter 2"+ above the target ignores Light cover at the target's level.

Hazards (Dangerous overlay)
A fighter that ends its move/activation inside a Dangerous area suffers it. Forced movement into one triggers it at once.
Fire / burning → Fire Acid pool → Poison Ice / slick → AGI 7+ or Off-Balance Electrified → Shocked Deep water → Swim (AGI) Dense smoke → Blind + Concealing
Falling Under 3": nothing. 3"+: land Prone. 6"+: also an Injury roll +1 Dmg per full 2" fallen, ignoring Armor. Voluntary drop may test AGI 7+ to land clean.
Conditions — what each does
No stacking (reapply = refresh) · mods capped ±3 · a new negative condition = +1 Stress (except Pinned/Shaken) · one token each · persistent ones resolve in the End Phase.
ConditionEffect
Pinnedranged non-wound. No Move/Charge/Sprint/Disengage; spend Move to clear; may Shoot/Interact.
Shaken1+ Stress: −1 all rolls. Always on, no test.
Downprone, out of fight. Heavy cover vs ranged; melee auto-hits; stabilize or bleed out.
Proneknocked flat (not injured). Heavy cover vs ranged; no Shoot/Charge/Sprint; standing = whole activation.
Hidden−3 to be hit. From Hide in concealing terrain; lost on move/shoot/reveal.
Suppressedcounts as Pinned and cannot React until Pinned clears.
Grappledheld within 1"; no Move/Charge/Sprint/Disengage; only attack grappler or opposed STR to escape.
Snaredtrap. No Move/Charge/Sprint/Disengage; Action + STR 7+ to break free.
Off-Balanceno Sprint/Charge. Ends end of next activation.
Hobbled−2" MOV. Ends end of next activation.
Provokedfirst attack vs anyone but the source at −1.
Fireeach End Phase, Injury at +1 Dmg ignoring Armor. Action to extinguish (auto).
Bleedeach End Phase −1 WND unless treated (Action + INT 7+, −2 w/o Med-Kit). Death clock at WND 1.
Poison−1 all rolls. Each End Phase STR 7+: pass ends it.
Blind−2 on sight-based rolls. Clears in End Phase.
Shocked−2 all rolls, cannot React. Clears in End Phase.
Nerve states: Bolt flee to edge · Broken freeze · BugOut rout & removed. Braced +1 Break · Cowed −1 next Break · Frightened can't React, −1 Break.
