1 The canvas — 12" × 36", 1" grid, 432 squares
This is exactly the defender's back three 12"×12" density squares on a 3'×3' board. Your placed structures are the large features of those three squares in a raid — the attacker crosses ~24" of neutral ground. Heavy lines mark the three 12" divisions.
Footprint classes: Building 6×6 minimum (enclosed, enterable) · Plant ~3×3 (tanks, masts, gensets — no interior) · Station 3×1 minimum (kiosks, benches). Because Buildings are 6×6 on a 12"-deep strip you fit at most two rows of them. Groundworks extends the canvas: I 18×36 (648 sq, 120 Mat) · II 18×48 (864 sq, 200 Mat).

4 Workers & raids
Workers One slot per structure that accepts one. A worker is assigned or not — no progression to track. Assigning ends the old benefit immediately; the new one starts producing next phase.
Assigned:      
Sabotage Any structure can be charged in any raid: Action + INT 7+ to arm; detonates after surviving 3 End Phases unless a defender spends Action + DEX 7+ to defuse (nat 1 = it goes off now). A detonated structure is Disabled until repaired.
Raid loot Attacker wins → carries off 25% of stored Credits and Materials (Vault excluded), capped 100 + 100. Defender wins → attacker keeps only what they physically looted. Floor: theft never takes a defender below 50 Cr and 50 Mat.
Raid board Your structures fill your three density squares; neutral terrain fills the attacker's six until the board totals 9–12 large features. Over-filled? Bench structures — the band wins over the layout, always. The defender takes every Priority tie.
Every Materials cost here is first-draft and untested — HQ tiers, Med-bay and Mess Hall especially. Storage capacities are provisional. Log anything that felt wrongly priced, and anything you wanted to buy and could not.
