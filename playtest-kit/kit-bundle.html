How to Play

Settlementsworking title
How to Play · 1
Two crews fight to control three objectives on a 3'×3' board over 6 rounds. Each round, players alternate activating their models. At the end of a round each player scores one point per objective they control. The player with the most points after Round 6 wins. Kills do not score. You need 1d10 dice, a tape measure (inches), terrain, and both crews.
1 Dice Mechanics
Every uncertain action is one roll: 1d10 + Stat + modifiers ≥ 7 to succeed.
Natural 1 always fails Natural 10 always succeeds modifiers total capped ±3
Stats added to the roll
STR melee DEX shooting INT tech / hack AGI climb / dodge NRV nerve WND wounds (1) MOV move (6")
Full stat detail on the References sheet.
A model with DEX +2 shoots a target in light cover (−1). It needs 1d10 + 2 − 1 ≥ 7 — a 6 or higher on the die.
2 Head-to-Head Rolls
When two models oppose each other directly — melee, a grapple, escaping a hold — both roll 1d10 + the relevant Stat. The higher total wins. A tie goes to the defender.
Attacker STR +3 rolls 4 → total 7. Defender STR +2 rolls 5 → total 7. The totals tie, so the defender wins.
3 Turn Order
A game is 6 rounds. Each round has three steps in order:
1 Prioritywho activates first
2 Activationsalternate, one each
3 End Phaseresolve & score
Priority Both players roll 1d10, adding +1 for the side with fewer models in play. The winner chooses to activate first or second. Re-roll ties.
Activations Players alternate, each activating one model, until every model has activated once.
End Phase — in order a) refresh all models · b) resolve ongoing conditions (Fire, Bleed) · c) Break tests for any model on 2+ Stress · d) score objectives (Rounds 2–6).
4 Activation
Each activated model gets one Move and one Action (in either order).
Moveup to 6"
Actionone of the below
ShootMeleeInteractHideStabilize
A model makes at most one attack per activation.
Sprint Uses both the Move and the Action: move up to 12" and do nothing else.
Charge Uses both: move up to 12" into base contact and make one melee attack at +1. Requires line of sight.
Orders A Specialist has 1, a Leader has 2, per round. An Order gives a friendly model (or the leader itself) one free Action or Reaction. One Order per model per round.
Ready Spend the Action to go Ready. When an enemy acts within your front arc and line of sight, make one Reaction (a shot, a charge, etc.). Ready is kept until used.
5 Movement
Basic move Up to 6" in any direction. Set your facing (a 180° front arc) as you move.
Obstacles & climbing An obstacle under 2" high is crossed for a flat −2" of movement, no test. Climbing, jumping a gap, or swimming requires an AGI ≥ 7 test during the Move. Difficult ground costs double. A failed test stops the model or causes a fall.
Disengaging To leave base contact, move away with your Move. Each enemy in contact makes one free attack at −2. The model keeps its Action but cannot Charge this activation.

How to Play · 2
6 Shooting
The target must be in range, in line of sight, and within your front arc. Roll 1d10 + DEX + modifiers ≥ 7. A miss has no effect. A hit leads to an Injury roll (§9). A hit that does not wound leaves the target Pinned.
Cover (penalty to the shot) Light −1, Heavy −2, Hidden −3. Blocked line of sight = no shot.
In melee A model in base contact cannot shoot, except a Sidearm fired at the enemy it is engaged with.
DEX +2 shooter, target 10" away in heavy cover (−2): 1d10 + 2 − 2 ≥ 7 — needs a 7 on the die.
7 Melee
Two models within 1" are Engaged. Enter melee by moving into contact, or by a Charge (+1 to the attack, requires line of sight).
Both models roll 1d10 + STR. The higher total wins; a tie goes to the defender. The winner makes an Injury roll against the loser. Facing does not apply in melee.
A charges B. A (STR +2, +1 for the charge) rolls 5 → total 8. B (STR +1) rolls 6 → total 7. A wins and makes an Injury roll.
8 Interact — Doors, Terminals, Items
The Interact Action opens or forces a door, uses a terminal, or picks up and uses an item.
Routine Opening an unlocked door or picking up an item succeeds automatically — no roll.
Forcing / hacking A stuck or barricaded door is forced with a STR test (1d10 + STR ≥ 7). A locked terminal is opened with an INT test (1d10 + INT ≥ 7).
A model in contact with a barricaded door spends its Action and rolls 1d10 + STR ≥ 7. On a success the door opens; on a failure it stays shut.
9 Damage & Injury
When a shot or blow lands, roll for Injury: 1d10 + weapon Damage − target's Armor ≥ 7.
Successlose 1 WND
Failureno wound — Pinned (shot) / Shaken (melee)
A hit either wounds or applies stress, never both.
Reaching 0 WND A shot or hazard leaves the model Down (prone and dying). A melee blow takes it straight Out of Action.
Stabilizing A Down model must be Stabilized by a friendly (Action + INT ≥ 7 in base contact) by the end of its next activation, or it is removed.
A rifle (+3) hits a target in light armor (−1). Injury: 1d10 + 3 − 1 ≥ 7 — a 5 wounds. At WND 1, the target goes Down.
10 Morale
Gaining Stress A model gains +1 Stress when it takes a non-wounding hit, gains a negative condition, or sees a friendly go Down or Out nearby.
Shaken A model with 1+ Stress is at −1 to all rolls. Automatic — no test.
Break test In the End Phase, each model with 2+ Stress rolls 1d10 + NRV − (Stress − 1) ≥ 7. (The Shaken −1 does not apply here.) Pass: clear all Stress. Fail: break —
2Bolt
3Broken
4+Bug Out
NRV +1 model on 3 Stress: 1d10 + 1 − 2 ≥ 7 — needs an 8. Rolls 5 → breaks (Broken).
11 Conceding (Bottling)
Rounds 1–3 A crew cannot simply leave. It withdraws (move every model off its own board edge; they stay in play until they leave) or offers a surrender, which the opponent may accept to end the game.
Round 4 onward A player may declare a bottle in their activation: the entire crew is removed at once and the game ends. The opponent wins regardless of score. A crew stops scoring the moment it bottles.

Set Up
1 What You Need
A 3'×3' board · terrain (below) · 1d10 dice · a tape measure marked in inches · both crews' models and unit cards · tokens for conditions and objectives.
2 Build the Board
Divide the 3'×3' into nine 12"×12" squares. Place at least one large building or ruin in each square — 9 to 12 buildings total. Then add 9 or more scatter pieces (cars, crates, low walls) until no clear line of fire crosses the whole board.
Terrain density Fewer than 9 buildings favours shooting; more than 12 favours close combat. 9–12 is balanced.
Access Every rooftop or upper floor needs a ladder or stairs, so models reach it without a test. Mark any dangerous areas and note the hazard.
3 Deployment
Each player has a 6"-deep zone along their own board edge (opposite edges; the zones are 24" apart). Roll off — the winner deploys first. Place all models within your zone.
4 Objectives
Place 3 markers on the centre line: one at the centre, one 12" to each side. Each marker sits on top of, or inside, a building — choose one when you place it.

Settlementsworking title
Scenario
Scenario: Take a Hold. Control the most objectives at the end of the game.
A Controlling a Marker
A marker is controlled by a standing model within 3" of it, with no enemy model within 3".
Contested If models from both sides are within 3", the marker is contested and no one controls it.
Who can control Down, Out and Broken models cannot control a marker. Shaken models control normally.
B Scoring & Winning
Scoring In the End Phase of Rounds 2–6, each player scores 1 point per marker they control. No scoring in Round 1. A marker lost before the End Phase scores nothing.
Winning The player with the most points after Round 6 wins. A crew that bottles or is wiped out stops scoring.
C First Game
Not used in the first game: scenario twists and hack-to-claim objectives. Add them once the basic game plays smoothly.
