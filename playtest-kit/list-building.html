1 What Credits actually buy
The core claim Points buy bodies and guns. Stats and skills are free — rank already caps them, so pricing them again would double-count. The board prices stats; Credits price the two things whose value never depends on the mission.
One number You buy with Credits, and the Credits you field are your Crew Rating. Owning ≠ fielding: stashed gear counts 0 toward Rating.
Free to everyone Fists · one Light Melee weapon · thick clothing. A civilian with a bat and a jacket.
Carry limits One armour · two hands (a Two-Handed weapon takes both) · up to two equipment items.

3 The pyramid — is your list legal?
Match Play □ Exactly one Leader   □ every Specialist has two lower-rank fighters beneath it   □ every Recruit has a Fighter-or-better   □ minimum four models
Campaign Start □ Exactly one Leader   □ minimum three models   □ every Specialist still has two lower ranks (the Recruit-per-Fighter rule is the only part dropped — a green crew can be all bodies, but not an all-Specialist elite)
No unit cap The pyramid and the budget do the work. At 1000 the legal maximum is about 11 fighters — a mob with bats.
Roster vs crew What you own is capped by housing: 12 body slots from the HQ, +6 per Bunkhouse. No per-head upkeep.

CREW RATING   (must be ≤ the format cap)
Campaign crews Each Level a fighter has gained adds its own Credits to that fighter: 15 per stat point · 20/35/55 per T1/T2/T3 skill · 45 for the Level-7 wound. A fully-levelled fighter carries +245 over their body. Scars cost nothing and refund nothing.
Why that matters Veterans crowd out rookies. The longer a campaign runs the smaller your fielded crew gets — that is the anti-snowball valve, and every list becomes a real question: field my best, or field the most?
Costs are on the 1000-Credit scale (the 5/8/16/24 ladder is retired). Match Play numbers are sim-validated for spread, not table-validated. Campaign Start at 500 is explicitly unvalidated — the sim contradicts it, and a rifle-armed Leader plus a rifle Fighter leaves no room for a legal third body. Log what happens.
