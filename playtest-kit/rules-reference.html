Rules Reference
Every uncertain outcome is one roll: 1d10 + Stat + mods ≥ 7.  Board 3'×3' · deploy 24" apart · 6 rounds (~1.5 h) · win on objectives, never kills.  WND 1 for nearly everyone.

9 Bottling — voluntary concession
Rounds 1–3 — never free You can't just lift the crew off. Either:
Fighting withdrawal — walk every model off your own edge (they stay in play & exposed until they leave); the crew has quit — can't contest or score.
Surrender — if the opponent accepts, the game ends at once as their win.
Round 4+ — clean bottle Declare in your activation: all your models are removed at once and the game ends. A concession — opponent wins, whatever the score. Scoring stops the instant you bottle.
