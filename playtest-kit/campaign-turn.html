1 Post-Battle — the moment the game ends
  1. Survival. A fighter is Safe — no roll — if it is still standing with the crew, left via a friendly edge, or ends in base contact with a friendly. Everyone else rolls Fate.
  2. Level-ups. Apply every qualifying trigger from the battle (kills · Glorious Deeds · surviving · objectives held), capped at 6 distinct sources per fighter. Resolve any Level crossed now.
  3. Bank Resources. Add the battle's Credits and Materials. Typical win ≈ 65 Cr + 33 Mat.
Fate — 1d10, +1 with a Med-bay, +2 if it has a worker (that is also the cap)

Floating levels (1, 4, 8) Add +1 to any stat, chosen when the level is earned — Primary included. A fighter's direction emerges from play, not from a box ticked at hire. Only the +6 campaign cap limits growth; creation tier caps do not apply.
Skill slots (3, 6, 10) Declare any path, then roll on any tier that path's stat has unlocked: +2 → T1 · +4 → T2 · +6 → T3. Roll 3 on that tier's chart, reroll duplicates, choose 1. Your Primary path always counts as unlocked at T1, so a slot is never dead. Cross-path picks are legal and cost you your own capstone — a real trade.
Ceilings A full track is +245 Cr, 6 stat points, 4 skills, one wound. MOV never rises from Levels (Fleet only). WND caps at 3 (Level 7 + Tough) — nothing may push past it. A fighter caps at Level 10.

3 Settlement Phase
  1. Build & upgrade against the catalogue — spend Materials. (Settlement Sheet)
  2. Assign or reassign workers. Reassigning ends the old benefit at once; the new one produces next phase.
  3. Assign Power across powered structures. Anything uncovered is Disabled.
  4. Resolve pending Captured decisions — Ransom or Brainwash due this phase.
  5. Resolve third-Scar retirements.
  6. Dispatch actions — one per HQ tier (I=1 · II=2 · III=3): Scout (reveal a territory's Twist or hidden Side Objective) or Scavenge (roll that territory's Loot table, no battle). Sabotage-by-dispatch is deliberately not in v1 — wrecking a structure needs a raid.
  7. Check caps. Now, and only now: anything over cap is lost.
Captured Held for the owner's next battle. The owner may declare a rescue Raid first — if the captor won't accept it inside that window the prisoner goes free. Otherwise the captor must choose: Ransom (half the fighter's Credits; refuse and they're lost as Dead) or Brainwash (upgraded Holding Cells; opposed 1d10+INT, two wins = permanent defection, any loss = they escape home).

4 Battle Prep
  1. Choose the territory — and therefore the scenario, from its card.
  2. Set the Crew Rating cap: standard 1000 · raid 750 · pitched 1500 · Campaign Start 500.
  3. Build the roster — which owned fighters to field (within housing), equipped within the cap. (Crew Builder)
  4. Resolve setup bonuses — Faction rule, Location benefit, territory-card modifiers.
  5. Roll the Twist once deployment is set: 1 Blackout (LOS 12") · 2 Live Board · 3 Reinforcements (R3, each returns one Down) · 4 Scavengers · 5 Foul Weather (open is Difficult) · 6 Clean.
