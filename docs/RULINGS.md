## 2026-08-31 — Skills, ranks and tiers

**1. Skills are granted by rank, as a flat count.**

| Rank | Skills at creation |
|---|:--:|
| Recruit | 0 |
| Fighter | 1 |
| Specialist | 2 |
| Leader | 3 |

*Replaces Campaign Start's "one skill per rank at that rank's tier".*

**2. Tier comes from stat allocation, not from rank.** A stat at +2 opens T1,
+4 opens T2, +6 opens T3, and a fighter may put their skills in any tier their
stats have unlocked. **The rank tier-cap table is deleted** — `Fighter 2×T1`,
`Specialist 1×T2 · 2×T1`, `Leader 1×T3 · 2×T2 · 4×T1` no longer apply.

**3. There is no rank restriction on T3.** A Specialist who spends 6 of their 7
stat points on one stat reaches T3 and may take it. The cost is being
one-dimensional, and that cost is paid in stats, not in a rule.

> **Struck:** *"Only a Leader ever reaches T3."* Never ruled. It was a gloss
> describing the cap table above, which is itself now deleted.

**4. Chained skills require their gateway.** A skill that uses a state only
another skill can create must have that skill. A T3 in a chain needs its T2,
which needs its T1 — so **Crushing Hold** needs **Squeeze** needs **Grapple**.

*Consequence, accepted:* a Leader taking a chained T3 spends all three creation
slots on that one chain. The chain is the build.

*Two chains exist in the current 150 skills:* **Grapple** (6 downstream) and
**Sharp Eyes / Muzzle Flash → Spotted** (5 downstream). Nothing else is chained;
`Hidden` and `Pinned` come from core actions, not skills.

## Still open — deliberately parked 2026-08-31

- **Multiple T3s.** Two candidates, no decision. **Pyramid:** no more T3s than
  you have T1s and T2s, so each T3 costs three skills (a 6-slot Leader gets
  2 T3). **Unlock:** one T1 and one T2 opens the tier, then any number
  (a 6-slot Leader gets 4 T3). Identical at creation; they only diverge for a
  veteran. The risk with unlock is that T1 and T2 become entry tolls rather
  than picks.
- **Same-path or any-path?** Whether the T1/T2 that unlock a T3 must be in that
  T3's own path. Leaning any-path, since the +6 stat gate already forces depth.
- **§14 contradicts the Level track.** §14 still says *"a stat hands you a skill
  every time it reaches a tier"* while §26.1 grants slots at levels 3 / 6 / 10.
  Both are live in the vault. Ruling 1 above supersedes §14, but the text has
  not been changed yet.
- **Level cadence.** Slots currently at 3 / 6 / 10. Raised, not decided.
