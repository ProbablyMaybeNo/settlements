# Settlements — Setting Tech Reference (2051)

**Purpose:** Grounding catalogue for the weapons / equipment / defences / structures of *Settlements*, a near-future skirmish wargame set in **2051**. Every item below is anchored to a **real programme in development today** so the setting reads as a plausible extrapolation, not space opera. Each entry gives what it is, current (2025–26) status with a source, a plausible 2051 maturity, and a one-line **GAME HOOK**.

**Setting logic:** By 2051 the trends already visible in 2025 have compounded — cheap attritable drones as a battlefield *currency*, directed energy as the affordable answer to them, pervasive squad networking, ubiquitous jamming that makes GPS optional, AI-fused targeting, and autonomous ground robots doing the dying. The interesting tension for a skirmish game is not "who has the biggest gun" but **network vs. jam, sensor vs. camo, mass vs. precision, and power vs. mobility.**

**Reading the confidence tags** (attached to each 2051-maturity line):
- `[near-certain]` — already fielded or in production; effectively guaranteed to be mature and commonplace by 2051.
- `[plausible]` — active development on a credible path; likely fielded by 2051 but the specifics are open.
- `[speculative]` — lab / demonstrator only; possible by 2051 but not assured. Use for "high-tech faction only" flavour.

*Compiled 2026-07 from real-world programme reporting. Sources are inline. Where a real programme was shelved or is struggling (DE M-SHORAD, RCV, Replicator's slip), that is captured deliberately — the friction is good setting texture.*

## Infantry small arms & kinetics

### XM7 rifle & XM250 automatic rifle (NGSW)
- **What it is:** SIG Sauer's 6.8mm rifle (XM7, based on the MCX-Spear) and belt/mag-fed automatic rifle (XM250) that replace the M4 and M249 at the squad level.
- **Status (2025-26):** First fielded to the 101st Airborne in March 2024; type-classified as standard (M7/M250) in May 2025. SIG has since shown a lightened "PIE" M7 and a shorter carbine, and the Army paused new M7 buys while it weighs configuration and an "XM8" carbine — future purchases are "pre-decisional" ([Military.com](https://www.military.com/daily-news/2024/03/29/army-has-finally-fielded-its-next-generation-squad-weapons.html), [The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/land/sig-sauers-m7-rifle-gets-official-army-seal-of-approval-despite-controversy)).
- **2051 maturity:** A 6.8mm-class squad weapon is the baseline. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Standard-issue trooper rifle profile — higher armour-penetration and range than legacy 5.56, at the cost of heavier ammo (fewer shots carried).

### 6.8×51mm Common Cartridge & hybrid-case ammunition
- **What it is:** The shared 6.8mm round for the NGSW family, using a three-piece hybrid case (steel head + brass body + aluminium washer) to survive ~80,000 psi for higher velocity and penetration.
- **Status (2025-26):** In production with the weapons; SIG delivered 825,000+ hybrid-cased rounds during Army testing (sold commercially as .277 SIG Fury). True Velocity's fully composite case was the competing lightweight approach ([Soldier Systems Daily](https://soldiersystems.net/2021/01/27/sig-ammunition-produced-rounds-of-6-8x51mm/), [Wikipedia – .277 Fury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.277_FURY)).
- **2051 maturity:** High-pressure hybrid/composite cases are solved and in service. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An "armour-piercing" ammo tier plus a weight-per-clip stat — heavier rounds mean a tighter ammo economy and a resupply token on the table.

### XM157 Fire Control smart optic
- **What it is:** Vortex Optics 1-8×30 variable optic with integrated ballistic computer, laser rangefinder, environmental sensors, digital compass, display overlay and wireless networking — an auto-correcting "smart scope."
- **Status (2025-26):** Fielded alongside the NGSW weapons; Vortex holds a ~$2.7B, 10-year deal for up to 250,000 units ([Military Embedded Systems](https://militaryembedded.com/radar-ew/sensors/fire-control-system-from-vortex-optics-wins-10-year-contract-with-us-army), [American Rifleman](https://www.americanrifleman.org/content/vortex-selected-to-produce-army-ngsw-fire-control-optic/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Networked ballistic-computer optics are standard issue. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An equipment upgrade granting first-shot accuracy/range bonus and squad target-sharing — but a jammable electronic dependency that can be "greyed out" by EW.

### Guided / precision small-arms rounds
- **What it is:** Self-steering bullets that adjust trajectory in flight via optical sensors and tiny fins to hit moving targets at extreme range.
- **Status (2025-26):** Lab/demonstrator only. DARPA's .50-cal EXACTO publicly hit moving targets in 2015; Sandia designed a comparable laser-guided .50-cal dart. No fielded infantry guided round exists ([DARPA](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2015/exacto-guided-bullet-demonstration), [Sandia National Labs](https://www.sandia.gov/research/guided-bullet-technology/)).
- **2051 maturity:** A guided sniper/anti-materiel round is very achievable; squad-level guided rounds less certain. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Rare "guided round" special ammo for marksman/anti-materiel weapons — ignores cover/evasion for one shot; limited-count consumable.

### Cased-telescoped (CT) & caseless ammunition
- **What it is:** Ammunition where the projectile sits inside a polymer case (cased-telescoped) or has no case at all, cutting weight and simplifying the feed path.
- **Status (2025-26):** Textron's CT weapons/ammo were the losing NGSW finalist and remain the most mature line (~37% ammo weight cut), but still a demonstrator. True caseless (cf. the historic H&K G11) remains unsolved for service due to heat/cook-off ([Textron Systems](https://www.textronsystems.com/products/ct-weapons)).
- **2051 maturity:** Polymer cased-telescoped is likely fielded somewhere; true caseless less certain. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A faction/tech-tier identity — "lightweight ammo" doctrine lets a unit carry more shots at equal encumbrance, favouring high-volume suppressive play.

### Lightweight weapon materials (polymer / carbon / titanium barrels)
- **What it is:** Composite-wrapped, polymer, titanium and additively-manufactured parts that cut weapon weight and manage heat without losing durability.
- **Status (2025-26):** In active use — carbon-fibre-wrapped barrels are mature; Avient is pushing "Composite Heat Release" barrels (2025); ARDEC has tested polymer/3D-printed barrels, though full pressure-bearing printed barrels remain hard at 60,000+ psi ([Avient/Defense & Munitions](https://www.defenseandmunitions.com/article/composite-heat-release-technology-for-firearm-barrel-manufacturing-avient-feature/), [ARES](https://armamentresearch.com/polymer-3d-printed-barrel-successfully-tested/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Composite/lightweight weapon construction is standard. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A weapon "weight class" modifier — lighter guns improve movement/AP or let a lone trooper carry a heavy weapon.

## Soldier systems

### IVAS → Anduril EagleEye / SBMC (AR combat HUD)
- **What it is:** A helmet-mounted augmented-reality visor fusing thermal/low-light night vision, digital map overlays, targeting and squad data into the soldier's field of view.
- **Status (2025-26):** Microsoft's HoloLens-based IVAS contract was novated to Anduril in early 2025; Anduril (with Meta) unveiled "EagleEye" at AUSA Oct 2025. The follow-on was renamed Soldier-Borne Mission Command (SBMC), with prototyping awards to Anduril and startup Rivet ([Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2025/02/11/anduril-and-microsoft-partner-to-advance-integrated-visual-augmentation-system-ivas-program-for-the-u-s-army/), [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/10/i-have-got-this-s-figured-out-anduril-unveiling-eagleeye-mixed-reality-device-at-ausa/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Networked AR combat visors are standard infantry kit. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Core "HUD" gear granting night-fight parity, shared spotting and target hand-off — with an EW/jamming vulnerability and a "greyed-out" downgrade state when the network is degraded.

### Networked squad vision & heads-up data sharing
- **What it is:** Squad-level tactical network (end-user device + software + radios) putting every soldier's position, spotted enemies and orders on a shared common picture, pushed into optics/HUDs.
- **Status (2025-26):** Fielded as Nett Warrior (Android + ATAK over tactical radio), increasingly fused with ENVG-B night-vision AR and, in Army experiments, with aided target recognition and small-drone feeds ([U.S. Army – ENVG-B](https://www.army.mil/article/243798/integrated_technology_takes_night_vision_to_a_new_level), [U.S. Army C5ISR](https://www.army.mil/article/287719/c5isr_center_research_connects_aided_target_recognition_with_small_uas_for_greater_squad_lethality)).
- **2051 maturity:** Pervasive squad data-sharing is baseline. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A squad "network" mechanic — when linked, any model's line-of-sight spots for the whole unit; sever/jam the network to isolate models and strip the bonus.

### Powered exoskeletons & exosuits
- **What it is:** Wearable powered frames that offload heavy loads and multiply strength/endurance, from full-body lift suits to lower-body load-carriage assists.
- **Status (2025-26):** No US full-body combat suit is fielded — Sarcos exited exoskeleton hardware in 2023 and rebranded to Palladyne AI (software); Lockheed's lower-body ONYX stayed a prototype. The Army targets a phased "Warrior Suit" (load-carriage first) on a ~2040 horizon, while active-powered *load-carry* exosuits are already in series production for industry/logistics (German Bionic's Exia, ~84 lb assist), and China's PLA is field-trialling porter exosuits ([Palladyne AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palladyne_AI), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/07/german-bionics-new-exoskeleton-supports-80-pound-lifts), [Defense One](https://www.defenseone.com/technology/2025/08/iron-man-himalayas-chinas-pla-embraces-exoskeletons/407797/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Load-carriage/logistics exos very plausible; full-body powered *combat* armour is a stretch. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An "exo-rig" trooper type — carries heavy/support weapons a normal model can't and ignores load penalties, but is slow, power-dependent, and a priority EMP/anti-materiel target.

### Physiological health-monitoring wearables
- **What it is:** Body-worn sensors/patches tracking heart rate, core temperature, hydration, fatigue and even chemical/radiation exposure, feeding real-time readiness to leaders.
- **Status (2025-26):** Moving to early fielding — the Army's WARP programme uses LifeLens patches (biometrics plus chemical/gas/radiation detection), with initial fielding to special-operations elements from late 2025 ([National Defense Magazine](https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2026/2/27/wearable-patches-could-monitor-troops-health-sense-danger), [Army SBIR](https://armysbir.army.mil/topics/wearable-technologies-physiological-monitoring/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Ubiquitous soldier biometric telemetry. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A command-layer feed — leaders see wound/fatigue/exposure states, enabling a "medic auto-alert" or morale/stress track; hazard patches trigger early CBRN warnings.

### Adaptive / active camouflage & signature management
- **What it is:** Materials that dynamically reduce or reshape a soldier's/vehicle's visual, infrared and radar signature — from tunable-emissivity "thermal cloaks" toward chameleon visual matching.
- **Status (2025-26):** Multispectral passive/adaptive signature management is real and fielding — Fibrotex USA supplies the Army's multi-spectral ULCANS camouflage and delivered next-gen systems in 2025; Vollebak demoed a graphene programmable thermal jacket and BAE's ADAPTIV morphs vehicle IR signature — but full real-time visual "invisibility" is lab-stage ([Fibrotex/Zona Militar](https://www.zona-militar.com/en/2025/05/16/fibrotex-usa-delivers-camouflage-systems-for-us-military-prepares-for-future-of-war/), [Vollebak](https://vollebak.com/en-us/pages/thermal), [DSIAC](https://dsiac.dtic.mil/articles/military-camouflage-technology-countering-thermal-imaging/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Multispectral/thermal signature management near-certain; active full-visual cloaking is the reach. Signature management `[plausible]`, true invisibility `[speculative]`.
- **GAME HOOK:** A "signature" stat with visual + thermal channels — adaptive-camo units gain stealth/first-strike unless the observer has the matching sensor (thermal optic beats visual camo), driving a sensor-vs-camo rock-paper-scissors.

## Directed-energy weapons

### DE M-SHORAD "Guardian" (50 kW Stryker laser)
- **What it is:** A 50 kW-class solid-state laser on a Stryker A1 for maneuver short-range air defence against drones, rockets and mortars.
- **Status (2025-26):** Four prototypes were fielded and combat-tested in the Middle East, but the Army found 50 kW hard to sustain on a moving vehicle (heat, power, wear) and soldier feedback was lukewarm; no further procurement — lessons feed an Enduring High-Energy Laser decision in ~FY26 ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2024/05/army-soldiers-not-impressed-with-strykers-outfitted-with-50-kilowatt-lasers-service-official-says/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Vehicle-mounted combat lasers plausibly clear the mobility/thermal wall by 2051. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A vehicle-only "laser point-defence" weapon: unlimited ammo but a heat/power track forcing cooldown turns — strong vs drones/light munitions, weak on the move at full power.

### Army Guardian / HELWS (RTX ~10-15 kW palletized C-UAS laser)
- **What it is:** Raytheon/RTX's High-Energy Laser Weapon System, a palletized laser that detects, tracks and burns down small drones.
- **Status (2025-26):** Combat-certified and fielded overseas since 2019 with 25,000+ operational hours; in 2025 RTX is expanding it to new platforms and use cases ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/07/rtxs-helws-anti-drone-laser-weapon-looking-for-new-use-cases/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Low-kW counter-drone lasers are already operational; ubiquity is assured. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A cheap deployable "laser turret" emplacement — low damage, but a dedicated drone-swatter that never runs out of shots.

### UK DragonFire
- **What it is:** MBDA/QinetiQ/Leonardo ~50 kW-class laser directed-energy weapon for cheap precision drone/missile kills.
- **Status (2025-26):** In Nov 2025 it made the UK's first above-horizon shoot-down of fast drones (up to 650 km/h); a £316M contract puts it on Royal Navy Type 45 destroyers, accelerated to 2027, at roughly £10 per shot ([Royal Navy](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2025/november/20/20251120-dragonfire-trials), [Naval News](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2025/11/uk-contracts-for-dragonfire-naval-laser-capability-to-help-build-cost-effective-ship-based-defence/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Ship/base-mounted DE interceptors are standard. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Faction identity for a Western/naval remnant — a "pennies-per-shot" heavy laser: expensive structure, near-free trigger, defining an attritional counter-fire playstyle.

### Israel Iron Beam / "Light Shield" (Rafael)
- **What it is:** Rafael's ~100 kW-class high-power laser air-defence system to burn down rockets, mortars and drones as a cheap layer under Iron Dome.
- **Status (2025-26):** Delivered to the IDF in Dec 2025 — the world's first operational high-power laser air-defence system, enabled by an adaptive-optics breakthrough ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/laser-based-iron-beam-interception-system-declared-operational/), [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/12/israels-new-laser-system-goes-active/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Fixed-site 100 kW+ laser air defence is commonplace. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A base-defence structure that auto-intercepts incoming indirect fire/drones on a die roll — reshapes the game by taxing artillery and air spam.

### Squad / light-vehicle lasers (LOCUST 20 kW, P-HEL / AMP-HEL)
- **What it is:** BlueHalo/AeroVironment's 20 kW LOCUST laser, mounted on Infantry Squad Vehicles and JLTVs for mobile short-range drone defence.
- **Status (2025-26):** The P-HEL variant is deployed overseas; in Sept-Oct 2025 the Army took its first two vehicle-mounted 20 kW LOCUST systems under AMP-HEL ([The Defense Post](https://thedefensepost.com/2025/09/04/av-us-locust-laser-weapon/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Light-vehicle counter-drone lasers are routine squad-level kit. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A mountable upgrade for a light transport/technical — turns a mobility piece into a roaming anti-drone screen at the cost of a hardpoint.

### THOR (AFRL high-power microwave)
- **What it is:** The Tactical High-power Operational Responder, a container-housed HPM emitter that fries drone electronics across a wide beam to defeat swarms.
- **Status (2025-26):** Demonstrated against a multi-drone swarm at Kirtland AFB (2023); C-130-transportable, set up in hours; a Leidos-built follow-on (Mjolnir) advances reliability ([Air & Space Forces Magazine](https://www.airandspaceforces.com/air-forces-thor-drone-swarm-demo/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Counter-swarm HPM is plausibly a fielded standard on second/third-gen systems. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An area "EMP burst" deployable — a cone/blast template that *disables* (not destroys) all drone/electronic units in it for a turn; no effect on hardened troops.

### Leonidas (Epirus counter-swarm HPM)
- **What it is:** Epirus's GaN solid-state, software-defined high-power microwave built to down entire drone swarms at once with a single pulse.
- **Status (2025-26):** Four IFPC-HPM units delivered to the Army by May 2024; a $43.5M Gen-II award followed; a 2025 live-fire defeated a 49-drone swarm (100% downed) and later a fibre-optic drone, with a mobile "Leonidas AR" on a tracked robot unveiled with General Dynamics ([Epirus](https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-leonidas-high-power-microwave-defeats-49-drone-swarm-100-of-drones-flown-at-live-fire-demonstration), [TWZ](https://www.twz.com/land/army-puts-50m-bet-on-next-gen-leonidas-high-power-microwave-counter-drone-tech)).
- **2051 maturity:** Solid-state counter-swarm HPM is a mature base/vehicle system. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "swarm-breaker" emplacement/vehicle module — one activation neutralises an entire drone unit in arc; hard-counters swarm factions, worthless vs conventional armour.

### Counter-drone directed energy (setting-level assumption)
- **What it is:** The broad shift to lasers + microwaves as the cheap, deep-magazine answer to mass/cheap drones that missiles can't affordably counter.
- **Status (2025-26):** Multiple programmes (HELWS, LOCUST, Iron Beam, Leonidas) are moving demo→fielded as the US and allies build a layered DE counter-UAS strategy ([Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/army-news/2025/microwaves-against-the-swarm-a-new-phase-in-us-counter-drone-strategy)).
- **2051 maturity:** A layered DE counter-drone umbrella is a given. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Baseline setting rule — cheap drones are a currency, and DE is the "sink" that stops them dominating; every serious force carries some counter-DE.

## Active protection & base defense

### Trophy (Rafael, hard-kill APS)
- **What it is:** The world's only combat-proven hard-kill APS, using radar-cued interceptors to shoot down incoming ATGMs/RPGs before impact; on US M1A2 Abrams and Israeli Merkava/Namer.
- **Status (2025-26):** Certified to the US Army's Modular APS Framework 2.0 in May 2025 — the first APS to do so — with a 2026 upgrade adding top-attack/anti-drone protection ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/trophy-active-protection-system-achieves-maf-2-0--302462152.html), [National Defense](https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2025/10/22/just-in-rafael-looks-to-upgrade-trophy-system-to-protect-against-drones)).
- **2051 maturity:** Hard-kill APS is standard on MBTs/IFVs. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A vehicle upgrade granting a limited-charge "intercept" save vs rockets/missiles/ATGMs — a few uses per game, then depleted; ineffective vs direct KE rounds and (until upgraded) top-attack.

### Iron Fist (Elbit, hard-kill APS)
- **What it is:** A lightweight hard-kill APS using a small interceptor + fuze to defeat ATGMs, RPGs, loitering munitions and even some KE rounds, sized for medium vehicles.
- **Status (2025-26):** Selected (Sept 2025) as the Bradley IFV APS with a $228M follow-on — the third US Army selection of Iron Fist, covering potentially 1,200+ vehicles ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/elbit-systems-awarded-228-million-follow-on-contract-to-provide-iron-fist-aps-for-us-army-bradley-ifv-upgrades-302669851.html)).
- **2051 maturity:** Lightweight APS on IFVs/medium vehicles is standard. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** The "light APS" tier — cheaper/lighter than Trophy, fits transports and IFVs, but a lower intercept save; lets even soft-skin vehicles buy some missile protection.

### Iron Curtain (Artis) & top-attack APS
- **What it is:** A close-in APS using downward-firing effectors to defeat threats (including top-attack) at the last instant, very close to the hull.
- **Status (2025-26):** Dropped from the Army's Stryker interim-APS competition in 2018 and never fielded; Artis markets a software-defined successor ("Sentinel") with no operational adoption ([Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/land/2018/08/24/iron-curtain-is-out-as-possible-active-protection-system-for-stryker/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Curtain_(countermeasure))).
- **2051 maturity:** Top-attack/close-in APS is plausible by 2051, likely via other vendors' tech. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A specialist "top-attack interceptor" option — the only APS that saves vs overhead/loitering-munition strikes; niche but crucial as drones own the sky.

### Soft-kill countermeasures & multispectral obscurants
- **What it is:** Rapid smoke/aerosol screens breaking line-of-sight across visual, IR and millimetre-wave bands (plus IR jam/decoy) to defeat optical, laser and radar seekers.
- **Status (2025-26):** Rheinmetall's ROSY multispectral system is in service in 11+ countries; the Army is soliciting a soldier-portable multispectral "Screening Obscuration Module" (UV-through-mmW, deployable in ~95 s over a 204 m area for ~12 min) ([Military Aerospace](https://www.militaryaerospace.com/sensors/article/55342507/army-looking-for-multispectral-smoke-screen-to-hide-warfighters-from-enemy-electro-optical-sensors), [Rheinmetall](https://www.rheinmetall.com/en/products/protection-systems/protection-systems-land/smoke-protection-systems)).
- **2051 maturity:** Multispectral screening (incl. mmW) as standard issue. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A deployable smoke template that blocks not just LoS but also laser/DE and guided-weapon locks through it — a cheap, universal counter to the DE-heavy meta.

### Centurion C-RAM (land Phalanx point/base defence)
- **What it is:** A land-based Phalanx: a 20mm Gatling (4,500 rpm) with radar that shoots down incoming rockets, artillery, mortars and drones over a fixed area.
- **Status (2025-26):** Fielded since 2006 and radar-upgraded for low-signature drones, but volume/cost limits vs mass cheap drones are pushing the Army toward missile-based IFPC Inc 2 (AIM-9X) as a successor layer ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centurion_C-RAM), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidhambling/2026/04/02/why-us-gatling-guns-are-not-stopping-irans-shahed-drones/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Gun CIWS persists but as a secondary layer behind DE/missiles. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A base-defence turret structure that auto-fires at incoming indirect fire/aircraft each turn — reliable but ammo-limited and saturable, so massed cheap attacks can overwhelm it.

### Reactive & emerging adaptive/electric armour
- **What it is:** Explosive reactive armour (ERA) tiles that detonate outward against shaped charges, plus next-gen concepts — electric armour (charged plates that vaporise a shaped-charge jet) and nanolaminated lightweight alloys.
- **Status (2025-26):** ERA is mature and widely fielded; electric armour remains a lab concept, and DARPA's Armor Challenge pursues nanolaminated armour at RHA-equal protection for ~half the weight ([Wikipedia — Electric armour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_armour), [Nanotech Now — DARPA Armor Challenge](https://www.nanotech-now.com/news.cgi?story_id=21824)).
- **2051 maturity:** ERA/adaptive passive armour near-certain; fielded electric armour is a plausible-to-speculative reach. `[speculative]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Tiered armour upgrades — ERA as a one-shot bonus save vs shaped-charge/HEAT that's spent when triggered; "electric armour" as a rare high-tech faction perk giving repeated shaped-charge saves while powered.

## Drones & counter-drone

### Recon nano-drone — Teledyne FLIR Black Hornet 4 (Soldier Borne Sensor)
- **What it is:** A ~33 g pocket-sized nano-helicopter giving one dismounted soldier a personal thermal/EO recon feed out to ~2 km.
- **Status (2025-26):** In active fielding — Teledyne FLIR won a 5-year US Army contract worth up to $91M under Soldier Borne Sensor Phase II, with Black Hornet 4 deliveries from Sept 2024 and parallel buys by Germany, Switzerland and others ([Military Embedded Systems](https://militaryembedded.com/unmanned/isr/black-hornet-4-nano-drones-to-be-delivered-to-us-army-by-teledyne-flir-defense)).
- **2051 maturity:** Squad-organic pocket recon drones are standard issue. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Deployable "scout token" — one per squad, reveals hidden enemies / removes cover bonuses in a radius; fragile, jammable.

### Loitering munitions — AeroVironment Switchblade 300/600
- **What it is:** Man-packable tube-launched "kamikaze" drones that loiter then dive onto personnel (300) or armour (600).
- **Status (2025-26):** Combat-proven in Ukraine and in US inventory; Oct 2025 AeroVironment unveiled Switchblade 600 Block 2, a Switchblade 400, and a 300 Block 20 with a modular EFP warhead, scaling to ~14,400 units/yr ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/10/av-launches-new-switchblade-variants-vtol-drone-design/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Precision loitering munitions replace much short-range indirect fire. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Backpack weapon card — indirect precision strike with a "loiter" delay (arrives next turn); pick soft-target vs anti-armour warhead at deploy.

### Adversary loitering munition — Russian ZALA Lancet
- **What it is:** Russia's primary anti-artillery/anti-vehicle loitering munition, now with AI terminal guidance.
- **Status (2025-26):** Routine frontline use (thousands of strikes); 2025 upgrades add longer range, a truck-mounted rapid launcher, AI terminal-homing (Lancet-3E) and 2-3 drone coordinated salvos to swamp defences ([The National Interest](https://nationalinterest.org/blog/buzz/russias-kamikaze-drones-now-deadlier-than-ever-ps-112325)).
- **2051 maturity:** Every peer force fields AI-homing loitering munitions as standard artillery. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Enemy-faction deployable that specifically hunts high-value/artillery units; can salvo to defeat point defence.

### One-way FPV / fibre-optic attack drones (Ukraine lessons)
- **What it is:** Cheap first-person-view quadcopters flown into targets as guided munitions, increasingly spooling an unjammable fibre-optic control cable.
- **Status (2025-26):** The dominant tactical killer of the Ukraine war; fibre-optic FPVs defeat electronic warfare entirely, Russia scaled to 50,000+ fibre drones/month by late 2025, hit rates ~50-80% ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/inside-ukraines-fiber-optic-drone-war)).
- **2051 maturity:** Mass attritable strike drones are the baseline infantry threat; jam-proof variants force constant cover discipline. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Ubiquitous cheap "drone strike" action any faction can spam; the fibre variant ignores ECM/jammers — must be killed kinetically or by cover/overhead concealment.

### Autonomous drone swarms (offensive & defensive)
- **What it is:** Groups of drones self-coordinating targeting/ISR/strike with minimal human input — both to attack and to screen friendly forces.
- **Status (2025-26):** Pre-fielding but accelerating — DARPA is soliciting "autonomous constellations" of up to 500 container-launched drones and DIU's $100M Orchestrator Prize / "Crucible" swarm trials run mid-2026; today's real "swarms" (e.g. Ukraine's Operation Spiderweb) are coordinated rather than fully autonomous ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/news-features/drone-swarms-packed-into-unassuming-containers-sought-by-darpa)).
- **2051 maturity:** Autonomous cooperative swarms (offensive + defensive screen) are mature and doctrinally central. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A single "swarm" unit with multi-token behaviour — spreads to saturate, reforms after losses; a defensive swarm deploys as a mobile intercept screen over a friendly unit.

### DoD "Replicator" mass-attritable autonomy initiative
- **What it is:** A Pentagon push to field thousands of low-cost attritable autonomous systems fast, then a second line focused on countering small drones.
- **Status (2025-26):** Mixed — DoD calls fielding "on track," but CRS notes only "hundreds" rather than the promised "thousands" hit the Aug 2025 target amid reliability/integration issues; Replicator 2 pivots to counter-sUAS ([USNI News](https://news.usni.org/2025/08/26/report-to-congress-on-defense-departments-replicator-initiative)).
- **2051 maturity:** "Mass over exquisite" attritable autonomy is settled procurement doctrine. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Faction identity — a cheap-and-numerous army that buys expendable autonomous units at a discount and treats losses as expected (attrition/reinforcement mechanic).

### Anduril Roadrunner-M (reusable interceptor) + Lattice
- **What it is:** A jet-powered VTOL interceptor that launches, chases down and destroys drones/cruise threats — and lands to be reused if it doesn't fire.
- **Status (2025-26):** Operationally deployed in combat evaluation and under expanded Pentagon orders, tied into Anduril's Lattice autonomy; in March 2026 the Army consolidated counter-drone buys into a Lattice-centred contract worth up to $20B ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/air/andurils-roadrunner-drone-hunting-drone-gets-expanded-order-from-pentagon), [DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/22/army-anduril-20b-ai-counter-drone/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Reusable autonomous air-defence interceptors are a standard base-defence layer. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Deployable structure/turret — an automated interceptor battery that intercepts incoming drone strikes on a die roll; "reusable" = doesn't deplete on a miss.

### Anduril Anvil / Anvil-M (kinetic interceptor)
- **What it is:** A small quadcopter that defeats drones by ramming them (Anvil-M adds fire control + explosive payload for faster Group 2 threats).
- **Status (2025-26):** Validated against USNORTHCOM requirements at Falcon Peak and Minot AFB exercises, launched from a ruggedized ground "Launch Box" ([The Defense Post](https://thedefensepost.com/2025/10/20/anduril-demos-cuas-falcon-peak/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Cheap kinetic interceptor drones are ubiquitous perimeter defence. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Low-cost defensive equipment card — one-shot "swat" that removes an incoming enemy drone; cheaper but shorter-ranged than a Roadrunner battery.

### Anduril Bolt / Bolt-M (man-packable loitering munition)
- **What it is:** A backpack-carried quadcopter loitering munition with interchangeable warheads, aimed by AI target-lock.
- **Status (2025-26):** Under a $23.9M USMC contract for 600+ systems (Organic Precision Fires-Light), deliveries Feb 2026-Apr 2027; ~40 min endurance, ~12.5 mi range, up to 3 lb warhead ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/uncategorized/inside-andurils-bolt-m-kamikaze-drone-program)).
- **2051 maturity:** AI-locked man-portable loitering munitions are standard squad firepower. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Infantry upgrade — gives a squad an organic precision top-attack shot with a short loiter/lock delay; swap warhead for anti-personnel vs anti-armour.

### C-UAS / counter-swarm layer (kinetic + non-kinetic)
- **What it is:** The mixed defensive stack — guns, interceptor drones, jammers and directed energy — that any 2051 force needs to survive the drone threat.
- **Status (2025-26):** Maturing fast across vendors; the counter-swarm HPM piece (Epirus Leonidas) is covered under *Directed-energy weapons* above, alongside kinetic interceptors (Roadrunner/Anvil) and EW jammers ([Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/army-news/2025/microwaves-against-the-swarm-a-new-phase-in-us-counter-drone-strategy)).
- **2051 maturity:** A layered C-UAS bubble is a baseline requirement, not a luxury. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Army-building rule — a force with no C-UAS is auto-punished by drone factions; encourages every list to spend points on a counter-drone answer (jammer, interceptor, DE, or gun).

## Ground robotics

### U.S. Army Robotic Combat Vehicle (RCV) program
- **What it is:** The Army's flagship effort to field unmanned/optionally-controlled armoured ground combat "wingman" vehicles in light/medium/heavy classes.
- **Status (2025-26):** Restructured/paused — after picking Textron's Ripsaw M3 in early 2025, the Army moved in May 2025 to cancel the planned award and halt formal development, pivoting to a cheaper software-first vendor consortium rather than killing robotic ground combat outright ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/05/army-to-cancel-planned-robotic-combat-vehicle-award-pause-howitzer-competition-sources/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Unmanned armoured combat "wingmen" exist and are fielded, though the path there is bumpy. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Unmanned armour unit that acts as an expendable point element — no crew-morale/suppression penalties, but vulnerable to jamming/hacking that can stun or turn it.

### Armed quadruped robot — Ghost Robotics Vision 60 (Q-UGV) / SPUR
- **What it is:** An AI-enabled four-legged robot carrying sensors or, in the SPUR configuration, a mounted 6.5mm rifle.
- **Status (2025-26):** Unarmed Vision 60s are in real use for base security/scouting (USAF Tyndall, Nellis, Dyess); armed SPUR variants are in experimentation — tested by Marine special operators and demoed at Army events, largely framed as anti-drone gun platforms ([The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/sea/rifle-armed-robot-dogs-now-being-tested-by-marine-special-operators)).
- **2051 maturity:** Quadruped robots for sentry/logistics/scout roles are common; autonomous armed engagement stays policy-gated and human-supervised. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Sentry/point-defence deployable — a robot dog with a sensor or weapon fit; the weaponised version is a mobile anti-drone/overwatch turret, but firing autonomously may cost a "control/authorization" resource.

### Optionally-manned combat vehicle — XM30 (ex-OMFV, Bradley replacement)
- **What it is:** The Army's next infantry fighting vehicle, designed to fight crewed or fully remote and to command robotic wingmen.
- **Status (2025-26):** In engineering/prototyping — GD Land Systems and American Rheinmetall are building full-scale prototypes (delivery early 2026) after a June 2025 Milestone B, though Army leadership hesitated in Feb 2026 to lock the design; LRIP targeted late 2027 ([European Security & Defence](https://euro-sd.com/2025/10/articles/armament/47126/xm30-enters-prototyping-phase-bradley-successor-taking-shape/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Optionally-manned armoured vehicles that can drop the crew for high-risk tasks are standard. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Convertible unit — the same vehicle runs "crewed" (better decisions/morale) or "remote" (immune to crew casualties, weaker autonomy), chosen at deployment.

### Medium combat/cargo UGV — Milrem Type-X / THeMIS
- **What it is:** A tracked unmanned ground vehicle family — THeMIS as a modular cargo/CASEVAC/weapons carrier, Type-X as a heavier robotic combat vehicle with up to a 50mm cannon.
- **Status (2025-26):** Combat-proven and in service — THeMIS has served with Ukraine since Aug 2022 and is used by 16 countries, with 2025 remote-weapon-station integrations; Type-X is the heavier armed development ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/09/european-firms-team-up-to-make-uk-specific-themis-combat-unmanned-ground-vehicle/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Modular tracked UGVs (swap cargo/casevac/weapon kits) are routine formation equipment. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Modular chassis unit — pick a mission module at deploy (haul supplies, evac wounded, or mount a weapon station); one platform, several roles.

### Autonomous resupply mule — U.S. Army S-MET (GDLS)
- **What it is:** An 8-wheeled robotic "mule" carrying up to ~1,000-2,500 lb of squad gear, evacuating casualties, and exporting power to recharge kit.
- **Status (2025-26):** Fielded — the Army completed delivery of ~624 S-METs by ~early 2025, ran fully-autonomous variants with units in Hawaii (2024), and is scoping a larger MMET follow-on ([Task & Purpose](https://taskandpurpose.com/tech-tactics/army-s-met-robotic-mule-fielding/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Autonomous logistics/casevac ground robots are standard organic support for foot units. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Support deployable — a mule that offloads carry-weight (unlocks heavier loadouts), recharges drone/energy equipment, and can drag a downed model to safety.

*(Powered load-carry exoskeletons — the logistics/combat cousin of these robots — are covered under **Soldier systems → Powered exoskeletons & exosuits**.)*

## EW, cyber & comms

### GNSS jamming & spoofing (battlefield PNT denial)
- **What it is:** Broadcasting noise on GPS/GNSS bands (jamming) or fake satellite signals (spoofing) to blind or misdirect enemy drones, munitions and navigation.
- **Status (2025-26):** A permanent condition of the Ukraine war and a standing feature of the Baltic/Black Sea/eastern Med — the Secure World Foundation calls GNSS interference "a constant of modern conflict" ([Inside GNSS](https://insidegnss.com/gnss-interference-now-a-constant-of-modern-conflict-swf-annual-report-finds/)); the counter has been fibre-optic tethered FPV drones that emit no jammable RF ([GIS Reports](https://www.gisreportsonline.com/r/ukraine-diy-drones/)).
- **2051 maturity:** GNSS denial is the default state of any contested zone; unjammable/fibered or inertial fallbacks are standard. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A deployable "Jammer Node" zone-marker that disables enemy guided/drone abilities and PNT-dependent gear inside its radius — countered only by wired or unguided units.

### Electronic-attack systems (TLS-BCT / Krasukha / Leer-3)
- **What it is:** Vehicle- and backpack-mounted suites fusing electronic attack, SIGINT and cyber to detect, locate and jam enemy emitters.
- **Status (2025-26):** The US Army's Lockheed-built TLS-BCT is fielding, with the dismounted TLS Manpack rolling out toward full BCT coverage by ~FY28 ([DOT&E FY2025](https://www.dote.osd.mil/Portals/97/pub/reports/FY2025/army/2025tls-bct.pdf)); Russia fields Krasukha-4 (jams radar/satcom to ~300 km) and Leer-3 (Orlan-paired cellular/GPS attack) ([Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/focus-analysis-conflicts/army/defence-security-industry-technology/analysis-how-russia-s-krasukha-electronic-warfare-system-disrupts-uavs-and-radars-in-ukraine)).
- **2051 maturity:** EW suites shrink to squad/platoon backpacks and drone payloads; every maneuver unit carries organic electronic attack. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An "EW Specialist" model or vehicle upgrade with a reactive ability to suppress an enemy unit's comms/overwatch or geolocate-and-mark whoever used a radio this turn.

### Battlefield mesh networks / resilient tactical comms (MANET)
- **What it is:** Self-healing mobile ad-hoc radio networks where every node relays for the others, so there is no central tower to knock out.
- **Status (2025-26):** Silvus StreamCaster and TrellisWare TSM radios are the fielded backbone; Anduril bridges MANET waveforms into one fabric and pairs Silvus radios to control unmanned vehicles in comms-denied conditions; Motorola acquired Silvus for $4.4B in 2025, signalling mass scaling ([Military & Aerospace Electronics](https://www.militaryaerospace.com/computers/article/16722120/silvus-anduril-join-forces-on-ad-hoc-networking-to-link-unmanned-vehicles-over-tactical-radios), [CTOL](https://www.ctol.digital/news/motorola-100m-silvus-expansion-manet-radios-drone-era/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Every soldier, drone and vehicle is a mesh node; the network degrades gracefully rather than failing. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "Relay Node" deployable extending a command/coordination aura; killing nodes shrinks the network and cuts squad buffs — a faction identity for a networked, drone-heavy army.

### AI-enabled offensive cyber at the tactical edge
- **What it is:** Autonomous/semi-autonomous software agents that find and exploit enemy network/device vulnerabilities in-battle for a tactical effect.
- **Status (2025-26):** DARPA's 2025 AI Cyber Challenge proved AI agents can autonomously discover, exploit and patch real software flaws at machine speed ([OODA Loop](https://oodaloop.com/analysis/disruptive-technology/lessons-learned-about-offensive-ai-the-darpa-ai-cyber-challenge-aixcc-and-team-atlantas-victory/)), and the Army's new PEO is pushing AI-enabled cyber to the tactical edge ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/08/new-army-pca-wants-more-ai-enabled-cyber-at-the-edge-for-offensive-defensive-ops/)).
- **2051 maturity:** On-device cyber agents that hijack or brick enemy drones/comms mid-fight are plausible but bounded by hardening and policy. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "Cyber Intrusion" action — roll to seize control of an enemy drone/turret for a turn or disable its activation; a specialist-hacker unit's signature attack.

### Assured / alternative PNT (GPS-denied navigation)
- **What it is:** Navigating without GPS by fusing inertial, vision-to-map matching, celestial star-tracking, magnetic-field (MagNav) and signals-of-opportunity sensing.
- **Status (2025-26):** Honeywell's HANA fuses vision/celestial/magnetic/inertial into one GPS-free stack ([Honeywell](https://aerospace.honeywell.com/us/en/products-and-services/products/navigation-and-sensors/navigation-systems/honeywell-alternative-navigation-architecture)); Q-CTRL's quantum magnetometer "Ironstone Opal" flew ~111× better than high-end inertial in GPS-denied trials, and assured-PNT is an active procurement priority ([GPS World](https://www.gpsworld.com/implementing-assured-pnt-for-static-and-dynamic-applications/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Multi-sensor assured PNT is standard kit; quantum-inertial and celestial nav make GPS optional. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An "Assured Nav" equipment tag letting a unit ignore the Jammer-zone penalty — a premium upgrade that keeps guided/drone abilities online in denied terrain.

### Counter-EW: LPI/LPD comms & frequency hopping
- **What it is:** Low-probability-of-intercept/detection waveforms — spread-spectrum, fast frequency hopping, tight beamforming — that keep a radio from being found, jammed or direction-found.
- **Status (2025-26):** LPI/LPD anti-jam is now a headline radio feature (Silvus markets frequency-hopping, beamforming "spectrum dominance"), and AI-driven adaptive beam-steering SDRs showed 3× LPI/LPD gains at the Navy's Silent Swarm 2025 exercise ([Silvus](https://silvustechnologies.com/why-silvus/spectrum-dominance/), [Blu Wireless](https://www.bluwireless.com/insight/lpd-lpi-stealth-tactical-communications/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Emission control and LPI are baked into all comms; being detected on the spectrum is itself a targeting event. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An "Emissions Control" stance — a unit stays hidden from enemy EW-marking but trades away its own network buffs while silent; a stealth-vs-coordination tradeoff.

## Sensing

### Multispectral / hyperspectral targeting sensors
- **What it is:** Cameras reading dozens-to-hundreds of narrow spectral bands per pixel, giving each material a "fingerprint" that defeats visual camouflage.
- **Status (2025-26):** Fielded on UAVs and recon platforms to expose camouflaged vehicles, disturbed earth and concealed positions under foliage ([Cubert](https://cubert-hyperspectral.com/en/hyperspectral-uavs-for-defense-security/)); the threat is real enough that Saab and others now market counter-hyperspectral camouflage ([Saab](https://www.saab.com/newsroom/stories/2024/october/emerging-solutions-to-beat-the-hyperspectral-threat)).
- **2051 maturity:** Lightweight hyperspectral is a standard drone/recon payload; visual camo alone no longer hides you. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "Spectral Scanner" sensor that negates an enemy's Camouflage/Stealth save within LOS — countered only by spectral-defeating cloak equipment (an arms race with adaptive camo).

### Through-wall / sense-through-wall radar (Camero-Tech Xaver)
- **What it is:** Handheld/portable radar that images live bodies and movement behind walls for urban breaching and clearing.
- **Status (2025-26):** Camero-Tech's Xaver line (100 handheld, 400 tactical, 1000 3D AI-tracking) is operationally fielded in ~60 countries and won fresh 2025 military/police contracts ([Defence Industry Europe](https://defence-industry.eu/camero-tech-showcases-proven-through-wall-radar-systems-at-mspo-2025-poised-for-rapid-delivery-across-europe/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Compact through-wall imaging is standard urban-assault kit with sharper 3D and auto-tagging of occupants. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "Wall Scanner" gadget revealing enemy models inside a building/behind cover before you breach, removing their ambush/hidden-deployment bonus.

### Distributed unattended ground sensor (UGS) networks
- **What it is:** Air-dropped or hand-emplaced seismic/acoustic/magnetic/IR sensors that autonomously detect foot and vehicle movement and relay it over a mesh.
- **Status (2025-26):** A growing fielded market (~$505M in 2025) built on multi-sensor fusion and low-power mesh; products like McQ's RANGER are in service ([Future Market Insights](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/unattended-ground-sensors-market), [McQ](https://www.mcqinc.com/products/mcq-ranger-unattended-ground-sensor/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Cheap, air-scattered smart-dust sensor fields blanket contested ground and cue fires automatically. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A pre-game "Sensor Field" deployable dropped in a zone — any enemy entering it is auto-marked/loses infiltration, feeding your indirect-fire units.

### AI Automatic Target Recognition (Project Maven / Maven Smart System)
- **What it is:** AI that ingests drone/satellite/sensor feeds and automatically detects, classifies and nominates targets for human decision.
- **Status (2025-26):** Palantir's Maven Smart System is a Pentagon program of record with 80,000+ users across the Joint Staff, combatant commands and NATO; the contract ceiling was raised toward ~$1.3B through 2029 ([CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/what-maven-smart-system-and-what-does-it-do), [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/pentagon-boosts-budget-for-palantirs-ai-software-in-major-expansion-of-project-maven/)).
- **2051 maturity:** AI ATR is ubiquitous and fast; the open question is how much lethal autonomy policy allows off the human loop. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A faction-level "Kill Web" resource — spend AI-targeting tokens to re-roll to-hit or auto-acquire the highest-value enemy target, defining a tech-heavy "sensor-shooter" army.

### Acoustic gunshot detection / counter-sniper sensing
- **What it is:** Microphone arrays triangulating a shooter's bearing, range and elevation from muzzle blast and supersonic crack.
- **Status (2025-26):** Mature and fielded — Raytheon/BBN's vehicle-mounted Boomerang has equipped thousands of US vehicles ([Defense Update](https://defense-update.com/20090901_boomerang.html)) — with the market shifting to sub-500g soldier-wearable modules and acoustic+optical fusion ([Research Nester](https://www.researchnester.com/reports/anti-sniper-detection-system-market/7301)).
- **2051 maturity:** Wearable gunshot detection is standard soldier kit, instantly cueing the squad and networked drones to the shooter. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "Shot Spotter" upgrade — the first time an enemy fires at your unit from concealment, the shooter is immediately revealed and can be counter-fired by any networked ally.

## Power, logistics & autonomy

### Project Pele — transportable military microreactor
- **What it is:** A truck/ship-portable 1.5-MWe high-temperature gas-cooled microreactor packed into four 20-ft containers to power a base off-grid.
- **Status (2025-26):** BWXT began core fabrication in July 2025 and delivered the full TRISO fuel core to Idaho National Lab on 5 Nov 2025; the assembled unit ships to INL in 2026, testing targeted 2027, power generation by 2028 ([ANS Nuclear Newswire](https://www.ans.org/news/2025-12-04/article-7595/project-pele-progress-bwxt-delivers-fuel-to-inl/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Mobile microreactors are a mature, fielded base-power option. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A deployable "Reactor Container" objective/structure — powers a faction's directed-energy and C2 assets; capturing or breaching it browns-out those systems.

### ANPI / Janus fixed-base microreactors
- **What it is:** DoD/DIU + Army programme to license and build commercial microreactors (kW up to ~20 MWe) permanently powering major installations.
- **Status (2025-26):** DIU authorized 8 vendors (incl. BWXT, General Atomics, Oklo, Radiant, Westinghouse, X-energy) in April 2025; the Army's Janus programme named 9 candidate posts, aiming to power bases by ~2030 ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/11/diu-seeks-microreactors-from-industry-as-army-ids-bases-for-nuclear-power/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Nuclear-powered garrisons and forward hubs are routine. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Faction identity trait — a "nuclear-secured base" grants unlimited power for shields/DEW but is a high-value strategic target on the campaign map.

### Deployable tactical microgrids (solar + storage)
- **What it is:** Containerized solar-plus-battery-plus-smart-control power packages that cut a forward base's diesel dependence and fuel-convoy exposure.
- **Status (2025-26):** Army policy targets a microgrid on every installation by 2035 and self-sustaining renewables + storage for critical missions by 2040; DIU's STEEP effort and vendors (PowerSecure, SFC Energy) field vehicle-transportable hybrid storage ([DIU](https://www.diu.mil/latest/enhanced-energy-storage-and-intelligent-power-management-systems-for-defense), [Microgrid Knowledge](https://www.microgridknowledge.com/military-microgrids/article/55166408/leading-the-charge-3-army-installations-launch-pioneering-microgrids)).
- **2051 maturity:** Silent, solar-buffered microgrids are the default forward-base power baseline. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Deployable "Microgrid" terrain piece — silent (no thermal/acoustic signature to detect), but its battery bank explodes if hit; feeds nearby powered equipment.

### Conformal Wearable Battery & soldier power
- **What it is:** A flat, flexible Li-ion vest battery acting as the central power source for a networked dismounted soldier's sensors, radios and AR optics.
- **Status (2025-26):** CWB 3.6.2 is in production with v3.7 upcoming; Army R&D is prototyping silicon-anode packs (~29% smaller/lighter for equal energy) to feed high-draw systems like IVAS ([U.S. Army Batteries](https://battery.army.mil/warfighter-hub/conformal-wearable-battery/)).
- **2051 maturity:** High-density body-worn power feeding AR/HUD and optics is standard soldier kit. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** Every trooper carries a "power budget" — running optics, active camo or exosuit boosts drains the wearable battery; depletion downgrades their sensor/targeting stats.

### Autonomous ground resupply (Leader-Follower + ULTRA)
- **What it is:** Driverless cargo trucks that either follow a crewed lead vehicle in convoy or self-navigate resupply runs, removing soldiers from the most-ambushed logistics leg.
- **Status (2025-26):** Army GVSC's Expedient Leader-Follower fields robotic-kitted trucks (one crewed leader, up to 9 driverless followers); Overland AI's ULTRA ran fully autonomous cargo runs (1,000 lb, 8+ hr) in GPS/comms-denied terrain at Exercise Agile Spirit 25 (July 2025) ([Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/army-news/2025/exclusive-report-u-s-army-tests-ultra-autonomous-tactical-vehicle-to-reduce-risk-in-frontline-logistics-operations)).
- **2051 maturity:** Uncrewed convoy resupply is normal doctrine. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A driverless "Mule" transport unit — no crew casualties, reconfigurable bay (ammo / casevac / sensors), but jammable and easily hijacked if left unescorted.

### DARPA RACER off-road autonomy stack
- **What it is:** A transferable software "autonomy stack" that lets any ground vehicle drive fast off-road with no GPS and no pre-mapped route.
- **Status (2025-26):** RACER finished final tests and transitioned to the services in Jan 2026 after 2025 Army demos — a robotic breaching platform clearing minefields at Ft. Hood (Oct 2025) and autonomous long-range recon with the 11th ACR ([DARPA](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2026/racer-finish-line)).
- **2051 maturity:** GPS-independent off-road autonomy is a commodity layer bolted onto most robotic vehicles. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** An "autonomy module" upgrade usable on multiple chassis — grants a robotic unit off-road movement and function even inside a GPS-jamming/EW bubble.

### TRUAS / TRV150 tactical resupply drone
- **What it is:** An electric VTOL quad-rotor cargo drone that autonomously flies ammo, water, blood and medical supplies (up to ~150 lb, ~70 km) direct to frontline squads.
- **Status (2025-26):** Malloy/SURVICE's TRV150 is on a ~200-unit USMC production contract, reached IOC in Nov 2024, and was exercised operationally by the 31st MEU and CLB-7 through 2025 ([AFCEA Signal](https://www.afcea.org/signal-media/marine-corps-tactical-resupply-program-gains-autonomous-flight-system)).
- **2051 maturity:** Autonomous aerial "last-mile" resupply is fully mainstream. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "Resupply Drone" deployable — spend an action to fly ammo/med tokens to a pinned squad; slow and interceptable, so it becomes a mini-objective for enemy AA.

### SPEE3D expeditionary metal 3D printing (EMU)
- **What it is:** A containerized field factory that cold-sprays dense metal parts plus heat-treats and machines them, replacing broken components in hours instead of shipping them in.
- **Status (2025-26):** SPEE3D's Expeditionary Manufacturing Unit is fielded/demonstrated with US, UK, Australian and Japanese forces; the US Army ran cold-spray field demos in 2025 printing metal on-site ([Additive Manufacturing Media](https://www.additivemanufacturing.media/products/spee3ds-expeditionary-manufacturing-unit-3d-prints-large-metal-parts-in-the-field)).
- **2051 maturity:** Point-of-need metal/composite fabrication of spares and even airframes is standard. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "Fab Unit" structure that repairs damaged vehicles/drones or prints new drone airframes between rounds — a logistics engine worth raiding to cripple an enemy's staying power.

### Palantir Maven Smart System / CJADC2 AI decision aid
- **What it is:** An AI-enabled command-and-control platform fusing sensor feeds into a single interface for faster targeting, awareness and cross-service coordination.
- **Status (2025-26):** Maven is in production across 10+ combatant commands; a March 2026 DepSecDef directive made it a formal Program of Record and the "cornerstone" of CJADC2, on top of a ~$100M Army Research Lab expansion ([DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/04/03/palantir-maven-feinberg-directive/)).
- **2051 maturity:** AI-fused targeting and decision support is the assumed C2 backbone. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A faction "C2 network" buff — while the command node lives, units share a targeting picture (reroll to-hit / act out of activation order); destroy the node and the army fights blind.

### SFC Energy Jenny methanol fuel cell (silent power)
- **What it is:** A backpack-sized direct-methanol fuel cell + battery hybrid giving silent, emission-free, thermally-quiet power for dismounted teams and sensor sites.
- **Status (2025-26):** Sold to US and NATO forces today; SFC unveiled a next-gen Jenny at SOF Week May 2025 rated ~100 W / 2,400 Wh with fuel energy density far above Li-ion ([SFC Energy](https://www.sfc.com/investors/sfc-energy-presents-next-generation-portable-tactical-fuel-cell-systems-at-special-operations-forces-week-sof-week-united-states-newsid2980688/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Signature-free fuel-cell power for recon/special-ops teams is common. `[near-certain]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A "silent power" special-ops trait — a fuel-cell team runs sensors/comms with no thermal or acoustic signature, defeating detection-by-power-draw rules.

### DEW power/cooling packs (directed-energy enablement)
- **What it is:** The onboard hybrid power+cooling pack (batteries recharged by gensets) that stores and dumps the energy a mobile high-energy laser needs to fire.
- **Status (2025-26):** nLIGHT is delivering 50-kW-class lasers for DE M-SHORAD; the Army calls the lasers "pretty mature" but flags mobile power/heat-dissipation as the hard problem, funding ~$445M FY26-28 toward an Enduring HEL ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/08/armys-laser-weapons-pretty-mature-could-contribute-to-next-gen-missile-defense/)).
- **2051 maturity:** Vehicle-portable pulsed power for DEWs is solved and widespread. `[plausible]`
- **GAME HOOK:** A laser weapon's "capacitor charge" mechanic — limited shots per turn gated by an onboard power reserve; moving fast or losing the genset caps its rate of fire.

## Faction-differentiation ideas

*Raw material for a later factions session. Each is a distinct answer to the 2051 battlefield's core questions — mass vs. precision, network vs. jam, power vs. mobility — built from the tech above. Five archetypes:*

### 1. The Remnant (high-tech military successor)
The inheritors of a collapsed conventional army's best gear: NGSW rifles with smart optics, IVAS/EagleEye HUDs, APS-protected optionally-manned vehicles (XM30), and combat/base lasers (DragonFire, Iron Beam) run off a captured microreactor. They field the fewest, most expensive models, win the sensor-and-firepower exchange, and play an attritional "pennies-per-shot" DE game — but every powered advantage collapses if their reactor/C2 node is taken or their network is jammed, so they are brittle at the edges.

### 2. The Scavengers (jury-rigged irregulars)
No fabs, no reactors — they run on looted fibre-optic FPV drones (immune to jamming), improvised loitering munitions, salvaged smoke, and whatever ammo they can 3D-print on a stolen SPEE3D unit. Cheap, numerous, and unpredictable, they ignore the network meta entirely (wired drones, emissions silence, unguided weapons) and punish high-tech foes by dragging fights into cover-dense terrain where sensors and DE matter less. Their weakness is sustained firepower and armour — they must win fast or bleed out.

### 3. The Swarm (drone-mass specialists)
Doctrine built on the DoD "Replicator" logic: buy expendable autonomous drones by the crate and treat losses as ammunition. Offensive and defensive swarms, one-way FPVs, Bolt-style man-packable munitions, and container-launched autonomous constellations let them saturate any defence — but they are hard-countered by counter-swarm HPM (Leonidas/THOR) and starved by any force that kills their relay/controller nodes. Their identity is a reinforcement/attrition mechanic no other faction has.

### 4. The Ghosts (EW / cyber faction)
They fight the spectrum, not the enemy: jammer nodes that black out GPS and drones, TLS-class electronic attack that geolocates and marks anyone who transmits, AI cyber-intrusion that hijacks enemy robots and turrets, and LPI/assured-PNT gear that keeps their own network alive in the dark. Small, elite, force-multiplier list — devastating against network-dependent factions (Remnant, Swarm), but weak in a straight kinetic fight and reliant on protecting their EW specialists.

### 5. The Foundry (robotics / automation faction)
An automation-first force where machines take the casualties: RCV/Milrem combat UGVs, armed robot dogs (Ghost Robotics SPUR), autonomous resupply mules, and a mobile fab/repair core that rebuilds losses mid-campaign. High staying power and no crew-morale penalties, but every unit is jammable and hackable (a natural nemesis for the Ghosts), and the whole force degrades if its C2/autonomy backbone is severed — trading human unpredictability for industrial resilience.

*Cross-faction design note: the archetypes form a rough rock-paper-scissors — Ghosts beat network-reliant Remnant/Foundry, Swarm beats slow armour but folds to HPM/DE, Scavengers dodge the whole network game but lack punch, and the Remnant out-guns everyone it can keep its power and network online against. That triangle is the tuning surface for a later factions pass.*
