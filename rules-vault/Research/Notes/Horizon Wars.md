> [!abstract] In one breath

## Presence is the cost

**Type:** Dice / Costing · **Take:** 📎 reference

Five stats, and **P (Presence) is simultaneously the unit's points cost and its close-combat value.** **[FACT]** Conventional elements have fixed profiles with P ∈ 1–3; only **mechs and aircraft** get a true point-buy budget.

**Why it's interesting.** It collapses the usual two-layer arrangement — a profile, plus a price derived from the profile — into one number. There is nothing to keep in sync, nothing to errata separately, and no possibility of the cost drifting from the thing it prices. **The cost *is* a stat, so it's on the card and it's used in play.**

**The obvious limit** **[INFERENCE]**: it only works because the stat it doubles as (close combat) is *correlated* with overall power, and because the game keeps conventional units on fixed profiles so the correlation never has to hold at the extremes. Point-buy is reserved for the two categories where it does.

**For Settlements.** Not adoptable — our cost comes from a multi-factor kill chain (`P(kill) = P(hit) × P(injure)`) and no single stat could carry it. **Recorded because it's the cleanest possible statement of "cost is a property of the unit"**, and because the *partial* application is the transferable idea: **fixed pre-costed profiles for the common stuff, point-buy only where customisation is the point.** That's a live option for the [[Ideas Inbox]] want to tier equipment — crafted gear as a point-buy kit, found/manufactured gear as fixed profiles.
