
## FIRST CONTACT

2027: THE ARRIVAL - Planet Earth is forever changed when an intergalactic starship materializes over the arctic circle and begins to orbit the Earth along the polar line. It's size and  beauty is breath taking, like a silver shard it cuts through the sky. It's sharp edges glimmering in the sun's glow. Everything we thought we knew about ourselves, the universe, reality itself, is gone in an instant.

2027: THE ALLIANCE - For months the alien vessel remained motionless following it's orbit around the planet each rotation the planet's citizens grew increasingly uneasy while their governments bickered about the appropriate next steps. As a response the world's super powers form a coalition of space agencies and private companies with ties to space. They call it TERA, The Extraterrestrial Relations Alliance.

2027: FIRST CONTACT - After months of attempted contact members of TERA were individually approached by strangers, men and women by bearing a message given to them by the aliens, it stated that FIRST CONTACT would be made during the first official TERA summit. Weeks later the message came true as a the alliance members were visited by a emmessaries from the alien vessel. The were humanoid, 8 feet tall, with large expressive black eyes and long elegant limbs. Their silver robes shimmering like mother of pearl in the light as they moved gfracefully, almost floating, across the floor. They each wore a metallic glove on their right hand it's glossy white exterior contained a variuety of buttons and small blinking lights. They had a similar device wrapped around the back of their heads seemingly fused to their skull it's white shell glowing from within. They stood in the center of the council chambers floor surrounded completely by the planets leaders. Accompanying the aliens were 3 human women, all dressed in the same robes, they led the emissaries into the council chamber where they stood surrounded on all sides by the planet's leaders who sat in silence unable to process what they were seeing desperatley trying to wrap their minds around the sight bvefore them. Suddenly one of the humans stood forward speaking loudly she announced the arrival of the emessareis, thanking the room for their patience and for the peacful first contact. Over the course of an hour the Alliance members did not speak or utter a word as they listened to the ominous message from the emessaries.
