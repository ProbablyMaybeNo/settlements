
## FIRST CONTACT

2027: THE ARRIVAL - Planet Earth is forever changed when an intergalactic starship materializes over the arctic circle and begins to orbit the Earth along the polar line. It's size and  beauty is breath taking, like a silver shard it cuts through the sky. It's sharp edges glimmering in the sun's glow. Everything we thought we knew about ourselves, the universe, reality itself, is gone in an instant.

2027: THE ALLIANCE - For months the alien vessel remained motionless following it's orbit around the planet each rotation the planet's citizens grew increasingly uneasy while their governments bickered about the appropriate next steps. As a response the world's super powers form a coalition of space agencies and private companies with ties to space. They call it TERA, The Extraterrestrial Relations Alliance.
