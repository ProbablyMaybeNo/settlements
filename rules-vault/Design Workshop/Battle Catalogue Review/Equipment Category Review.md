## Category direction

- Equipment primarily unlocks actions, movement options and other capabilities.
- Reusable gear normally grants a capability rather than a permanent Stat bonus.
- Most items, especially direct test bonuses, should be single-use consumables.
- Direct test bonuses are normally +2 for one declared eligible test; they do not modify Injury rolls. Exact use timing remains part of drafting.
- Medical supplies and explosives follow their own action procedures and are consumed when used.
- Tools and field gear, explosives and incendiaries, medical supplies, and stims are subcategories of Equipment. Armour has its own catalogue. Shields belong to Equipment.
- Drones, turrets, specialist mines and fields remain in the separate skill-gated Deployables catalogue. A proposed breaching charge can have an ordinary-equipment procedure.
- Reuse existing actions and conditions where possible.
