# Battle Catalogue Review

Edit the catalogue notes below to shape the battle-testing profiles. This overview displays the same tables through live embeds, so each table has one editable owner.

**To edit:** open a catalogue through its name below, switch to editing mode, and change its table cells or add rows. Return here to compare all catalogues together.

**Prices are deferred:** all game costs will be rebuilt after the rules and catalogue changes are settled. Campaign catalogues, preparations and chems are excluded.

These are working catalogues seeded from the current ChatGPT list. They are separate from the generated official item library; edits here persist and do not automatically approve or alter official cards.
