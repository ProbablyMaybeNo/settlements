## Explicit removals and held suggestions

Safety harness, trauma dressings, portable ladder and field supply case are Deferred and removed from active catalogue membership. The fire extinguisher is held out following the tentative removal; its blank additions row is not a reinstatement. A portable bridge remains an unapproved suggestion.

The existing Stimulant and Anti-anxiety dose were not explicitly removed; their effects and semantic dates remain unchanged, with concise sentences replacing shorthand. Proposed Stims is not silently substituted for Stimulant. Hooked is deferred. No Blinded rule is introduced.

Shields move from Armour to Equipment; the folding barricade moves from Deployables to Equipment. Item IDs, existing anchors, feedback and approval records are retained. Generated catalogue rows use the existing workspace renderer. Armour, weapons, deployable effects, skill effects, campaign systems and prices are not redesigned.

The Tough skill’s old failed-medkit-test trigger has no application after automatic revival. Its effect is not redesigned here; exclude it from an equipment test rather than inventing a replacement.

## Essential decisions

1. **Shield:** which protection applies? Recommended: single-use; cancel one ranged attack from the bearer’s front arc, including all its hits. No shield variants in this test.
2. **Load-bearing harness:** current Heavy profiles have no STR requirement. Recommended: ignore Unwieldy’s penalty after movement; keep normal hands and handling eligibility. This needs an explicit decision before replacing the agreed STR-requirement capability.
3. **Fire timing and actions:** recommended: first check at this round’s End Phase, then once each End Phase; no ordinary actions while burning. Keep the stated failed-first-check missed activation. The movement and 4+ extinguishing/escalation effects are already drafted.
4. **Poison timing and antidote:** recommended: −2 MOVE immediately, then at each later round’s End Phase; antidote restores only MOVE lost to Poisoned. Natural MOVE determines unconsciousness. No additional poison source is invented.
5. **Unanswered stim additions:** recommended: Crank/Dexies/Twitch give +2 to the next declared AGI/INT/DEX test this battle; Stims gives +2 MOVE for the battle. Juice’s +2 STR for the battle is already settled. Approve these proposed effects together, or leave them outside the first test.
