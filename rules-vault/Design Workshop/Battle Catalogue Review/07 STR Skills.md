| Grapple | Core | Spend Core and make an opposed STR test against one enemy within 1 inch. Failure spends Core without effect. Success makes you Grappling and the enemy Grappled. The captive cannot take actions or react and must remain within 1 inch as the holder moves along a legal route. At the start of each activation, the captive gets a free opposed STR escape test: success ends the hold and permits its normal activation; failure leaves it unable to act. While Grappling, move at half speed and use only one-handed weapons; Hauler supplies its stated exceptions. These weapon permissions allow shooting outside the held engagement. Enemy ranged attacks targeting you resolve against the captive instead. If an enemy ranged attack at another friendly passes through either model, roll one d6 for the whole attack before hit dice: 4+ keeps the intended target; 1–3 redirects it to the captive. Resolve the normal attack against the actual target, using its protection. For this interception only, the two held models do not themselves make the intended shot illegal; solid terrain and other models still block LOS normally. Area effects still affect every model in their final area. During your activation you may release the captive freely, moving it directly away to a position 3 inches from you. Stop on collision: another unit Pins both; a structure Pins the released unit. Resolve falls normally. The hold also ends if you become Pinned, Down, Unconscious or gain a morale condition; an automatic ending leaves both models in place.
