# AGI — skill compatibility review

**15 selected skills reviewed. Recommendations only: no rule card or skill approval is changed.**

The **Selected rule** column preserves the recovered 75-skill version. It is not a claim that all those effects remain playable after later decisions. The current 39-skill cards are comparison proposals; their reductions are not automatically adopted. Later explicit decisions, including removals, take precedence.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Stalker | Uses concealed approaches and close-range control to choose engagements. |
| Ghost | Stays concealed while applying ranged pressure. |
| Duelist | Wins close combat through agile weapons, defence and repositioning. |
| Traceur | Uses vertical routes and evasive movement to reach useful positions. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype / role | Selected rule | Check and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ambush** | Stalker | **Attack modifier.** After a successful CHARGE begun HIDDEN, test AGI. Pass: one melee weapon makes an UNANSWERED attack with +4 to hit and injure; the second weapon attacks normally. Fail: no bonuses; the target may spend an unused ACTION to make an UNANSWERED attack first, followed by your normal attack if still able. | **REVISE — The original +4 hit/+4 Injury plus Unanswered creates a large burst; its failed-test free enemy attack bypasses normal ALERT and reaction limits. Rear attacks alone are not Unanswered.**<br><br>Use the compact proposal: test AGI before moving on a Charge begun Hidden. Success gives Unanswered and +2 to hit; failure only removes Hidden and the bonuses, with the normal Charge continuing. No free enemy attack or Injury bonus. The defender still cancels successes, including critical-for-critical, but cannot inflict hits in the Unanswered exchange. |
| **Return to the Shadows** | Stalker; general melee repositioning | **Triggered.** Once per turn after CLOSE COMBAT, move up to 6 inches without disengage attacks. End behind or in heavy cover and outside LOS of READY enemies. Become HIDDEN if normal hiding requirements are met. | **CLARIFY — It deliberately bypasses Core Disengage and adds movement. Unrestricted triggers after defensive exchanges or free counterattacks can create repeated escapes.**<br><br>Once per own activation after a melee exchange, if still conscious/unpinned, move up to 6 inches without Disengaging. Do not enter a new engagement. End in/behind heavy cover and outside ALERT enemies’ LOS. Gain Hidden only if the normal Hide location requirements are met; do not automatically Hide in ordinary enemy LOS. No attack or movement chain follows. |
| **Incapacitate** | Stalker | **Action.** Once per turn, make a 6-inch RANGED ATTACK using AGI and one attack die. A hit causes STUNNED; do not roll for injury. | **REVISE — Shared Stunned now means Pinned and a missed activation, much stronger than its old until-next-activation lockout. An unexplained ranged AGI stun should not bypass equipment and cover.**<br><br>For the first test, make this Core against a conscious enemy in contact: one AGI hit die versus 13+, normal applicable modifiers, no Injury roll; a hit applies shared Stunned and normal hit Stress. No extra Damage, automatic Down or Subdued aftermath. Reapplications use Stunned’s no-extra-missed-activations rule. |
| **Ghost** | Ghost | **Passive.** Once per turn, resolve a ranged action without losing HIDDEN from firing. Other reveal triggers still apply. | **CLARIFY — Quiet weapons already preserve Hidden when shooting from cover. The skill remains useful by allowing a different weapon, but must not bypass other reveal triggers.**<br><br>Once per round, shooting a one-die non-Automatic firearm from cover does not itself remove Hidden. Exclude Blast/Cone; normal proximity, exposure, hit, movement and Marked reveal rules still apply. It offers weapon freedom, not stacking concealment with Quiet. |
| **Phantom Shot** | Ghost | **Passive.** When SHOOTING while HIDDEN, hits that fail to wound inflict additional STRESS. Additional amount requires ruling. | **CLARIFY — The additional Stress amount was never fixed, and Stress is now added for hits before Injury rather than only failed Injury.**<br><br>Specify +1 additional Stress for each hit from shooting while Hidden that fails to cause a Wound. This is extra to ordinary hit Stress, not a second ordinary Stress charge. Keep the global Stress maximum; normal weapon Suppression remains once per attack. Do not add accuracy or Injury bonuses. |
| **Show Yourself** | Ghost | **Passive.** Enemies can only target this unit within 6 inches while it is HIDDEN, instead of 12 inches. | **REVISE — Changing only targeting range is ineffective if the unit is still automatically revealed by ordinary exposed LOS within 12 inches.**<br><br>Reduce this unit’s Hidden targeting limit and its exposed-LOS reveal distance from 12 to 6 inches. Keep the immediate 3-inch/LOS reveal and all other reveal triggers, especially Marked. It never allows shooting through solid terrain or prevents Observe. |
| **Ambidextrous** | Duelist | **Passive.** When dual wielding two 1 HANDED melee weapons that BOTH have AGILE, the off-hand contributes its full attack dice instead of one. | **REDUNDANT — All current Agile melee weapons print one Attack Die. Full off-hand Attacks is therefore still one die, including a Goliath-held spear. Charge adds its bonus to the primary weapon.**<br><br>The original permission has no meaningful current-profile payoff. For an immediate test, replace it with one failed-hit reroll per own melee exchange when wielding two Agile one-handed melee weapons. Otherwise retain the permission but hold the skill until relevant profiles exist; do not silently turn it into extra attack dice. |
| **Blade Fury** | Duelist | **Triggered.** Once per turn, after DOWNING an enemy in melee, make a free MOVE and ATTACK against another enemy within 6 inches. If that enemy is DOWNED, repeat once more against an enemy within 3 inches. | **REVISE — Two successive free Move-and-Attack follow-ups interact explosively with Rampage, Parry and Rage, beyond the ordinary own-activation attack limit.**<br><br>Allow one bounded follow-up: after your own Fight Downs its target, spend your unused Move to move up to 3 inches and make one further melee exchange. This explicitly permits entering that engagement. No further Blade Fury, Rampage, reaction or Charge bonus. It cannot follow a Charge/Aerial Assault because Move has already been spent. |
| **Parry** | Duelist | **Triggered.** When an enemy fails to hit this unit in melee, make an UNANSWERED melee counterattack against that enemy. This may trigger BLADE FURY. Counterattack recursion requires ruling. | **REVISE — Current melee already lets a defender cancel successes and injure its attacker in the same exchange. A second counterattack duplicates that payoff and can recurse into Blade Fury.**<br><br>Replace the extra counterattack with a defensive reroll: once per round when defending in melee, reroll one failed Attack Die before cancelling successes. Resolve any resulting hits through the same exchange; no new attack, reaction or Blade Fury trigger is created by the reroll itself. |
| **Acrobatic** | Traceur | **Passive.** Climb scalable structures without climbing gear or access points. Climbing costs movement equal to half the structure's height. | **REVISE — Anyone may now climb without equipment, so that permission is redundant. Paying half the structure height instead of current half-speed climbing creates a fourfold vertical speed change.**<br><br>Climb at normal movement cost instead of half speed, retaining the usual AGI test, safe finishing position and failure rules. This gives independent faster climbing; deployed climbing gear still offers a shared ladder/no-test route. Do not retain the obsolete 3-inch jump ceiling from the reduced catalogue. |
| **Dodge** | Traceur; general defence | **Reaction.** When targeted by an enemy CHARGE or MELEE ATTACK, test AGI. On success move 3 inches. Resolve the charge, but omit its melee attack if this unit is no longer in engagement range. | **REVISE — A second independent 3-inch escape overlaps the shared Dive reaction and could evade the ALERT/once-per-round limits.**<br><br>Use the compact reroll: when making a legal Dive reaction, reroll a failed AGI test. Spend ALERT, retain the one-reaction limit, and become Pinned as normal. It does not grant a free reaction or automatic movement and adds no extra Disengage procedure. |
| **Aerial Assault** | Traceur | **Attack modifier.** From a structure at least 3 inches high, select an enemy within charge range. Roll to charge, then test AGI. If both succeed, move into engagement and make an UNANSWERED attack with +1 to hit and injure per full 2 inches of starting height above ground, maximum +4/+4. | **REPLACE — Aerial Assault is now a common Move + Core action with an AGI test and +2 attack dice. The old skill is no longer the action’s unlock and carries a conflicting height-scaled hit/Injury bonus.**<br><br>Replace this skill entry with Safe Landing: once per own activation, reroll the failed AGI test for a common Aerial Assault. A second failure uses the normal falling rule. Keep the common action’s +2 dice; no extra height-scaled bonuses or duplicate Charge roll. The new name/effect requires approval. |
| **Quick** | General mobility | **Passive.** Gain +2 MOVE. | **KEEP — A flat MOVE increase remains useful for every build. The compact +1/max-7 version was a separate reduction; armour and battle-long +4 MOVE chems make the full mobility stack important to test.**<br><br>Keep the selected +2 MOVE. It changes distance, not action count or the two-hand/Hidden rules, and applies before ordinary movement penalties. Do not silently impose the reduced catalogue’s cap of 7. Evaluate Quick plus the MOVE chem in movement tests before pricing. |
| **Sneak** | General stealth mobility; Stalker/Ghost synergy | **Passive.** Ignore the movement penalty while HIDDEN. | **CLARIFY — Normal Move removes Hidden; calling this a normal full-speed Move could accidentally cancel the very benefit it grants.**<br><br>Your Sneak action may move up to full MOVE while retaining Hidden. Other half-speed terrain/load effects still apply once. This does not permit Hidden Sprint, Charge or bypass proximity/LOS reveal rules. |
| **Sprinter** | General mobility; ranged repositioning synergy | **Passive.** DASH using only the MOVE action instead of spending both actions. | **REVISE — Move-only double MOVE leaves Core for shooting and multiplies Quick/chem movement. It does not inherently allow a Charge after spending Move.**<br><br>Proposed test limit: a Move-only Sprint travels up to MOVE +4 inches and removes Hidden, leaving Core available. A following Shoot is legal; mid-Sprint Run + Gun is not automatic. Charge/Aerial Assault still require both unused actions. This is a suggested smaller distance bonus, not the approved original double-MOVE value. |

## Category priority

Common climbing/Aerial Assault and one-die melee invalidate old unlocks; bound follow-up attacks and movement/chem combinations.
