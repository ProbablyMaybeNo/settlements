# AGI — twelve-skill working list

Three archetypes with three skills each, plus three general skills. Specific skill wording may override normal modifier, action and reaction limits.

**Status:** STR effects are accepted. DEX, AGI and INT archetype cuts follow Ross’s latest direction; individual effects remain subject to their category review. NRV’s Berserker swaps and general shortlist below are recommendations.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Stalker | Uses Hidden approaches for close-combat ambushes. |
| Ghost | Applies ranged pressure while concealed. |
| Duelist | Uses dual melee weapons for close-combat damage. |

## Skills

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule | Recommendation / status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ambush** | Stalker | Charge from Hidden and test AGI. Success makes one weapon’s attack Unanswered with +4 hit and Injury. On failure, the target may spend an unused action to attack Unanswered first. | Keep the ambush payoff and risk; use the current Charge procedure. |
| **Return to the Shadows** | Stalker | Once per round after melee, move up to 6 inches without Disengaging. End in/behind heavy cover and outside ALERT enemies’ LOS; Hide if eligible. | Keep the escape and movement exception. |
| **Incapacitate** | Stalker | Core: make a one-die AGI ranged attack within 6 inches. A hit causes Stunned instead of Injury. | Keep; use the shared Stunned condition. |
| **Ghost** | Ghost | Once per round, shooting does not itself remove your Hidden condition. Other reveal triggers still apply. | Keep; no new weapon restriction is needed. |
| **Phantom Shot** | Ghost | When shooting while Hidden, non-wounding hits inflict additional Stress. | Choose the missing amount; I suggest +1 extra Stress per attack that hits without wounding. |
| **Show Yourself** | Ghost | While Hidden, enemies can target you only within 6 inches instead of 12. | Keep. Use 6 inches for the matching Hidden exposure distance too. |
| **Ambidextrous** | Duelist | When using two Agile one-handed melee weapons, use the off-hand weapon’s full Attack Dice. | Keep. Current one-die eligible weapons give it no extra dice. |
| **Blade Fury** | Duelist | Once per round, after Downing an enemy in melee, move and attack another within 6 inches. If it goes Down, repeat once against another within 3 inches. | Keep the explicit follow-up attacks; no added unused-Move cost. |
| **Parry** | Duelist | When an enemy fails to hit you in melee, make an Unanswered counterattack. It may trigger Blade Fury. | Keep the counterattack. Set a trigger limit to prevent mutual Parry loops; suggest once per round. |
| **Quick** | General | Gain +2 MOVE. | Keep. |
| **Sneak** | General | Move at full speed while Sneaking instead of half speed. | Keep. |
| **Sprinter** | General | Sprint using only Move instead of Move + Core. | Keep the action-cost exception and normal Sprint distance. |

## Parked from the previous selection
