# Marksman decisions

- **Marked:** until the start of the marking unit’s next activation. Surveillance drones use the same timing.
- **Steady Breathing:** +12 inches to two-handed ranged weapons; no marking or Mark Target range benefit.
- **Head Shot:** retain Marked and stationary triggers. Test +2 and +4, or a +4/+6 bonus restricted to one-die non-Automatic firearms. The value and restriction are not locked. Paired pistols and Brutal shotguns are test cases, not new restrictions.
