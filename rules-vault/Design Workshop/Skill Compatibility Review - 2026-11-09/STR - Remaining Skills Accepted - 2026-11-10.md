# Remaining STR skills — selected effects accepted

Ross accepted the remaining twelve STR skills at their selected effects and rejected the report’s added restrictions and expanded explanations. Retain concise wording; do not unpack “ignore the hit’s effects” into a new subsystem. The Grapple/Hauler/Carrying/Throw replacement remains the separately confirmed version.

This accepts the effects below, not my alternative revisions. In particular, retain Tough’s selected +1 Wound as a specific skill exception rather than the obsolete self-treatment reroll or my proposed Injury reroll. Do not restore unrelated retired rules.

## Accepted effects

| Skill | Role | Selected rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brace | Tank | BRACE until the end of the turn: incoming attacks suffer -4 to hit. This unit cannot move, act or react while BRACING. |
| Come On Then | Tank | Test STR. On success, enemies within 6 inches must target this unit with their ATTACK actions. Duration and legal-target exceptions require ruling. |
| Tough as Nails | Tank | Once per round, when attacked in melee without being wounded, ignore the hit's effects. Subsequent attacks against this unit suffer -4 to injure until the end of the turn. |
| Rampage | Charger | After DOWNING an enemy with a CHARGE ATTACK, move the remaining charge distance. If this brings the unit within 1 inch of another enemy, make a free MELEE ATTACK against it. |
| Bull | Charger | When declaring a CHARGE, test STR. On success, pass through non-LOS-blocking scatter, obstacles and barricades. Enemies contacted test AGI; on failure, push them 2 inches in your chosen direction and PIN them. Cannot pass reinforced doors, buildings or LOS-blocking structures. |
| Human Bullet | Charger | Determine CHARGE distance using MOVE plus the total of 2d6 instead of 1d6. |
| Rage | Berserker | When resolving a MELEE ATTACK, add up to +3 Damage to its Injury rolls. After the attack is resolved, gain 1 STRESS for each point added. |
| I'm About to Break | Berserker | Once per turn when CHARGED, use STRESS as positive modifiers instead of penalties for that combat. Afterwards gain 1 STRESS and immediately make a BREAK test, ignoring beneficial Stress modifiers from friendly units, skills or equipment. |
| Blowing Off Steam | Berserker; general takedown recovery | Whenever this unit DOWNS an enemy, remove 2 of its STRESS. |
| Goliath | General weapon/loadout flexibility | Wield two 2 HANDED melee weapons as if they were 1 HANDED. The off-hand still contributes only one attack die unless another rule explicitly overrides it. |
| Tough | General resilience; Tank synergy | Gain +1 WOUND, subject to the unit's Wound ceiling. |
| Big Fella | General heavy-weapon handling | Ignore UNWIELDY on this unit's weapons. |

## Pinning discussion

The current working list still says that each hit gives 1 Stress and a hit unit with a Wound remaining becomes Pinned. Ross is checking whether ordinary hits should instead give Stress only, with Pinned caused by specific effects. No change to Pinning, and no two-hit threshold, is approved by this discussion.

The earlier compatibility table is retained as historical review; its suggested changes to these twelve effects are superseded by this acceptance.
