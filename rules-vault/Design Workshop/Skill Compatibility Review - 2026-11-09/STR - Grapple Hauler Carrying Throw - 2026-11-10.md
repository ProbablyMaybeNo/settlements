# Grapple, Hauler, Carrying and Throw

Ross’s 10 November replacement rules and three follow-up answers. This supersedes the earlier STR report’s recommendations for Grapple, Strong Arm and Throw; the other twelve STR skills remain under review. Strong Arm is renamed **Hauler**, retaining V2-309 and its original file/anchor for link continuity.

## Drafted testing rules

### Grapple and Grappled

Spend Core and make an opposed STR test against one enemy within 1 inch. Failure spends Core without effect. Success makes you Grappling and the enemy Grappled. The captive cannot take actions or react and must remain within 1 inch as the holder moves along a legal route. At the start of each activation, the captive gets a free opposed STR escape test: success ends the hold and permits its normal activation; failure leaves it unable to act. While Grappling, move at half speed and use only one-handed weapons; Hauler supplies its stated exceptions. These weapon permissions allow shooting outside the held engagement. Enemy ranged attacks targeting you resolve against the captive instead. If an enemy ranged attack at another friendly passes through either model, roll one d6 for the whole attack before hit dice: 4+ keeps the intended target; 1–3 redirects it to the captive. Resolve the normal attack against the actual target, using its protection. For this interception only, the two held models do not themselves make the intended shot illegal; solid terrain and other models still block LOS normally. Area effects still affect every model in their final area. During your activation you may release the captive freely, moving it directly away to a position 3 inches from you. Stop on collision: another unit Pins both; a structure Pins the released unit. Resolve falls normally. The hold also ends if you become Pinned, Down, Unconscious or gain a morale condition; an automatic ending leaves both models in place. Throw can release a captive by its own 6-inch procedure instead.

### Hauler — formerly Strong Arm

Ignore the movement reduction from Grappling or Carrying a unit or structure. While Grappling, you may also use non-Heavy two-handed weapons. This does not grant that two-handed permission while Carrying, ignore Unwieldy, add hands or waive other carrying restrictions.

### Carry and Carrying

Spend Core and test STR while in base contact with a structure no larger than 3 inches or a Down unit. Success makes you Carrying it; failure spends Core and leaves it in place. No Grapple skill is required. Move at half speed and Fight or Shoot with only one one-handed weapon. During your activation you may freely Drop the load into an open position within 2 inches. One unit carries one load; there is no joint two-carrier action. Existing Sprint/Charge restrictions remain. Hauler and the load-bearing harness ignore their stated movement penalties, not the one-handed Carrying restriction. Carrying an enemy does not itself Secure it or make it a captured prisoner; use Secure and extraction for capture. Mission cargo still follows its scenario-specific handling rules.

### Throw

While Carrying a structure or unit, or Grappling an enemy, spend Core and test STR. Failure spends Core and retains the load or hold. Success ends Carrying or the hold and moves the thrown model 6 inches in a straight line away from you; do not also apply the ordinary 3-inch release. Thrown unit: stop on another unit and Pin both. Collision with Heavy terrain causes one Damage 4 Injury roll. Resolve falls normally. Thrown structure: each unit it contacts moves with it for the remaining distance and suffers one Damage 4 Injury roll. Resolve any resulting falls. Use normal forced-movement stops at immovable terrain and legal non-overlapping final positions; the structure’s stated pushing of contacted units overrides the ordinary stop-on-a-unit rule. Use normal Injury and casualty rules for these automatic collision hits; falls are separate hazards.

## Confirmed follow-up answers

- Retain the existing automatic release triggers: holder Pinned, Down, Unconscious or gaining a morale condition.
- Throw can use a conscious Grappled enemy as well as a Carried structure or Down unit.
- Roll interception once for the whole shooting attack, not per Attack Die.

## Integration

- Failed ordinary STR tests spend Core without moving the load or releasing the hold. Successful free activation-start escape ends Grappled and permits the normal activation; it adds no free separation move.
- Existing forced movement stops at solid obstructions and uses non-overlapping final positions. Throwing a structure has its stated exception for pushing contacted models. Apply each stated collision Injury once per affected unit; falls remain separate.
- Dropping uses an open position within 2 inches. Voluntary Grapple release goes to 3 inches from the holder; Throw uses its own 6-inch movement instead.
- The old ordinary Carry requirements of Grapple ownership, free pickup during Move and prior Secure are superseded. Secure/extraction still determines actual prisoner capture. Mission-specific cargo rules remain specific exceptions.
- Existing one-carrier and no-Sprint/Charge carrying restrictions are retained; Hauler does not waive them. Its two-handed permission applies to Grappling, not Carrying. Harness movement relief still applies to Carrying; it does not automatically gain Hauler’s grapple movement/weapon exceptions.
- The interception is a specific exception to the two held models blocking the intended shot; it does not permit shooting through walls or remove normal area-effect hits.
- No blanket Ignore Heavy/Unwieldy is retained in Hauler. Use the harness and Big Fella for their own defined benefits.
