# Final wording review — sixty skills

Reviewed all five twelve-skill working lists. The structure works; explicit modifier, action and reaction exceptions are not defects. No additional limits are proposed simply because a skill is strong.

## Material issues

| Skills / area | Issue | Minimal recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gunslinger and Ambidextrous | Every current eligible weapon has one printed Attack Die. Its full off-hand dice therefore equal the ordinary allowance: neither skill currently adds anything. | Give the off hand one extra die, or supply eligible multi-die weapon profiles. Decide before testing their benefit. |
| Brace, Come On Then, Tough as Nails | “End of turn” is ambiguous under alternating activations, and Come On Then has no expiry. Ending Brace with its own activation could make it ineffective. | Use a named expiry. Suggested common choice: start of the skill user’s next activation. No extra targeting rules needed. |
| Phantom Shot | Additional Stress has no amount. | Suggested test value: +1 Stress per non-wounding hit. |
| Parry | Two Parry users could repeatedly counter each other after cancelled/failed attacks. | “A Parry counterattack cannot trigger another Parry.” No once-per-round nerf is required merely to stop that loop. |
| Hacker’s remote terrain role | The current portable Hack only disables electronic deployables/drones. Neural Uplink’s bonus alone does not permit operating a terminal from range. | Let Neural Uplink operate a terminal through a hacking kit at the existing 8-inch portable-Hack range, in addition to its test bonus. This permission needs adopting. |
| Rage and Implode | Their intended combo meets the ordinary rule allowing only the largest skill bonus. The simulator must not decide whether their Injury benefits stack by accident. | Suggested explicit exception: “Rage and Implode stack.” Otherwise retain the normal skill stacking rule. |

## Test choices, not blockers

- **Head Shot:** compare +2/+4 and the restricted single-shot +4/+6 options. For the restricted test, define single-shot by the weapon’s printed one die/non-Automatic profile, not the final modified attack pool. No value is locked.
- **Hit Stress:** retain +1 per hit as the baseline; compare +1 per attack as a separate test configuration.
- **Human Bullet:** derive Charge declaration range from MOVE +2d6, rather than leaving the ordinary MOVE +6 check in the code.
- **Implode:** its increased maximum uses the highest morale band at 5+ Stress; it does not gain immunity above 5.
- **Watch Out:** “your action or reaction” has been expressed as “your Core or ALERT.” The granted ally shot is its intentional exception.
- **Chemical Cocktail:** the two-dose permission overrides the ordinary one-dose limit, but does not itself grant free actions. Doc, available actions and any legal reactions still supply their stated administration opportunities. Do not invent free doses.
- **Stabilize:** the concise wording separates automatic revival from its conscious-unit Stress treatment. There is no new revival test.
- **Auras:** use one shared definition in the simulation contract, rather than repeating radius, recipient and duration conventions in every skill. Any conventions not already fixed must be labelled simulation assumptions.

## Simulation integration

The current Skill Lab README explicitly targets the September 75-skill/d20-15+ contract. Current owning cards also retain some older melee and morale wording. Consolidate and snapshot the sixty-skill selection with the current core rules before modifying/running that engine. This is implementation/data work, not another set of player-facing rules.

No simulations were run for this review. The report identifies functional gaps, not balance verdicts; parked skills remain recoverable.

## Weapon update — 2026-11-10

Dagger/Shiv and Sword/Machete now print ATK 2, so Ambidextrous can improve the off-hand melee contribution. Gunslinger still needs a useful benefit while the current one-handed firearms print ATK 1. This update does not approve the other pending skill recommendations.
