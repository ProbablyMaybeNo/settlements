# DEX — skill compatibility review

**15 selected skills reviewed. Recommendations only: no rule card or skill approval is changed.**

The **Selected rule** column preserves the recovered 75-skill version. It is not a claim that all those effects remain playable after later decisions. The current 39-skill cards are comparison proposals; their reductions are not automatically adopted. Later explicit decisions, including removals, take precedence.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Marksman | Prepares precise long-range shots and creates targets for friendly fire. |
| Gunslinger | Combines sidearms, target selection and mobile shooting. |
| Breacher | Enters contested spaces and supports close-range clearing. |
| Watcher | Protects friendly movement and punishes enemy actions through reaction fire. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype / role | Selected rule | Check and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mark Target** | Marksman | **Move.** Spend a MOVE action to MARK an enemy within 24 inches and LOS until the start of this unit's next activation. Friendly shooting gains +2 to hit it and may target it within 24 inches even while HIDDEN, subject to weapon range. | **CLARIFY — The old Move cost, next-activation expiry and Hidden targeting exception conflict with the newer shared Marked procedure and the Surveillance drone’s use of it.**<br><br>Use the newer procedure: Core/DEX against one legally targetable visible enemy within 24 inches. Success Marks it through this End Phase: friendly shooting +2; remove/prevent Hidden; one Marked enemy per crew, replacing the previous mark. Do not bypass Hidden target eligibility at declaration unless a specific source permits it. Down removes the mark. |
| **Head Shot** | Marksman | **Passive.** Gain +4 to injure MARKED targets with RANGED ATTACKS if this unit has not moved this turn. | **REVISE — Marked already improves accuracy. A +4 Injury bonus applied to all three machine-gun dice would undermine single-shot weapon roles and high armour.**<br><br>Use +2 Damage against a Marked target with a genuinely one-die, non-Automatic ranged weapon; exclude Blast and Cone. Do not qualify an Automatic weapon by reducing its pool to one die. This follows the bounded compact proposal while retaining the Marksman payoff. |
| **Steady Breathing** | Marksman | **Passive.** Increase the range of this unit's 2 HANDED RANGED weapons and MARK TARGET by 12 inches. | **REVISE — The original range boost benefits machine guns and template launchers as much as rifles; extending Mark Target also expands team support rather than only the marksman’s reach.**<br><br>Keep +12 inches of weapon range, restricted to one-die two-handed firearms. Exclude Automatic, Blast and Cone, and do not extend Mark Target. This does not bypass LOS or Hidden restrictions. Treat the resulting 48-inch sniper range as a test value, not an assumed global range cap. |
| **Gunslinger** | Gunslinger | **Passive.** When dual wielding 1 HANDED RANGED weapons, the off-hand contributes its full attack dice to the combined SHOOT action. | **REDUNDANT — Every currently listed one-handed firearm prints one die. Full off-hand Attacks therefore equals the ordinary one-die off hand. The reduced +1 extra die is a different effect, not a faithful implementation.**<br><br>Preserve the agreed full-Attacks exception, but do not present it as a useful first-test purchase until an eligible multi-die sidearm exists. Do not add a weapon just to justify the skill. If an immediate replacement is wanted, my preference is one failed-hit reroll per own shooting attack while firing two Sidearms; that would require approval. |
| **Bullet Time** | Gunslinger; general multi-die shooting | **Attack modifier.** Once per turn when resolving a SHOOT action, divide the combined attack dice between the initial target and additional enemies within 6 inches of it. Each target requires LOS and the contributing weapon's range. | **CLARIFY — Automatic guns can use it without dual wielding; single-die pools cannot split. It overlaps the old Spray and Pray targeting effect.**<br><br>Keep splitting the existing pool among visible legal enemies within 6 inches of the initial target. Allocate all dice before rolling, retain each die’s weapon Damage/range/traits, and never generate extra dice or attacks. Blast and Cone remain ineligible. Differentiate Spray and Pray through suppression rather than another near-identical split rule. |
| **Run + Gun** | Gunslinger; general mobile shooting | **Passive.** Resolve a SHOOT action at any point during this unit's normal MOVE, then complete any remaining movement. Grants no additional movement or free attack. | **CLARIFY — The chosen version already spends normal movement and shooting rather than granting free actions. Old one-reaction-window wording does not fit activation-start/end triggers.**<br><br>Spend both unused Move and Core; make a normal Move, Shoot once at a declared point along that route, then finish the move. Standard activation-start/end reactions and the shot’s Return Fire apply. No extra shot, Sprint shooting, reaction immunity or new mid-movement reaction window. |
| **Breach** | Breacher | **Action.** Once per turn, select an enemy within 12 inches, even outside LOS. Move up to MOVE through doorways without spending actions or testing, then SHOOT. This unit cannot be targeted by reactions until the shot resolves. The shot requires normal LOS and weapon range. | **REVISE — Automatic movement, door bypass, shooting and reaction immunity bundle too many benefits. Unlocked doors already open freely during movement.**<br><br>Use the compact door-entry effect: once per round during normal Move, test STR to force one ordinary locked door without spending Core. Success continues movement; failure stops it. Reinforced blast doors are excluded. A following shot spends normal Core and permits normal reactions; no free attack or immunity. |
| **Spray and Pray** | Breacher | **Attack modifier.** Once per turn when SHOOTING an initial target within 12 inches, divide attack dice between it and other enemies within 8 inches of it and LOS. Each enemy DOWNED adds one die to allocate to a remaining eligible target. | **REVISE — Its splitting duplicates Bullet Time, while dice gained per Down can produce a casualty chain. New ordinary hits already Pin surviving targets.**<br><br>Give it a suppression role: before shooting a target within 12 inches, choose suppression fire. Keep normal hit dice, but replace Injury rolls with +1 additional Stress per hit; ordinary hit Stress/Pinning still apply. No casualty-generated dice. It may combine with legal pool splitting, but does not add another split procedure. |
| **On Me** | Breacher | **Triggered.** Nominate a friendly within 6 inches and LOS. Immediately after this unit finishes its activation, activate that friendly. Activation eligibility requires ruling. | **REVISE — Consecutive friendly activations are an explicit exception to whole-unit alternation and can create a large burst if repeated or chained.**<br><br>If retained, make it once per battle: spend Core, nominate an unactivated conscious/unpinned friendly within 6 inches and LOS, finish your activation, then activate it before the opponent. It receives only its normal remaining actions, cannot activate twice or trigger another On Me. This is a proposed bounded exception, not an already approved change to alternation. |
| **Watch Out!** | Watcher | **Reaction.** When a friendly within 12 inches and LOS is CHARGED, spend this unit's action or reaction to give that friendly a free RANGED ATTACK before the charge completes. | **REVISE — Ordinary ALERT shooting already covers the watcher’s own shot. The original’s distinct benefit is enabling the threatened ally to shoot, but whose token/reaction is spent was unfinished.**<br><br>Proposed single assisted reaction: when a friendly within 12 inches and your LOS is Charged, spend your ALERT to let it make the normal anti-Charge shot without needing its own ALERT. It must see the charger in its forward arc and have weapon range. Both units must be eligible to react and both count as Reacted. Resolve this as one assisted reaction, never a reaction triggering another; no second shot or unused Core is granted. |
| **Cover Me** | Watcher | **Reaction.** Once per turn, when an enemy in LOS SHOOTS a friendly, intervene with a free SHOOTING ATTACK resolved as an opposed DEX test. On success the enemy attack misses; on failure the friendly becomes PINNED and SHAKEN. Damage/Stress resolution requires ruling. | **REMOVED — Ross explicitly removed the covering-shot exception on 19 October. Its opposed shooting and Shaken result also conflict with simultaneous ordinary Return Fire and the unified Stress system.**<br><br>Keep removed. Do not restore it as part of recovering the 75-skill review list. Use ordinary reactions/Return Fire; this report records the original entry for completeness, not a new playable slot. |
| **Eyes Peeled** | Watcher; general ranged takedown support | **Triggered.** When this unit DOWNS an enemy with a RANGED ATTACK, gain a READY token. Keep it or give it to a friendly within 12 inches and LOS. | **CLARIFY — Kill-triggered ALERT remains distinct from ordinary Core/Order acquisition, but needs to respect the one-token and one-reaction limits.**<br><br>Once per round after your shooting attack Downs an enemy, gain ALERT or give it to one eligible friendly within 12 inches and LOS. Gain no second token, extra reaction or additional activation. Award it after the attack resolves; subsequent voluntary movement/another regular action can still remove it normally. |
| **Planning Ahead** | General weapon/loadout flexibility | **Passive.** Equip two 2 HANDED RANGED weapons, or one 1 HANDED and one 2 HANDED RANGED weapon. Choose the active weapon at the start of each activation; it cannot change during that activation. | **REVISE — The selected spare-weapon effect conflicts with the normal two weapon slots/no spare allowance. Treating carrying as simultaneous wielding would break the two-hand restriction.**<br><br>If retained, explicitly allow one spare two-handed firearm beyond normal weapon-slot capacity. At activation start choose the active weapon; a two-handed weapon still occupies both hands and cannot combine with an off hand. No extra attacks or mid-activation switch. This is a specific proposed carrying exception, not a third hand or three-slot weapon. |
| **Speed Loader** | General firearm handling | **Passive.** Once per turn, RELOAD one RANGED weapon while resolving a MOVE action. | **CLARIFY — Reload currently costs Core. The original Move-linked reload is a meaningful skill exception; the later two-weapons-per-Core rewrite is optional rather than automatically authoritative.**<br><br>Keep once per own activation: reload one carried weapon while resolving a normal Move, without spending Core. It grants no shot or extra movement and does not apply during Sprint or Charge. Normal ammunition/Single-use restrictions remain. |
| **Pin Them Down** | General suppression; Watcher synergy | **Passive.** RANGED hits that fail to wound also cause PINNED. Generate STRESS normally; pinning adds no extra Stress. | **REDUNDANT — Every ordinary successful hit already Pins a surviving target. The selected rule adds no effect.**<br><br>Remove from the first testing selection. Do not invent a second Pinning condition or fill the slot merely to reach fifteen. If another suppression skill is later wanted, differentiate it from the proposed Spray and Pray and weapon Suppression first. |

## Category priority

Marking must be shared; one-die sidearms make Gunslinger ineffective; split-fire and protective reactions need distinct benefits.
