# DEX — twelve-skill working list

Three archetypes with three skills each, plus three general skills. Specific skill wording may override normal modifier, action and reaction limits.

**Status:** STR effects are accepted. DEX, AGI and INT archetype cuts follow Ross’s latest direction; individual effects remain subject to their category review. NRV’s Berserker swaps and general shortlist below are recommendations.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Marksman | Prepares precise ranged attacks. |
| Gunslinger | Uses close-range dual weapons and mobile shooting. |
| Watcher | Supports allies through reaction fire and suppression. |

## Skills

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule | Recommendation / status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mark Target** | Marksman | Core/DEX: Mark a legal enemy within 24 inches until your next activation. Friendly shooting gains +2; the target loses Hidden. | Timing accepted: expire at the start of the marking unit’s next activation. |
| **Head Shot** | Marksman | Against a Marked target, gain an Injury/Damage bonus if you have not moved this round. | Test +2 and +4; also test +4 or +6 restricted to one-die, non-Automatic firearms. My preference is +6 restricted; not locked. |
| **Steady Breathing** | Marksman | Add 12 inches to your two-handed ranged weapon range. | Accepted. It neither Marks a target nor extends Mark Target. |
| **Gunslinger** | Gunslinger | When firing two one-handed ranged weapons, use the off-hand weapon’s full Attack Dice. | Keep. Current one-die sidearms give it no extra dice. |
| **Bullet Time** | Gunslinger | When shooting, divide your Attack Dice between the initial target and enemies within 6 inches of it. | Keep as the shared pool-splitting skill. |
| **Run + Gun** | Gunslinger | Shoot at any point during your normal Move, then finish moving. | Keep; uses the normal Move and Core. |
| **Watch Out!** | Watcher | When a friendly within 12 inches and LOS is Charged, spend your action or reaction to let it Shoot before the Charge completes. | Keep the assisted shot. Decide whose reaction is spent. |
| **Pin Them Down** | Watcher | Your ranged hits that fail to wound also cause Pinned. | Keep. The clarified hit rule makes this a distinct benefit. |
| **Eyes Peeled** | Watcher | After Downing an enemy with shooting, gain ALERT or give it to a friendly within 12 inches and LOS. | Keep. |
| **Planning Ahead** | General | Carry two two-handed ranged weapons, or one one-handed and one two-handed ranged weapon. Choose your active weapon at activation start. | Keep; this is the carrying-limit exception. |
| **Speed Loader** | General | Once per round, reload one ranged weapon while taking a Move action. | Keep; this is the Reload action-cost exception. |
| **On Me** | General | After your activation, immediately activate a friendly within 6 inches and LOS. | Keep; its purpose is the activation-order exception. |

## Parked from the previous selection

Breach, Spray and Pray, Cover Me.

Breach is removed. Replacing Spray and Pray with one shared Bullet Time split-fire skill is recommended. Pin Them Down moves into Watcher; On Me is a general choice. Head Shot’s value is still a test candidate.
