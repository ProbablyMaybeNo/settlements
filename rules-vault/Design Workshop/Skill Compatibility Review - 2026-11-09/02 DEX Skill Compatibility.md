## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Marksman | Prepares precise long-range shots and creates targets for friendly fire. |
| Gunslinger | Combines sidearms, target selection and mobile shooting. |
| Breacher | Enters contested spaces and supports close-range clearing. |
| Watcher | Protects friendly movement and punishes enemy actions through reaction fire. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule for review | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mark Target** | Marksman | Core/DEX: Mark a legal enemy within 24 inches until your next activation. Friendly shooting gains +2; the target loses Hidden. | Timing accepted: expire at the start of the marking unit’s next activation. |
| **Head Shot** | Marksman | Against a Marked target, gain an Injury/Damage bonus if you have not moved this round. | Test +2 and +4; also test +4 or +6 restricted to one-die, non-Automatic firearms. My preference is +6 restricted; not locked. |
| **Steady Breathing** | Marksman | Add 12 inches to your two-handed ranged weapon range. | Accepted. It neither Marks a target nor extends Mark Target. |
| **Gunslinger** | Gunslinger | When firing two one-handed ranged weapons, use the off-hand weapon’s full Attack Dice. | Keep. Current one-die sidearms give it no extra dice. |
| **Bullet Time** | Gunslinger; general multi-die shooting | When shooting, divide your Attack Dice between the initial target and enemies within 6 inches of it. | Keep. |
| **Run + Gun** | Gunslinger; general mobile shooting | Shoot at any point during your normal Move, then finish moving. | Keep; uses the normal Move and Core. |
| **Breach** | Breacher | Choose an enemy within 12 inches, even outside LOS. Move up to MOVE through doorways without testing, then Shoot. No reactions until the shot resolves. | Keep the entry/shot effect. Decide its combined action cost; I suggest Move + Core. |
| **Spray and Pray** | Breacher | Shoot a target within 12 inches; split dice among enemies within 8 inches of it. Each enemy Downed grants another die to allocate. | Keep the clearing/continuation effect. Test it with automatic weapons. |
| **On Me** | Breacher | After your activation, immediately activate a friendly within 6 inches and LOS. | Keep; its purpose is the activation-order exception. |
| **Watch Out!** | Watcher | When a friendly within 12 inches and LOS is Charged, spend your action or reaction to let it Shoot before the Charge completes. | Keep the assisted shot. Decide whose reaction is spent. |
| **Cover Me** | Watcher | Intervene in a shot at a friendly with an opposed DEX shooting test; winning cancels the enemy shot. | Already removed by Ross; do not restore. |
| **Eyes Peeled** | Watcher; general ranged takedown support | After Downing an enemy with shooting, gain ALERT or give it to a friendly within 12 inches and LOS. | Keep. |
| **Planning Ahead** | General weapon/loadout flexibility | Carry two two-handed ranged weapons, or one one-handed and one two-handed ranged weapon. Choose your active weapon at activation start. | Keep; this is the carrying-limit exception. |
| **Speed Loader** | General firearm handling | Once per round, reload one ranged weapon while taking a Move action. | Keep; this is the Reload action-cost exception. |
| **Pin Them Down** | General suppression; Watcher synergy | Your ranged hits that fail to wound also cause Pinned. | Keep. The clarified hit rule makes this a distinct benefit. |
