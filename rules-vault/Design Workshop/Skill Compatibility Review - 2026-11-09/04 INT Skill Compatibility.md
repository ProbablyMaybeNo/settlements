**15 selected skills — concise compatibility pass.**

Concise wording for the selected effects, with current-rule adaptations identified in the recommendation. Skills may override normal caps, actions and reactions. These four sets remain for review; original wording is retained in the linked selected catalogue.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Hacker | Manipulates electronic terrain and enemy devices. |
| Tactician | Shapes deployment, activation timing and access to battlefield opportunities. |
| Artificer | Fields specialised drones, turrets, traps and mines. |
| Medic | Keeps fighters useful through revival, Stress treatment and consumables. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule for review | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Neural Uplink** | Hacker | Gain +4 INT on Hacking tests. | Keep. |
| **Trojan** | Hacker | Test INT to hijack an enemy electronic deployable and immediately use it. Single-use devices may instead be triggered or defused. | Use the current portable-Hack procedure; I suggest control for that one operation. |
| **Interrupt** | Hacker | From a terminal, oppose an enemy terminal interaction within 12 inches with INT. Success cancels it and Freezes that terminal. | Use the current network/ALERT exception and shared Freeze duration. |
| **Tunnels** | Tactician | Before deployment, place two tunnels at least 8 inches from enemies/deployment, 12 apart and 6 from the edge. Hold back two units to arrive through them. | Unavailable for the initial test; leave the optional reserve procedure for later. |
| **Strategist** | Tactician | Once per battle, choose who activates first during an initiative phase. | Keep. |
| **Tagged** | Tactician | Test INT within 12 inches and LOS of an uncontested objective to control it remotely until an enemy captures it. One tag per crew. | Remote capture was removed; do not restore. |
| **MOLLE** | Artificer | Carry up to three different deployables. | Proposed Artificer replacement: Drone Pilot, unlocking aerial drones. |
| **Technician** | Artificer | Gain +4 INT when setting up a deployable. | Proposed Artificer replacement: Drone Operator, unlocking ground drones. |
| **Quick Drop** | Artificer | Once per round after deploying, place another carried deployable for free within LOS and 8 inches of the first. | Proposed Artificer replacement: Field Engineer, unlocking turrets, traps and mines. |
| **Stabilize** | Medic | Gain +4 INT on Stabilize tests. Successful treatment also clears Stress; you may Stabilize a conscious unit to clear its Stress. | Keep the Stress treatment. Automatic revival itself needs no test or bonus. |
| **Chemical Cocktail** | Medic | You may administer two chems to the same unit; normal action costs apply. | Explicitly allow two doses that round, overriding the ordinary one-dose limit. |
| **Doc** | Medic | Carry up to three different chems. Once per round, administer one free chem to a friendly within 6 inches. | Keep the packing and administration benefits; doses are still consumed. |
| **Inspector Gadget** | General equipment capacity | Carry up to three equipment items. | The vest duplicates this. Suggest +1 equipment slot instead, allowing it to stack with the vest. |
| **Stims** | General tactical movement; Tactician synergy | Core: give two friendlies within 6 inches an extra Move next activation. Each gains 1 Stress afterwards. | Keep the extra movement. Rename the skill Field Coordination to distinguish the Stims item. |
| **Smart** | General physical interaction | Once per round, take a free Interact action. | Keep. Use Interact’s normal scope rather than every INT-based task. |

## Review focus

The three deployable-category unlocks were agreed; replacing the three older Artificer slots is a proposed way to retain fifteen skills. No new device limits are added here.
