# INT — skill compatibility review

**15 selected skills reviewed. Recommendations only: no rule card or skill approval is changed.**

The **Selected rule** column preserves the recovered 75-skill version. It is not a claim that all those effects remain playable after later decisions. The current 39-skill cards are comparison proposals; their reductions are not automatically adopted. Later explicit decisions, including removals, take precedence.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Hacker | Manipulates electronic terrain and enemy devices. |
| Tactician | Shapes deployment, activation timing and access to battlefield opportunities. |
| Artificer | Fields specialised drones, turrets, traps and mines. |
| Medic | Keeps fighters useful through revival, Stress treatment and consumables. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype / role | Selected rule | Check and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Neural Uplink** | Hacker | **Passive.** Gain +4 INT when resolving HACKING tests. | **KEEP — The original conditional +4 still fits the skill-category cap and works on terminal and portable Hacking. Its reduced Terminal Training replacement was narrower. A +4 INT chem can also contribute, so high-end tests may reach the natural-1 floor.**<br><br>Keep +4 to Hacking tests, not an increase to printed INT or every technical action. Do not use it for Repair, Deploy, medicine, damage or a drone’s fixed +3 tests. Use the largest applicable skill bonus, natural-1 failure and normal Stress. |
| **Trojan** | Hacker | **Action.** Test INT to take control of an enemy DEPLOYABLE with ELECTRIC. Immediately resolve an action with it. SINGLE USE deployables may be triggered or defused; resolve their action if triggered, then remove them in either case. | **CLARIFY — Drones now have independent paired activations, operator/control rules and fixed +3 tests. Hijacking must not grant permanent ownership, a second full activation or an illegal turret shot.**<br><br>Core with hacking kit, visible enemy electronic device within 8 inches, INT test. Success resolves one legal Move or Core operation, using the device’s normal profile, then returns control; no objective action or operator transfer. Trigger/defuse a Single-use device normally and consume it. No invented turret firing permission, ALERT token or second drone activation. |
| **Interrupt** | Hacker | **Reaction.** While in base contact with an active terminal, when an enemy interacts with another terminal within 12 inches, make an opposed INT test. On success cancel the interaction and FREEZE that terminal until the end of the next turn. May be used after this unit has activated. | **CLARIFY — This is the specific network exception to ordinary LOS/activation reaction triggers. It still needs ALERT and a defined end point; generic reactions cannot counter it.**<br><br>While touching an active terminal, spend ALERT to oppose an enemy hacking on the same network within 12 inches of you, without needing LOS to the hacker. Opposed INT replaces that Hack’s test: your win cancels it and Freezes that enemy terminal through this End Phase; your loss means it succeeds. Ties favour the active hacker. Once per round; no reaction chain. |
| **Tunnels** | Tactician | **Setup.** Before deployment place two tunnel tokens, 8 inches from enemy deployment or units, 12 inches apart and 6 inches from the board edge. Secretly hold back two crew units. During battle deploy each at a different tunnel, then activate it normally. Enemies may react to arrivals. Arrival timing and distance minima require ruling. | **DEFER — Hidden reserves/arrival timing are unfinished and the tunnel option is unavailable for the initial test. Ordinary service hatches are terrain, not this skill’s reserve network.**<br><br>Keep outside the first battle-testing selection. Do not restore the historical builder or invent arrival rules during this compatibility pass. A later scenario/optional module can supply a complete reserve procedure before this skill is reconsidered. |
| **Strategist** | Tactician | **Setup.** Once per game, during a turn's initiative phase, decide who activates first. | **CLARIFY — Still distinct from the normal initiative roll. Define when its single use replaces the choice so no extra activation is inferred.**<br><br>Once per battle, after the round’s initiative rolls and before either crew activates, choose which player takes the first activation. No action cost, reroll, extra activation or change to subsequent alternation. |
| **Tagged** | Tactician | **Action.** Test INT within 12 inches and LOS of an UNCONTESTED objective. On success your crew controls it until an enemy captures it. Your crew may have only one TAGGED objective at a time. | **REMOVED — Remote objective capture was explicitly recorded as removed in the newer design direction. It bypasses physical objective interaction and is not the same as Marked.**<br><br>Keep the remote-control skill out. Use physical objective control and scenario Interact rules. Do not rename this Mark Target or restore remote scoring to fill the Tactician group; identify the resulting gap for later design. |
| **MOLLE** | Artificer | **Passive.** Equip up to three unique DEPLOYABLES for battle. | **REPLACE — Three deployables alone do not provide the newly required category skills, and capacity must respect item slots and the active-device limits.**<br><br>Use this roster position for the agreed Drone Pilot unlock: permits aerial drones, with normal paid equipment capacity, one controlled drone and paired operator/drone activation. It grants no extra item, free Deploy or pilot-Stat bonus to drone tests. The correspondence to this old slot is my proposed organisation. |
| **Technician** | Artificer | **Passive.** Gain +4 INT when testing to set up a DEPLOYABLE during battle. | **REPLACE — A setup bonus is functional, but the accepted deployable plan needs a separate ground-drone unlock. Pilots no longer improve a drone’s tests through their own Stats.**<br><br>Use this roster position for Drone Operator: permits ground drones, with normal equipment, deployment, control-range, shutdown/restart and repair rules. No extra +4 firing bonus or free device. This replaces the old bonus in the proposed 15-slot layout; it does not claim you previously chose this exact swap. |
| **Quick Drop** | Artificer | **Triggered.** Once per turn after successfully placing a DEPLOYABLE, place another for free within LOS and 8 inches of the first. Uses carried equipment; creates no free device. | **REPLACE — Remote free placement can violate contact placement and the operator’s one-active-deployable limit. The third accepted unlock category is currently missing from the old roster.**<br><br>Use this position for Field Engineer: permits turrets, traps and mines. Follow each profile’s placement, arming and active-device limits. It grants no free second deployment. Ordinary climbing gear/barricades remain usable without this skill. This is the proposed third Artificer slot. |
| **Stabilize** | Medic | **Action modifier.** Gain +4 INT for STABILIZE tests. Success also clears all target STRESS. May spend an ACTION to STABILIZE a non-DOWN unit and clear its STRESS. Once per turn. | **REVISE — Automatic medkit revival eliminates its INT bonus. The medical Stress-control role remains distinct and does not need to become another revival test.**<br><br>When you revive a teammate with a medkit, leave it at 0 Stress instead of 2; all revival limits and Pinned still apply. Separately, once per round spend Core in contact with a conscious friendly and pass INT to clear its Stress. This does not clear Pinned/morale or restore Wounds. No extra test is added to automatic revival. |
| **Chemical Cocktail** | Medic | **Passive.** May resolve two CHEM actions on the same unit. This permission does not make either action free. | **REVISE — The new shared chem limit is one dose per recipient per round. Two CHEM actions cannot be completed from one ordinary Core without a stated exception; +4 battle-long bonuses make free stacking substantial.**<br><br>Proposed explicit exception: once per own activation, spend Core to administer two different carried chem doses to yourself or one friendly in contact. Consume both, allow that recipient two doses that round, and apply ordinary non-stacking/addiction rules. No third dose, free stock or second activation. Flag combined +4 chems for testing. |
| **Doc** | Medic | **Passive.** Equip up to three unique CHEMS. Once per turn, resolve a free CHEM action on a friendly within 6 inches. | **REVISE — Three chem types competes with the zero-slot utility vest’s ordinary three-item capacity. Its free ranged administration also differs from the new contact/Core procedure.**<br><br>Give it packing plus one clear action exception: two paid chem doses may share one equipment slot; once per own activation administer one carried dose to a friendly within 6 inches and LOS without Core. It still counts against the recipient’s dose limit. No automatic medkit range/revival bonus and no free replacement doses. |
| **Inspector Gadget** | General equipment capacity | **Passive.** Equip up to three EQUIPMENT items for battle. | **REVISE — The free-slot utility vest already gives three equipment slots, making an alternative total-of-three cap redundant.**<br><br>Make the skill a specific +1 equipment-slot exception that stacks with one utility vest: base 2, skill 3, both 4. It grants no gear, armour or weapon slots and waives no category skill. This increased capacity is a proposal for sign-off, not permission to ignore existing limits before approval. |
| **Stims** | General tactical movement; Tactician synergy | **Action.** Once per turn, give two friendly units within 6 inches an additional MOVE during their next activation. Each gains 1 STRESS at the end of that activation. | **REPLACE — Stims is now also the name of the +4 MOVE consumable. Two extra full Move actions introduce timing/action tracking and multiply Quick and the movement chem.**<br><br>Rename the skill Field Coordination. Proposed effect: Core/INT, choose up to two conscious/unpinned friendlies within 6 inches and LOS. Each gains +2 inches on its next unused normal Move this round, then gains 1 Stress. No additional action, Charge/Sprint extension or action refresh. Keep the consumable’s separate name/profile. |
| **Smart** | General physical interaction | **Passive.** Resolve one free INTERACT action per turn. Qualifying action categories require ruling. | **REVISE — A broad free Interact can include objectives or electronics and generate extra terrain XP. This should not be solved by undoing the agreed XP-per-successful-interaction rule.**<br><br>Use the compact narrow reroll: once per round reroll one failed direct physical Interact test, including mission work. Exclude Hack, Revive, Secure, Deploy, attacks and skill actions. Keep normal Core costs and normal XP awards; no blanket free Interact action. |

## Category priority

Insert the three agreed device-category unlocks, update medicine/chems/capacity, and keep removed remote capture out.

The proposed MOLLE/Technician/Quick Drop → Drone Pilot/Drone Operator/Field Engineer correspondence uses the three Artificer slots. The **category unlocks themselves were agreed**; this exact replacement mapping is a recommendation. Tactician has gaps because Tunnels is held out and Tagged stays removed. This review does not invent filler or claim fifteen playable INT choices.
