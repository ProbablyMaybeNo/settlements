# INT — twelve-skill working list

Three archetypes with three skills each, plus three general skills. Specific skill wording may override normal modifier, action and reaction limits.

**Status:** STR effects are accepted. DEX, AGI and INT archetype cuts follow Ross’s latest direction; individual effects remain subject to their category review. NRV’s Berserker swaps and general shortlist below are recommendations.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Hacker | Operates terminals and interactive terrain from range. |
| Artificer | Uses drones and deployables. |
| Medic | Provides support, treatment and revivals. |

## Skills

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule | Recommendation / status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Neural Uplink** | Hacker | Gain +4 INT on Hacking tests. | Keep. |
| **Trojan** | Hacker | Test INT to hijack an enemy electronic deployable and immediately use it. Single-use devices may instead be triggered or defused. | Use the current portable-Hack procedure; I suggest control for that one operation. |
| **Interrupt** | Hacker | From a terminal, oppose an enemy terminal interaction within 12 inches with INT. Success cancels it and Freezes that terminal. | Use the current network/ALERT exception and shared Freeze duration. |
| **Drone Pilot** | Artificer | Unlock aerial drones. | Agreed deployable category skill; proposed Artificer roster slot. |
| **Drone Operator** | Artificer | Unlock ground drones. | Agreed deployable category skill; proposed Artificer roster slot. |
| **Field Engineer** | Artificer | Unlock turrets, traps and mines. | Agreed deployable category skill; proposed Artificer roster slot. |
| **Stabilize** | Medic | Successful revival also clears Stress. Once per round, Core/INT +4 in contact with a conscious friendly clears its Stress. | The INT test is for the conscious-unit treatment; medkit revival remains automatic. |
| **Chemical Cocktail** | Medic | You may administer two chems to the same unit; normal action costs apply. | Explicitly allow two doses that round, overriding the ordinary one-dose limit. |
| **Doc** | Medic | Carry up to three different chems. Once per round, administer one free chem to a friendly within 6 inches. | Keep the packing and administration benefits; doses are still consumed. |
| **Inspector Gadget** | General | Carry up to three equipment items. | The vest duplicates this. Suggest +1 equipment slot instead, allowing it to stack with the vest. |
| **Stims** | General | Core: give two friendlies within 6 inches an extra Move next activation. Each gains 1 Stress afterwards. | Keep the extra movement. Rename the skill Field Coordination to distinguish the Stims item. |
| **Smart** | General | Once per round, take a free Interact action. | Keep. Use Interact’s normal scope rather than every INT-based task. |

## Parked from the previous selection

Tunnels, Strategist, Tagged, MOLLE, Technician, Quick Drop.

Tactician is parked. The Artificer trio uses the three agreed deployable-category unlocks in place of MOLLE, Technician and Quick Drop. The Stims skill may be renamed to distinguish it from the consumable.
