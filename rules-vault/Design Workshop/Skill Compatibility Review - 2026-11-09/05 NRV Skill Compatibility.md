**15 selected skills — concise compatibility pass.**

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Commissar | Restores allies’ morale and helps them use their available actions effectively. |
| Berserker | Channels personal or transferred Stress into aggressive resistance. |
| Anchor | Sustains nearby allies through persistent protective and pressure effects. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule for review | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inspire** | Commissar | Core/NRV: remove 2 Stress from up to three friendlies within 8 inches. | Keep. |
| **Rally** | Commissar | Core/NRV: Rally up to three Broken friendlies within 8 inches. | Keep the group Rally. Consider including the other morale conditions. |
| **Motivate** | Commissar | Core/NRV: choose two friendlies within 8 inches. Next activation they may use Move as Core and repeat an action. | Keep the action conversion/repetition; do not require them to repeat. |
| **Sin Eater** | Berserker; general Stress support | Core: transfer up to 3 Stress total from friendlies within 6 inches to yourself, up to your capacity. | Keep. |
| **Implode** | Berserker | Increase maximum Stress by 2. Before a melee attack, remove up to half your Stress, rounded down, gaining that much hit and Injury bonus. Once per round. | Keep. Apply the existing highest-Stress morale result at 5+; no extra table is needed. |
| **Last Laugh** | Berserker | Once per round, when an enemy attack would Down you, make one final legal Shoot or melee attack against that enemy first. | Keep the final attack; do not replace it with ordinary Return Fire. |
| **Hold Fast** | Anchor | Core/NRV: activate a 6-inch aura giving friendlies −2 to incoming Injury rolls. | Keep the flat protection; no cover or Help Recover requirement. |
| **Menacing** | Anchor | Core/NRV: activate a 6-inch aura. Friendly attacks from it inflict +1 Stress if they hit. | Keep. |
| **Lean on Me** | Anchor | Core/NRV: activate a 6-inch aura transferring Stress inflicted on other friendlies to you, up to your capacity. | Keep. |
| **Roar** | General charge defence; Anchor synergy | Core/NRV: until your next activation, enemies must pass NRV to Charge you or friendlies within 3 inches. | Keep. |
| **On Your Feet** | General recovery support; Commissar synergy | Core/NRV: remove Pinned from up to three friendlies within 8 inches. | Keep; this is specific friendly recovery. |
| **Commanding Presence** | General leadership; Commissar synergy | Once per round, a friendly within 8 inches and LOS may use your NRV for a Break test. | Keep. |
| **Beta Blocker** | General composure; Berserker/Anchor synergy | Halve Stress penalties, rounding up. Break tests still use full Stress. | Keep the printed exception; do not add another exclusion for Rally. |
| **Unflinching** | General reaction resilience; Watcher cross-stat synergy | Once per round, when a hit would remove your ALERT, you may retain it. | Keep. Non-wounding hits no longer automatically Pin, so the original works. |
| **Big Aura** | General aura specialisation; Anchor synergy | Increase your aura ranges by 3 inches and maintain two auras at once. | Keep; it is an aura-specialist choice. |
