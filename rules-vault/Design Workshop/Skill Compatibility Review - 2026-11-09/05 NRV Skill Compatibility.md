# NRV — twelve-skill working list

Three archetypes with three skills each, plus three general skills. Specific skill wording may override normal modifier, action and reaction limits.

**Status:** STR effects are accepted. DEX, AGI and INT archetype cuts follow Ross’s latest direction; individual effects remain subject to their category review. NRV’s Berserker swaps and general shortlist below are recommendations.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Commissar | Restores allies’ morale and coordinates their actions. |
| Berserker | Turns personal Stress into combat power. |
| Anchor | Supports nearby allies with persistent auras. |

## Skills

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule | Recommendation / status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inspire** | Commissar | Core/NRV: remove 2 Stress from up to three friendlies within 8 inches. | Keep. |
| **Rally** | Commissar | Core/NRV: Rally up to three Broken friendlies within 8 inches. | Keep the group Rally. Consider including the other morale conditions. |
| **Motivate** | Commissar | Core/NRV: choose two friendlies within 8 inches. Next activation they may use Move as Core and repeat an action. | Keep the action conversion/repetition; do not require them to repeat. |
| **Rage** | Berserker | Add up to +3 Damage to a melee attack. Afterwards gain 1 Stress per point added. | Proposed move from STR to NRV, replacing Sin Eater. |
| **I'm About to Break** | Berserker | Once per round when Charged, turn Stress penalties into bonuses for that combat. Afterwards gain 1 Stress and take a Break test. | Proposed move from STR to NRV, replacing Last Laugh. |
| **Implode** | Berserker | Increase maximum Stress by 2. Before a melee attack, remove up to half your Stress, rounded down, gaining that much hit and Injury bonus. Once per round. | Keep. Apply the existing highest-Stress morale result at 5+; no extra table is needed. |
| **Hold Fast** | Anchor | Core/NRV: activate a 6-inch aura giving friendlies −2 to incoming Injury rolls. | Keep the flat protection; no cover or Help Recover requirement. |
| **Menacing** | Anchor | Core/NRV: activate a 6-inch aura. Friendly attacks from it inflict +1 Stress if they hit. | Keep. |
| **Lean on Me** | Anchor | Core/NRV: activate a 6-inch aura transferring Stress inflicted on other friendlies to you, up to your capacity. | Keep. |
| **Commanding Presence** | General | Once per round, a friendly within 8 inches and LOS may use your NRV for a Break test. | Keep. |
| **Beta Blocker** | General | Halve Stress penalties, rounding up. Break tests still use full Stress. | Keep the printed exception; do not add another exclusion for Rally. |
| **Big Aura** | General | Increase your aura ranges by 3 inches and maintain two auras at once. | Keep; it is an aura-specialist choice. |

## Parked from the previous selection

Sin Eater, Last Laugh, Roar, On Your Feet, Unflinching.

Recommended Berserker trio: Rage generates Stress, I’m About to Break exploits it when Charged, Implode spends it for offence. Park Sin Eater and Last Laugh. Keep Commanding Presence, Beta Blocker and Big Aura as the three general choices; Roar, On Your Feet and Unflinching remain alternatives. These NRV choices are not yet locked.
