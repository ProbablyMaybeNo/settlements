# NRV — skill compatibility review

**15 selected skills reviewed. Recommendations only: no rule card or skill approval is changed.**

The **Selected rule** column preserves the recovered 75-skill version. It is not a claim that all those effects remain playable after later decisions. The current 39-skill cards are comparison proposals; their reductions are not automatically adopted. Later explicit decisions, including removals, take precedence.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Commissar | Restores allies’ morale and helps them use their available actions effectively. |
| Berserker | Channels personal or transferred Stress into aggressive resistance. |
| Anchor | Sustains nearby allies through persistent protective and pressure effects. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype / role | Selected rule | Check and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inspire** | Commissar | **Action.** Once per turn, spend an ACTION and test NRV. On success remove 2 STRESS from up to three friendlies within 8 inches. | **CLARIFY — Removing Stress remains useful even without an Injury penalty because it improves combat and non-combat tests and later morale. The source’s three targets differ from the reduced two-unit proposal.**<br><br>Core/NRV minus your Stress versus 13+. Success removes up to 2 Stress from each of up to three friendlies within 8 inches and LOS, including yourself if eligible. It does not clear Pinned or count as a successful Rally; retain existing morale conditions until their removal rule applies. |
| **Rally** | Commissar | **Action.** Once per turn, spend an ACTION and test NRV. On success RALLY up to three friendlies with a break condition within 8 inches. This RALLY test ignores Stress penalties; use the shared Rally outcome. | **CLARIFY — There is already a Core Rally for one friendly within 3 inches. The skill’s added value is group size/range; it does not need a silently Stress-free test.**<br><br>Core; make one normal NRV−Stress Rally test for up to three friendlies within 8 inches and LOS. Success clears their morale and Stress, leaving them Pinned. Include Broken, Bolting and Bugging Out rather than only Broken. A Broken source can use it only on itself; a fleeing source gets no new self-action permission. |
| **Motivate** | Commissar | **Action.** Once per turn, spend an ACTION and test NRV. Choose two friendlies within 8 inches. During their next activation each may use its MOVE as an ACTION and repeat an action. | **REVISE — The selected effect converts Move rather than granting an extra action; old simulations sometimes required repetition incorrectly. It conflicts with the default non-conversion rule only if not written as a specific exception.**<br><br>Core/NRV; choose up to two unactivated conscious/unpinned friendlies within 8 inches and LOS. In their next activation this round, each may exchange its unused Move for Core instead. No extra activation, action refresh, forced identical action or second own attack; the ordinary attack limit remains. Benefits do not stack or chain through another Motivate. |
| **Sin Eater** | Berserker; general Stress support | **Action.** Once per turn, spend an ACTION to transfer up to 3 STRESS in total from friendlies within 6 inches to this unit, limited by its remaining Stress capacity. | **CLARIFY — Transfer remains meaningful but cannot erase overflow or secretly Rally a unit. It may feed Implode, subject to the normal Stress maximum.**<br><br>Core, once per round: transfer up to 3 Stress total from other friendlies within 6 inches and LOS to yourself, limited by your remaining capacity. Excess stays with the donor. Transfer does not remove Pinned/morale, and transferred points cannot bounce through another transfer skill. |
| **Implode** | Berserker | **End Phase.** Increase maximum STRESS by 2. During the End Phase, instead of resolving this unit's BREAK test, it may IMPLODE. Remove all STRESS. During this unit's next activation, gain +1 to melee hit and Injury rolls for each point removed, up to +4. | **REVISE — Its Stress-7 ceiling extends beyond the current table’s literal row of 5. Removing Stress already improves hit chances; a skill bonus is additional. Historical automatic Break avoidance is not part of the new contract.**<br><br>Keep maximum Stress 7, treating 5+ as Bugging Out. Once per own melee attack, before rolling, remove up to half current Stress rounded down and gain that many skill hit/Damage bonuses for the exchange. Remaining Stress still penalises hits, ordinary End Phase Break still occurs, and only the strongest skill bonus applies alongside Rage or Ambush. It never replaces a Break test. |
| **Last Laugh** | Berserker | **Triggered.** Once per turn, when an enemy attack would DOWN this unit, first make one final SHOOT or MELEE ATTACK against that enemy if it is a legal target. A second last attack would double-reward those exchanges and trigger further chains.**<br><br>Once per battle when an enemy attack would Down you, if you have not already attacked in that exchange, make one legal shot or melee attack against that attacker before becoming Down. | **Hold Fast** | Anchor | **Aura.** Spend an ACTION and test NRV to activate a 6-inch AURA. Armour 6 and this skill’s protection are separate sources.**<br><br>Core/NRV to activate the 6-inch aura. Injury rolls against eligible friendlies in range and LOS suffer −2, without requiring cover. Include the source. Use the shared aura duration/limit; apply only the largest skill Injury penalty, so this does not add to Tough as Nails −4. It does not protect from explicitly armour/skill-ignoring hazards. |
| **Menacing** | Anchor | **Aura.** Spend an ACTION and test NRV to activate a 6-inch AURA. A friendly attacking from within range/LOS adds 1 extra Stress if that attack scores any hit, once per attack. Include the source. It does not add one per die, improve Injury or create automatic Panic; obey the recipient’s Stress cap. |
| **Lean on Me** | Anchor | **Aura.** Spend an ACTION and test NRV to activate a 6-inch AURA. Two transfer auras can otherwise produce loops or destroy Stress.**<br><br>Core/NRV activates a 6-inch aura affecting other friendlies in range/LOS. Transfer only Stress the source can accept; excess stays where inflicted. Each Stress point transfers at most once. No transfer of wounds, hits or Pinning and no instant Rally. Keep normal Break timing for the source. |
| **Roar** | General charge defence; Anchor synergy | **Action.** Once per turn, spend an ACTION and test NRV. Define failure before spending actions rather than turning a failed permission check into an unexplained lost activation.**<br><br>Core/NRV; until your next activation, enemies must pass a normal NRV test to declare a Charge against you or a friendly within 3 inches and your LOS. Check before paying Charge actions. Failure bars that protected target for this activation but permits other legal choices. One test per charger against this effect; no stacking repeat tests from multiple Roars. |
| **On Your Feet** | General recovery support; Commissar synergy | **Action.** Once per turn, spend an ACTION and test NRV. It removes Pinned, not Stress, and therefore differs from Inspire/Rally.**<br><br>Once per round, Core/NRV; success removes Pinned from up to three friendlies within 8 inches and LOS. The source must be conscious/unpinned and able to use Core. No Wounds, morale/Stress removal, action restoration or second activation. Down/Unconscious are ineligible. |
| **Commanding Presence** | General leadership; Commissar synergy | **Triggered.** Once per turn, a friendly within 8 inches and LOS may use this unit's NRV for a BREAK test. | **CLARIFY — Substituting NRV must not also substitute the leader’s Stress or grant an extra morale test. The selected range is 8 inches; the reduced 6-inch version was a proposal.**<br><br>Once per round, one friendly within 8 inches and LOS may use this conscious, non-fleeing unit’s NRV for a Break test. Apply the testing unit’s own Stress and other modifiers. No additional test or automatic Rally. Extending this to Rally would be a separate optional change, not assumed here. |
| **Beta Blocker** | General composure; Berserker/Anchor synergy | **Passive.** Halve STRESS penalties, rounding up. This does not reduce Stress points or change Break thresholds/severity. BREAK and RALLY ignore Stress penalties for all units. | **CLARIFY — Stress now penalises non-combat tests too, while Injury never uses it. Break and Rally both subtract full Stress, so both need the exception stated.**<br><br>Halve ordinary Stress penalties, rounding the penalty up: Stress 3 gives −2. Keep the actual Stress counter unchanged. Break and Rally use full Stress and normal condition bands. No Injury benefit, movement bonus, Stress removal or extra capacity. |
| **Unflinching** | General reaction resilience; Watcher cross-stat synergy | **Triggered.** Once per turn, when a hit would remove this unit's READY token, it may retain it. | **Big Aura** | General aura specialisation; Anchor synergy | **Passive.** Increase this unit's AURA ranges by 3 inches. Two active auras are not two free activations; the 6-inch base becomes 9 inches.**<br><br>Keep +3 inches to your aura ranges and permission to maintain two different auras. Accept it as a specialist follow-up rather than adding a new built-in aura just to make it universally independent. |

## Category priority

Use current Stress/Break timing; make recovery, auras and reactions useful without duplicated attacks, overflow loss or transfer loops.

## Proposed shared aura wording

Core and a normal NRV test activates an aura. Measure from the source’s base; recipients need range and LOS. Include the source for Hold Fast/Menacing, exclude it as a donor for Lean on Me. Maintain one aura, or two different auras with Big Aura. Each costs its own activation/test and persists until replaced or the source becomes Down, Unconscious, leaves battle or gains a morale condition. Pinning alone does not cancel an already active aura. No upkeep action, duplicate stacking or repeated Stress transfer. These common details are recommendations for approval, not recovered agreed text.
