## Latest confirmed rules

- Each hit adds 1 Stress. Only losing a Wound while retaining Wounds causes Pinned; zero Wounds causes Down. Specific effects can still Pin.
- Maximum Wounds: 3, or 4 for a crew leader. Tough grants +1 within that limit.
- Marked lasts until the marking unit’s next activation, including surveillance drones.
- Steady Breathing adds 12 inches to two-handed ranged weapon range only.
- Head Shot’s +2/+4 and restricted single-shot +4/+6 remain test options.
- Breach is removed. Bullet Time and Spray and Pray overlap; keeping one split-fire skill is recommended.

## Review status

The archetype direction for STR, DEX, AGI and INT follows the discussion. NRV’s twelve-skill shortlist is proposed. Recoverable parked skills are listed in each report. The selected roster is not a claim that all sixty effects, costs or prices are final.
