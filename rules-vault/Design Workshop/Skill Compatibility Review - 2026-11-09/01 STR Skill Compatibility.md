# STR — skill compatibility review

**15 selected skills reviewed. Recommendations only: no rule card or skill approval is changed.**

The **Selected rule** column preserves the recovered 75-skill version. It is not a claim that all those effects remain playable after later decisions. The current 39-skill cards are comparison proposals; their reductions are not automatically adopted. Later explicit decisions, including removals, take precedence.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Playstyle |
| --- | --- |
| Grappler | Controls one enemy and changes positions through holds, hauling and displacement. |
| Tank | Holds exposed positions and absorbs pressure while protecting nearby allies. |
| Charger | Uses reach and momentum to engage or displace enemies. |
| Berserker | Trades personal Stress and risk for close-combat pressure. |

**General** skills support multiple builds. Secondary synergies are shown in the table; these labels impose no prerequisites or mandatory bundles. NRV retains three archetypes and six general/specialist choices.

## Skill-by-skill report

| Skill | Archetype /role | Selected rule | Check and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Grapple** | Grappler | **Action.** Make an opposed STR test against an enemy within 1 inch. On success, it becomes GRAPPLED; see [[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Second Draft/Draft 2 - Keywords]] for holding, escape and Meat Shield. Historical GRAPPLED: held unit cannot act/react normally and gets an opposed STR escape at activation start; holder moves half speed. Meat Shield redirects shooting on an unmodified d20/10+; order and Crush were unresolved. | **REVISE — The original GRAPPLED keyword includes a start-of-activation escape and unresolved Meat Shield shooting. The newer saved hold uses Core to escape and bars both units from attacking; Strong Arm must state its exceptions.**<br><br>Use Core/opposed STR to hold an engaged enemy. The captive cannot move independently; escape costs Core/opposed STR, then separate 1 inch. Both normally lose attacks/reactions/equipment use; the holder may move the captive at half MOVE and release it during its activation. End on holder Pinned, incapacity or morale. Leave the unresolved Luck/Meat Shield and automatic Crush out of the first test. |
| **Strong Arm** | Grappler; general carrying/Heavy-weapon use | **Passive.** Ignore HEAVY. While holding a GRAPPLED target, move at full MOVE, INTERACT and attack with 1 HANDED weapons. Cannot CHARGE, DASH, CLIMB or use 2 HANDED equipment while GRAPPLING. | **CLARIFY — The newly approved harness already removes carrying penalties and Unwieldy. Strong Arm still has a distinct slot-free benefit and its original one-handed grapple permissions; it is not simply the reduced casualty-carry skill.**<br><br>Retain full-speed carrying/holding without equipment, and one-handed attacks/Interact while holding as explicit exceptions to Grapple. Normal Core costs and one own attack still apply. Keep no Sprint, Charge, Climb or two-handed use while holding. Ignore only a printed Heavy movement penalty, not every Heavy restriction or Unwieldy. Give this skill its own pickup permission so it does not require Grapple for casualty/object carrying. |
| **Throw** | Grappler; independent terrain control | **Action.** Throw adjacent scatter or obstacle terrain no larger than 3 inches a distance in inches equal to STR. May instead throw a GRAPPLED target. Terrain hitting a unit causes PINNED. A thrown unit hitting a structure suffers an injury roll at +3 Damage; collision with another unit PINs both. | **REVISE — Throwing people or 3-inch terrain creates forced-movement, falling and damage interactions well beyond the current small-object rule.**<br><br>Use the bounded testing version: Core/STR, loose object in contact no larger than a 1 × 2-inch footprint, visible target within 3 inches. Success Pins and pushes it 1 inch directly away; failure places the object halfway. Resolve ordinary hazards. Exclude people, anchored scenery and mission objects for this test. |
| **Brace** | Tank | **Action.** BRACE until the end of the turn: incoming attacks suffer -4 to hit. This unit cannot move, act or react while BRACING. | **CLARIFY — A one-opponent-activation meaning of turn would make this unreliable. The original defensive stance is distinct from the later, unapproved heavy-cover/Stress-reduction rewrite.**<br><br>Keep the original −4 to incoming hit rolls. Spend Core; the stance lasts until the start of your next activation, and you cannot move, act or react while maintaining it. End it on incapacity or forced movement. It stacks with cover as a separate skill penalty, but not another skill hit penalty. Flag the combined cover/stance protection for testing rather than silently substituting the reduced version. |
| **Come On Then** | Tank | **Action.** Test STR. On success, enemies within 6 inches must target this unit with their ATTACK actions. Duration and legal-target exceptions require ruling. | **REVISE — Compulsory targeting is unfinished and can require illegal shots or dictate an opponent’s entire activation. The reduced catalogue removed it as a proposal, not proof that you rejected its Tank role.**<br><br>Keep the protective role but use soft pressure: Core/STR; until your next activation, enemies within 6 inches and your LOS suffer −2 to shooting at other units. They remain free to move, attack you or choose another action. This is a proposed replacement effect, not an approved forced-target rule. |
| **Tough as Nails** | Tank | **Triggered.** Once per round, when attacked in melee without being wounded, ignore the hit's effects. Subsequent attacks against this unit suffer -4 to injure until the end of the turn. | **CLARIFY — Hits now give Stress before Injury. Ignoring a non-wounding melee attack therefore needs to identify exactly which Stress/Pinning is removed; the subsequent protection can combine strongly with Armour 6.**<br><br>Once per round, after a melee exchange leaves you conscious and unwounded, ignore only that incoming attack’s Stress and Pinning. Subsequent Injury rolls against you suffer −4 until this End Phase. Do not undo older Stress/conditions or an Injury. Use only the strongest skill Injury penalty, so Hold Fast does not add another −2. |
| **Rampage** | Charger | **Triggered.** After DOWNING an enemy with a CHARGE ATTACK, move the remaining charge distance. If this brings the unit within 1 inch of another enemy, make a free MELEE ATTACK against it. | **REVISE — A follow-up attack exceeds the default own-activation attack allowance. Unlimited retriggering and continuing after being Down in a simultaneous exchange would be excessive.**<br><br>Allow one explicit follow-up per own activation: survive a Charge exchange and Down its target, then use only remaining rolled Charge distance to reach a second enemy and fight once. No fresh movement roll, extra Charge die, further Rampage or Blade Fury chain. It cannot be used during a reaction; normal enemy reactions still apply. If you are Pinned/Down or have no distance left, stop. |
| **Bull** | Charger | **Attack modifier.** When declaring a CHARGE, test STR. On success, pass through non-LOS-blocking scatter, obstacles and barricades. Enemies contacted test AGI; on failure, push them 2 inches in your chosen direction and PIN them. Cannot pass reinforced doors, buildings or LOS-blocking structures. | **REVISE — The original route-clearing version can Pin several models before also resolving a normal attack. Current Shove supplies a simpler complete displacement procedure.**<br><br>Use Charge ending in Shove at +2 opposed STR instead of its melee exchange. Push 2 inches directly away; Pin only on collision, with both units Pinned on a model collision. Retain normal terrain traversal and hazards. No additional Charge attack or ploughing through a line of enemies. |
| **Human Bullet** | Charger | **Passive.** Determine CHARGE distance using MOVE plus the total of 2d6 instead of 1d6. | **CLARIFY — MOVE +2d6 remains distinct from ordinary MOVE +1d6. Keeping the ordinary MOVE +6 declaration limit would prevent using its full benefit.**<br><br>Keep MOVE +2d6 and permit declaring a target within MOVE +12 along a legal route. Spend Move + Core and resolve normal failure and reaction rules. It does not grant an additional attack die beyond the ordinary Charge bonus or increase the fixed-distance movement of unrelated actions. |
| **Rage** | Berserker | **Attack modifier.** When resolving a MELEE ATTACK, add up to +3 Damage to its Injury rolls. After the attack is resolved, gain 1 STRESS for each point added. | **CLARIFY — The preserved catalogue grants Damage, whereas the later compact proposal offers +2 for one Stress. Current Stress affects future hit/non-combat tests, not Injury. Rage and Implode Damage bonuses are in the same category.**<br><br>Keep the selected variable +1 to +3 Damage option for the first comparison, declared before your own melee exchange; gain that much Stress afterwards even on a miss, and require capacity when declared. No defensive/reaction use. Use the largest skill Damage bonus rather than adding Rage and Implode. The historical hit-bonus interpretation is not silently added. |
| **I'm About to Break** | Berserker | **Triggered.** Once per turn when CHARGED, use STRESS as positive modifiers instead of penalties for that combat. Afterwards gain 1 STRESS and immediately make a BREAK test, ignoring beneficial Stress modifiers from friendly units, skills or equipment. | **REVISE — Changing a −5 Stress penalty into +5 swings hit totals by ten and exceeds the ordinary skill-bonus cap. It also must not independently grant a reaction.**<br><br>When defending against a Charge, ignore your Stress penalty for that exchange instead of turning it into a positive bonus. Afterwards gain 1 Stress and immediately take a normal Stress-subtracted Break test. Once per round; no free ALERT or additional attack. This retains aggressive resistance with a smaller numerical swing. |
| **Blowing Off Steam** | Berserker; general takedown recovery | **Triggered.** Whenever this unit DOWNS an enemy, remove 2 of its STRESS. | **CLARIFY — Still useful under per-hit Stress. Mandatory removal can undermine an intended Implode build, and simultaneous mutual Down should not create a recovery or revival.**<br><br>After causing an enemy to become Down, you may remove 2 Stress if still conscious. This removes Stress only, never Pinned, morale conditions or Wounds. Keep the optional choice so players can retain Stress for Implode; do not award extra removals for attacking an existing casualty. |
| **Goliath** | General weapon/loadout flexibility | **Passive.** Wield two 2 HANDED melee weapons as if they were 1 HANDED. The off-hand still contributes only one attack die unless another rule explicitly overrides it. | **CLARIFY — This is a deliberate exception to the two-hand weapon rules. Most melee weapons now print one die, so full off-hand dice would not inherently add anything.**<br><br>Keep: treat two-handed melee weapons as one-handed for this unit, permitting two in its two weapon slots. Use normal primary dice plus one off-hand die; retain each weapon’s traits and Damage. It does not apply to firearms, Blast/Cone, carrying hands or equipment, and does not make a weapon Agile. |
| **Tough** | General resilience; Tank synergy | **Passive.** Gain +1 WOUND, subject to the unit's Wound ceiling. | **REPLACE — The original extra Wound conflicts with the newer one-Wound-human rule. The compact self-treatment replacement also fails because self-revival is prohibited and medkits now work automatically.**<br><br>Proposed replacement: once per battle, force one successful Injury roll against this unit to be rerolled; the new result stands. This is one die, not the entire attack or automatic cancellation of a Wound. Retain one Wound and normal casualty rules. |
| **Big Fella** | General heavy-weapon handling | **Passive.** Ignore UNWIELDY on this unit's weapons. | **KEEP — The harness now grants the same Unwieldy immunity, but costs an equipment slot; a skill offering it without gear remains a real alternative. The reduced −1 penalty was a later proposal.**<br><br>Keep full immunity to Unwieldy, with ordinary hands, capacity and all other Heavy restrictions. No stacking benefit with the harness. My earlier armour-penalty replacement is not needed to make the original skill functional; an armour specialist can be considered separately if wanted. |

## Category priority

Resolve the original Grapple package, retain slot-free handling choices, and replace +1 Wound rather than reviving obsolete self-treatment.
