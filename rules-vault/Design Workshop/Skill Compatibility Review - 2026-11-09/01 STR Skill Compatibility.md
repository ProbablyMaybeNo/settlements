# STR — twelve-skill working list

Three archetypes with three skills each, plus three general skills. Specific skill wording may override normal modifier, action and reaction limits.

**Status:** STR effects are accepted. DEX, AGI and INT archetype cuts follow Ross’s latest direction; individual effects remain subject to their category review. NRV’s Berserker swaps and general shortlist below are recommendations.

## Archetypes

| Archetype | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Grappler | Restrains enemies and controls their position. |
| Tank | Withstands pressure and protects nearby allies. |
| Charger | Uses momentum to reach and displace enemies. |

## Skills

| Skill | Archetype / role | Concise rule | Recommendation / status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Grapple** | Grappler | Core/opposed STR against an enemy within 1 inch. Success holds it: no actions; free opposed escape at activation start. The holder moves half speed and uses one-handed weapons. Shots at the holder hit the captive; shots through either model at a friendly redirect to the captive on d6 1–3, once per attack. Free release to 3 inches; collisions Pin and ledges cause falls. Holder Pinned/incapacitated/morale ends the hold. | Drafted. Use the linked full Grapple rule for the collision outcomes. |
| **Hauler** | Grappler | Ignore movement penalties while Grappling or Carrying. While Grappling, you may use non-Heavy two-handed weapons. | Accepted. Strong Arm is renamed Hauler. |
| **Throw** | Grappler | Core/STR while Carrying, or use a Grappled enemy. Throw 6 inches straight away. Unit collisions Pin both; Heavy-terrain collisions cause Damage 4 Injury. Thrown structures push contacted units for the remaining distance and cause Damage 4 Injury. Resolve falls normally. | Drafted. |
| **Brace** | Tank | BRACE until the end of the turn: incoming attacks suffer -4 to hit. This unit cannot move, act or react while BRACING. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
| **Come On Then** | Tank | Test STR. On success, enemies within 6 inches must target this unit with their ATTACK actions. Duration and legal-target exceptions require ruling. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
| **Tough as Nails** | Tank | Once per round, when attacked in melee without being wounded, ignore the hit's effects. Subsequent attacks against this unit suffer -4 to injure until the end of the turn. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
| **Rampage** | Charger | After DOWNING an enemy with a CHARGE ATTACK, move the remaining charge distance. If this brings the unit within 1 inch of another enemy, make a free MELEE ATTACK against it. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
| **Bull** | Charger | When declaring a CHARGE, test STR. On success, pass through non-LOS-blocking scatter, obstacles and barricades. Enemies contacted test AGI; on failure, push them 2 inches in your chosen direction and PIN them. Cannot pass reinforced doors, buildings or LOS-blocking structures. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
| **Human Bullet** | Charger | Determine CHARGE distance using MOVE plus the total of 2d6 instead of 1d6. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
| **Goliath** | General | Wield two 2 HANDED melee weapons as if they were 1 HANDED. The off-hand still contributes only one attack die unless another rule explicitly overrides it. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
| **Tough** | General | Gain +1 Wound, up to the unit’s limit: 3, or 4 for a leader. | Accepted. |
| **Big Fella** | General | Ignore UNWIELDY on this unit's weapons. | Accepted; no added restrictions. |
