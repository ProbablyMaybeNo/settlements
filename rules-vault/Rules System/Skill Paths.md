# 🧠 Skill Paths

Skills are the **third lever**: stats decide *if* you hit, weapons decide *how bad*, **skills decide what else happens**. A skill is a **verb or a conditional exception** — a new option or situational effect, **not** a flat stat increase (those are the least interesting, so we avoid them — where a numeric edge is needed it's **conditional** (e.g. +1 to hit *in the open*) and kept **high-tier**).

## How it works — one currency
Everything runs off **stat points**, spent only on the five **path-stats** (STR, AGI, DEX, INT, NRV):

- **Every +2 in a path-stat unlocks that path's next tier** — T1 at +2 · T2 at +4 · T3 at +6.
- **Every 2 stat points spent = 1 skill point.**
- Spend a skill point on any skill from a **tier you've unlocked**.

Investment does double duty: raising a stat both opens deeper skills *and* pays for them. **Specialise deep** (6 into STR → Combat T3 + 3 combat skills) or **spread wide** (2 into three stats → T1 in three paths).

| Rank | Stat points | → Skills | Start cap |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Recruit** | 2 | 1 | +2 |
| **Specialist** | 4 | 2 | +4 |
| **Leader** | 6 | 3 | +4 |

Campaign growth lifts the cap to **+6** (Tier 3). See [[Unit Design]] · [[Progression]].

## The five paths
Each path-stat has one path with three tiers. **WND and MOV have no path** — they're fixed (1 and 6"), raised only by a specific skill below. *Path names are WIP.*

| Stat | Path | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **STR** | Combat / Muscle | melee, force, grappling, breaking, hauling |
| **DEX** | Shooting / Perception | ranged, aim, spotting, trick shots |
| **AGI** | Movement / Acrobatics | climbing, dodging, repositioning, escaping |
| **INT** | Expertise / Knowledge | hacking, traps, medicine, tech, terrain |
| **NRV** | Bravery / Morale | rallying, resisting fear, reckless aggression |

## Skill scaffold
First-pass placement into plain **Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3** (low / mid / high power). This is a *scaffold*, not the final list — building out the full skill list is its own dedicated pass (we'll plan it first).

> [!info] Two rules for that pass
> ① The genuinely powerful skills live in **T3**. ② **Every tier must carry skills worth taking** — no filler padding out the good ones. Favour *connected* skills (a grapple line, a shooting line) and some **cross-path** combos.

### Combat / Muscle (STR)
- **T1** — *+1 melee attack* (conditional); *Knockback* push X"; *Breakdown* autopass the breach interaction; *Deadlift* pick up & move scatter terrain; *Strong Arm* double thrown range.
- **T2** — *Muscle Override* force a controlled door without the terminal; *Human Shield* give cover to a friendly; *Super Slam* melee hits Stun; *Grapple* a hit grapples the target (escape = opposed STR; a grappled unit can be carried).
- **T3** — *Falcon Punch* melee hits explode (splash); *Squeeze* head-to-head STR vs a grappled foe, they fail = auto-hit; *The Muscle* +2 injury **on a charge**; *Slam & Throw* hurl a grappled enemy.

### Shooting / Perception (DEX)
- **T1** — *Long Barrel* +5" range; *Ready to React* free shot at −2 vs a charger; *Bank Shot* ricochet 1 bounce (6") around a corner.
- **T2** — *Sniper* ignore long-range penalty; *I See You* ignore Hidden when targeting; *Lookout* lend a friendly your LOS for charges; *One in a Million* trigger a terrain feature at long range.
- **T3** — *Dead Eye* +1 to hit vs targets **in the open**; *Quick Shot* two shots for one action, 2nd at −2; *Gunslinger* fire two pistols in one action.

### Movement / Acrobatics (AGI)
- **T1** — *Sure-Footed* auto-pass leap/climb/jump; *Like a Cat* ignore fall damage; *Dodge* −1 to be hit in melee; *Leaper* double jump/leap distance.
- **T2** — *Houdini* become Hidden when targeted at long range; *Sidestep* move 3" when charged (may break the charge); *Water Walker* cross liquids as normal; *Slide* move through one enemy during a move/sprint; *Double Dash* Sprint for one action.
- **T3** — *Ghost Blade* use AGI instead of STR in melee; *Dual Wield* two 1-handed melee weapons (2nd attack −2); *Trading Spaces* swap board position with an enemy within 12"; *In-N-Out* free disengage + 3" after a charge hit.

### Expertise / Knowledge (INT)
- **T1** — *Hacker* +1 with terminals; *Locksmith* +1 lockpicking; *Trapper* place a trap free once/turn.
- **T2** — *Medic* auto-revive a Down friendly once/turn; *Computer Whiz* use a terminal twice for one action; *Mastermind* +2 in terminal head-to-heads; *Turret Tamer* seize a turret within 12" LOS.
- **T3** — *Lockdown* freeze another terminal for a turn; *Kaboom* place a remote mine, detonate any time (even mid-enemy-action); *Camo King* untargetable by ranged for a turn.

### Bravery / Morale (NRV)
- **T1** — *Steady* clear 1 Stress from yourself once/turn; *Rattle-Proof* ignore the first Stress each round; *Battle Cry* an enemy that charges you gains +1 Stress.
- **T2** — *Rally* spend an action to clear a Nerve state on a friendly within 6"; *Fearless* immune to **Shook**; *Bloodlust* +1 melee attack the turn a friendly goes Down within 6".
- **T3** — *Iron Will* auto-pass one Nerve test per game; *Fanatic* can't Bolt or Break — instead of cracking it gains a reckless-charge bonus; *Unbreakable* friendlies within 6" gain −1 to Stress taken.

### WND / MOV
WND and MOV **have no path and no tiers** — they stay **1** and **6"** forever. The *only* exception is a handful of specific skills (a Tough skill granting +1 WND, a Fleet skill granting +2 MOV), placed when we build the full list. There's no standing option to raise them.
