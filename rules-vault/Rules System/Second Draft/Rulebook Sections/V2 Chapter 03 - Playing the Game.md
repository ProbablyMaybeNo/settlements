# 3. Playing the Game

## 3.1 Your Crew and Settlement

![[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Text Assemblies/V2 Text - Persistent play#Persistent play]]

## 3.2 Choosing a Battle Roster

Agree a Crew Rating, then select fighters and equipment from your settlement. The Crew explains values, composition and carrying limits.

## 3.3 Miniatures and Equipment

Represent equipment clearly, agree proxies and use facing marks. Unit profiles and equipment entries provide the specific numbers.

## 3.4 Preparing a Battle

Choose a scenario with a primary and secondary objective. Establish the battlefield, terrain functions and deployment before play. Scenario specifics are reserved for Ross.

![[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-354 - Non-killing victory routes#^text-s075-b001]]

## 3.5 Following a Battle

Resolve the Start Phase, alternate fighter activations and reactions, then resolve the End Phase. The standard format is a 36-inch-square board and six rounds.

## 3.6 After the Battle

Record the result, casualties, equipment changes and earned rewards. Resolve experience and the Settlement Round before preparing your next crew.
