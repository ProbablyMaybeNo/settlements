# Reaction Chains — For Testing

> [!warning] TEST VARIANT — NOT ADOPTED
> Target-only counterfire candidate requested by Ross. Current rules still prohibit reactions triggering reactions. No playtest of this variant has yet occurred; fun and balance are untested.

## Target-only counterfire candidate

1. Resolve the initial action and its normal first reaction at their existing timings. Ordinary Return Fire retains its existing simultaneous resolution: complete both attacks and all outcomes before considering this test's reply. If the initial reaction interrupts an unfinished action, keep that action paused until the reply window ends, then resume or end it under the existing interruption rules.
2. After a **Shoot or Return Fire reaction** and all outcomes of that exchange resolve, **only the fighter targeted by that shooting reaction** may make **one counter-shot against the fighter that just reacted**. Check the target's existing READY, consciousness/conditions, unengaged status, forward arc, LOS, legal target and weapon range at that moment. This adds only this post-resolution trigger for the test; it permits neither arbitrary targets nor third-party joining. Resolve the counter-shot completely. It is a separate shot, not retroactive simultaneous Return Fire.
3. The counter-shot spends READY immediately and marks Reacted under existing costs. **One reaction per fighter per round remains absolute**, including fighters in the first exchange; READY is not refreshed and no repeated or free reaction is granted. Ordinary attack restrictions and READY-loss rules remain. An original acting fighter may reply only if independently eligible, still holding READY and not already Reacted.
4. There is **at most one eligible target's reply to the initial shooting reaction**. The initial reacting shooter has already Reacted and cannot answer again. Declining or lacking an eligible target ends the sequence immediately; non-shooting reactions do not extend it. Resolved shots and outcomes are never cancelled or rewound by later shots. Any interrupted original action then resumes or ends under the existing rules.

For this test only, replace the ban on shooting reactions triggering further shooting reactions with the single target-only reply above. Keep one reacting model per individual trigger: the original action permits its normal one reactor, and that shooting reaction permits only its eligible target's separate reply. No simultaneous multishooter pile-on, third-party chain or reaction stack is permitted. All other core limits remain unchanged.

## Abstract tabletop example — not played

Assume both fighters meet normal eligibility, arc, LOS and range requirements. A completes a Ready Core Action. B spends READY to Reaction Shoot at A at that action's end and misses. A still has READY and has not Reacted, so A may spend READY for the test counter-shot at B after B's shot resolves. B has already Reacted and cannot reply again; no other fighter can join. Two READY tokens were spent. If A had lost READY or become ineligible, there would be no counter-shot.

## Tiny playtest checklist

- [ ] Record the longest sequence's duration.
- [ ] Record fighters involved: at most two reacting fighters, including the eligible target; no third-party joining.
- [ ] Record READY spent and any reply prevented by existing eligibility or READY-loss rules.
- [ ] Check whether ordinary activation decisions remain meaningful.
- [ ] Check whether larger crews gain excessive advantage.
