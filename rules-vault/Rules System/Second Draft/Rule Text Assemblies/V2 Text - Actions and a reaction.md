# Actions and a reaction

### Actions and a reaction

Mara activates, spends her Move to advance into cover, then spends her Core to Ready. Her activation ends and the opponent activates a fighter. At the end of that enemy's completed Move, Mara has line of sight to the enemy within her forward arc and weapon range and meets the reaction eligibility requirements. She spends READY to Shoot and marks Reacted. The shot hits but fails Injury: the enemy gains Stress and becomes Pinned. Its completed movement is not undone; resolve or forfeit any unused Core slot according to its new state. Mara cannot react again this round; gaining replacement READY does not clear her Reacted marker.

A Pinned fighter can wait while its crew activates other fighters. Once nearby enemies have spent their attacks and reactions, an exposed target may provide an opening: activate the Pinned fighter, spend Move to stand, then use Core to Shoot in that same activation. The opponent cannot insert a normal activation between standing and shooting, but eligible reactions still apply. Until it stands, incoming ranged hits suffer −2. An enemy able to reach it in melee instead gains +2 to hit. Choosing the recovery moment matters; standing immediately is not compulsory.
