# Opposed melee

### Opposed melee

A STR 2 attacker rolls 17 and 8, for totals **19 and 10**. A STR 1 defender rolls 14 and 6, for **15 and 7**. Only the attacker's 19 exceeds the defender's highest 15, so the attacker scores one hit. The defender has no die higher than 19 and scores none. Neither side counts ordinary test successes here. If both highest totals were 19, neither would score a hit.
