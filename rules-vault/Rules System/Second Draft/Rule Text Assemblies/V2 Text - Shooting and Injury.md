# Shooting and Injury

### Shooting and Injury

A DEX 2 rifle user at Stress 1 shoots a target in heavy cover with two attack dice. They have no other bonus, so each die adds 2 − 1 − 2 = **−1**. Rolls of 14 and 9 become 13 and 8: one hit at the ordinary 13+ threshold. The rifle's Damage 2 attacks Armour 1. An Injury roll of 13 becomes 14, so it fails: the target gains 1 Stress and becomes Pinned. A 14 would total 15 and put the target Down instead. The shooter's Stress never modifies Injury.
