## Consequential proposed decisions

One Wound for all humans; one own attack and one reaction per round; three-die attack ceiling; no Stress penalty to Injury; simultaneous ordinary shooting for Return Fire; thirty-nine skills with no free attack chains; utility-only drones; physical Claim; ten injury outcomes; modest winner income; full rating for advances and discounted gear. See the audit for reasons and provenance.

These decisions are written to make a coherent playable proposal. They have not been separately adopted by Ross or validated by playtests.
