# Settlements Rules System Version 2

**Individual rules:** [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards - Start Here|Open the rule-card library]] · [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Settlements Rulebook - Assembled|Read the agreed chapter structure]]

Rule text is now maintained in the individual cards and reused here through live embeds. Existing section links remain available. Detailed objective/scenario proposals are retained separately for Ross; this reorganization does not establish balance or playtesting.

Settlements is a skirmish game about ordinary people fighting over the things their community needs. Cover a friend's advance, open a route through a building, shut down an enemy device, recover someone who has fallen, and get the job done. Your settlement and its people continue from one battle to the next, whether or not you play a campaign.

> [!info] Working edition
> This is the working rules text for Version 2; scenario specifics are reserved for Ross. New and revised mechanics, catalogue costs, and progression rates await playtesting. A familiar game of six rounds with 5–10 fighters per side aims for about 60 minutes; that duration has not yet been demonstrated. Design decisions and research evidence are kept in [[Draft 2 - Design Requirements and Research Audit]], outside the playing rules.
