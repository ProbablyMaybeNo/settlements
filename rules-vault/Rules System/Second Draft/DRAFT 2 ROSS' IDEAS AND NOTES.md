## MECHANICS

### Campaign Mechanics

Add rivalries as a persistent mechanic and part of the players evolving roster. Offer rules that are simple but effective at generating a narrative between your crew and your opponents. 

Perhaps add a "hatred" keyword as a injury result which carries over between games.
