# Sentry turret

## Summary

Its assault rifle uses the current Assault rifle profile through a 180-degree firing arc. It has 1 Wound and Armour 2. A persistent ALERT token permits multiple reactions per round. It powers down at the start of its deploying unit’s next activation. A qualified unit may spend Core and pass an INT test to reactivate it; enemy hackers may seize a powered-down turret through a terminal.
