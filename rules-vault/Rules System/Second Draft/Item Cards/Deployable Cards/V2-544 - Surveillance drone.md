# Surveillance drone

## Summary

Core Target: choose a visible enemy within 12 inches. Roll d20 +3 against 13+, ignoring cover and Hidden penalties. Success Marks it until the start of this drone’s next activation: friendly shooting gains +2; the target loses Hidden and cannot Hide. Your crew may have one Marked enemy; a new mark replaces the old one.
