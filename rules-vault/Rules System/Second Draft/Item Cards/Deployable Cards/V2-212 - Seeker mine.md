# Seeker mine

## Summary

Deploy within 3 inches. It arms at the end of the operator’s activation. The first enemy entering within 6 inches triggers it without needing line of sight. Move it immediately by the shortest legal route towards that enemy, up to 12 inches; it may climb walls, cross open windows and fall any distance. Detonate within 1 inch, or at its closest reachable point if it cannot get there. Resolve a 3-inch-diameter Blast at Damage 2.
