# Bull

## Summary

When declaring a Charge, test STR. Success lets you pass through non-LOS-blocking scatter, obstacles and barricades. Contacted enemies test AGI; on failure, push them 2 inches in your chosen direction and Pin them. Reinforced doors, buildings and LOS-blocking structures still stop you.
