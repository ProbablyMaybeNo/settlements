# Mark Target

## Summary

Spend Core and test DEX against a visible enemy within 24 inches. Mark it until the start of your next activation: friendly shooting gains +2, and it loses Hidden and cannot Hide. Your crew may have one Marked enemy; a new mark replaces the old one.
