# Grapple

## Summary

Core/opposed STR holds enemy; neither attacks/reacts/operates. Drag half speed; target Core/opposed STR escapes with 1″ separation.

## Rule text

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Skill | Grapple |
| Use | Core; engaged |
| Effect | Opposed STR against one engaged enemy. Pass: hold it. Neither can attack, react, or operate equipment while the hold remains. The holder may drag at half MOVE and release at the start of its own turn. The held fighter may spend Core to oppose STR and escape; success releases it and allows a free 1-inch separation. Down, Unconscious, Pinned, or a morale condition on the holder ends the hold. No human-shield or automatic takedown effect. |
