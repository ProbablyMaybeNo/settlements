# Throw

## Summary

While Carrying, or using a Grappled enemy, spend Core and test STR. Failure retains the load or hold. Success ends it and throws the model 6 inches in a straight line away from you. A thrown unit stops on another unit and Pins both; hitting Heavy terrain causes a Damage 4 Injury roll. A thrown structure pushes contacted units for its remaining distance and causes a Damage 4 Injury roll against each. Resolve falls and normal solid-terrain stops.
