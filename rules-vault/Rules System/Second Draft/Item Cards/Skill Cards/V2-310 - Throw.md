# Throw

## Summary

Core/STR throws a carried load or Grappled enemy 6 inches; unit collisions Pin, Heavy-terrain or thrown-structure hits use Damage 4; normal falls.

## Rule text

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Skill | Throw |
| Use | Core; Carrying or Grappling |
| Effect | While Carrying a structure or unit, or Grappling an enemy, spend Core and test STR. Failure spends Core and retains the load or hold. Success ends Carrying or the hold and moves the thrown model 6 inches in a straight line away from you; do not also apply the ordinary 3-inch release. Thrown unit: stop on another unit and Pin both. Collision with Heavy terrain causes one Damage 4 Injury roll. Resolve falls normally. Thrown structure: each unit it contacts moves with it for the remaining distance and suffers one Damage 4 Injury roll. Resolve any resulting falls. Use normal forced-movement stops at immovable terrain and legal non-overlapping final positions; the structure’s stated pushing of contacted units overrides the ordinary stop-on-a-unit rule. Use normal Injury and casualty rules for these automatic collision hits; falls are separate hazards. |
