# Cargo crane

## Summary

Move along its declared cable by up to 6 inches total per interaction. Raising, lowering, picking up cargo and rotating through 360 degrees may be included. Dropping held cargo takes an interaction. Units beneath test AGI: success moves them 1 inch clear of its footprint; failure resolves Crush. If the crane first needs to collect cargo, collecting and dropping require separate interactions.
