# Lift or cargo elevator

## Summary

Move up or down by up to 6 inches per interaction; riders and cargo travel with it. Controls may be in contact or linked to a terminal. A hacker may force the lift to drop; resolve Falling for its occupants. Use Displace for units beneath a normal descent.
