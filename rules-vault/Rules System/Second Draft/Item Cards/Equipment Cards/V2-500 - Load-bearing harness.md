# Load-bearing harness

## Summary

Ignore Unwieldy and the movement reduction for carrying or dragging a load. Hands, capacity and other carrying restrictions remain unchanged. Reusable.

## Design notes

The capability is agreed, but current weapon profiles have no STR handling threshold. No new threshold is invented. Ross’s earlier intent mentioned movement penalties: confirm whether this should instead ignore Unwieldy’s penalty after movement. Rank and skill requirements are not waived.

Source: [[Settlements/Design Workshop/Battle Catalogue Review/Equipment Category Review]]. Drafting is not approval or evidence of battle testing.

### Follow-up decision — 2026-11-09

Ross explicitly approved ignoring Unwieldy and the half-movement carrying penalty. This replaces the inactive STR-threshold proposal. The harness does not remove the hand requirement, Sprint/Charge restrictions or handling eligibility. Strong Arm is unchanged.
