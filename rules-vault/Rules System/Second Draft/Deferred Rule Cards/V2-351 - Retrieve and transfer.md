# Retrieve and transfer

## Summary

Core/contact takes one cargo; giver Core passes to contact friendly. Down drops cargo; drones cannot carry.

> [!warning] Earlier proposal — reserved for Ross
> Retained for complete design coverage. Detailed objective/scenario rules are deferred and excluded from the assembled rulebook.

## Rule text

**Retrieve** picks up a portable objective with a Core Interact in contact. A fighter may carry one; the scenario identifies it as normal or Heavy. Passing it to a friendly in contact takes the giver's Core. A Down fighter drops it. A drone cannot carry it.
