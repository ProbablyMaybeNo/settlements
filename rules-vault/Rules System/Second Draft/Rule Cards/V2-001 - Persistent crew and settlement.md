Persistent rosters

## Rule text

### Persistent rosters

*Settlements takes place in an ever changing universe that evolves over time partly due to you, the players, influence. As the universe grows so does your little slice of the metaphorical pie. This is represented in the rules by persistence mechanics relating to your settlement and crew. When players first start their Settlements journey they are given the materials and credits needed to hire a crew to help protect and build a basic settlement. As you play the game your settlement will grow and take shape so too will it's inhabitance who become part of your Settlements Roster and can be assigned to work in the settlement or fight battles as part of the "Battle crew." Some of the settlements inhabitants might have a more detailed history than others but conceptually they are all individual characters with their own backstories, personalities, preferences, and inherent aptitude for mindless violence. 

*Settlements start with enough bunks for 12 crew members. To entice more than 12 inhabitants to move to the settlement you must gather the materials needed to build additional accommodations. See thge structure list for details on which structures increase the Settlement Roster cap. Inhabitants on your Settlement Roster can be assigned to strcutres and provide a boost to that structures benefit or they can be recruited into the Battle Crew and given equipment, weapons, armor, etc. Players determine the Crew Rating or points limit for their upcoming battle. Then they recruit inhabitance from their settlement pool and equip them with items from their settlement stance. Each crew member, weapon, equipment, etc, has a predetermined points cost based on their cost in credits. The combined total of all the units and their equipment cannot exceed the determine crew rating for the battle. After a battle the SETTLEMENT round begins, all inhabitants are placed back into the SETTLEMENT ROSTER and can be assigned jobs or added to the Battle Crew for an upcoming battle.
