# Persistent crew and settlement

## Summary

Your settlement keeps a persistent roster of inhabitants. It starts with housing for twelve.

## Rule text

Your settlement keeps a persistent roster of inhabitants. It starts with housing for twelve.

^text-s002-b000

Before each battle, choose a Battle Crew and equipment within the agreed Crew Rating. After battle, inhabitants return to the Settlement Roster.

^text-s002-b001

Progress carries between battles without requiring a story or map campaign. This test set contains the approved crew and aftermath foundations; settlement construction and unreviewed services remain outside the battle packet.
