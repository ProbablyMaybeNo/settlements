# Orders

## Summary

Leaders 2 orders/round; specialists 1. Free READY to another eligible friendly within 12 inches/LOS during own activation; Activated allowed; one READY held maximum.

## Rule text

Each round, each leader has 2 orders and each specialist has 1 order. Reset these allowances at the start of each round; unused orders do not carry over.

^orders-allowance

During the issuer's own activation, issuing an order is free and spends neither Move nor Core. Spend 1 order to grant 1 READY token to another friendly fighter within 12 inches and line of sight of the issuer. The issuer cannot order itself.

^orders-issue

The recipient must be standing and conscious, and cannot be Pinned, Down, Unconscious, Captured, otherwise incapacitated, or have any morale condition (Broken, Bolting, or Bugging Out). A fighter that has already Activated this round can receive READY from an order.

^orders-recipient

A fighter can hold at most one READY token. Receiving READY from an order does not grant an additional reaction or a new activation. The existing limit of one reaction per fighter per round, reaction eligibility, and READY-loss rules still apply.

## Design notes

> [!question] Leader identity needs clarification
> V2-034 distinguishes the nominated crew leader from the Leader training grade, and says acting leadership grants no stat or action bonus. Ross's Orders ruling has not yet specified which meaning of leader receives the 2-order allowance. No recruitment-grade, crew-composition or acting-leadership rule has been changed to decide this.

> [!question] Timing within the issuer's activation needs clarification
> Issuing during the issuer's own activation is approved. Whether an order may interrupt an action or reaction already being resolved, or must wait until that resolution finishes, has not been decided. No finer timing exception has been added.

> [!question] Issuer conditions need clarification
> Ross specified the recipient's condition restrictions. Whether a Pinned or morale-affected issuer can give orders has not been separately decided; no exemption from existing condition restrictions is created here.
