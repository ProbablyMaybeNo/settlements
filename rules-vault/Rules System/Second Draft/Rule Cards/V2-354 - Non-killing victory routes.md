# Non-killing victory routes

## Summary

Every mission must offer primary and secondary success without killing opponents.

## Rule text

Every mission must offer primary and secondary success without killing opponents.

^text-s075-b001

Looting a casualty may be an optional story objective, but cannot be the only way to score. Do not award routine extra points for casualties.
