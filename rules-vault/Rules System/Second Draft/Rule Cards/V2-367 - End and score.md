# End and score

## Summary

End round six, scenario ending or withdrawal; finish current action for scenario ending; score final objectives.

## Rule text

End after round 6, an agreed scenario ending, or voluntary withdrawal. Finish the current action before a scenario ending; withdrawal follows [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Settlements Rules System Version 2#10 Stress and morale|stress and morale]]. Score any end-of-battle objectives, then record the winner or draw.
