# Area attacks, smoke and fire

## Summary

Blast

## Rule text

**Blast**

^text-s065-b000

1. Choose a visible impact point within weapon range. It must be on a flat surface or a model, including a friendly.

2. Make one normal shooting test. On a hit, centre the template there. On a miss, scatter it a d6 inches in a random direction.

3. Use a circle 3 inches in diameter unless the source specifies 5 inches.

4. Automatically hit models in the footprint and within 3 inches above or below it, including friendlies. Solid obstacles and floors block the blast. It cannot reach around corners.

^text-s065-b001

5. Roll Injury for affected fighters using the source's Damage and effects. Devices use their hit-based damage rules. Do not make another hit roll for each target.

**Cone**
Place the narrow end of an 8-inch teardrop at the shooter's forward base edge. Aim it in the attack direction, including up or down. Models wholly or partly beneath its directed footprint are automatically hit.

Solid terrain blocks flame. Do not roll to hit or scatter. Resolve the source's Injury or device damage. A persistent fire zone is created only if the profile says so.

**Smoke.** Its source gives placement, size and duration. Use the Smoke grenade profile for its thrown placement and Scatter; smoke emitters and terrain vents use their own rules.

^text-s065-b002

Smoke extends indefinitely upwards. It blocks line of sight through it between units outside. Units inside count as in Heavy cover. A unit shooting from inside treats a target outside as in Heavy cover.

**Molotov cocktails.** Use the Molotov profile: its Blast applies On Fire instead of Injury. It creates no separate persistent fire zone. Resolve On Fire using Equipment conditions.

A fire zone exists only when a source explicitly creates one. Follow that source’s footprint, duration and exposure procedure; do not create a zone from ordinary flame attacks or Molotovs.
