# Measuring and shared conventions

## Summary

Measure distances between bases and movement along the actual route. You may measure before choosing an action.

## Rule text

Measure distances between bases and movement along the actual route. You may measure before choosing an action.

^text-s004-b001

Units are engaged within 1 inch on a reachable surface.

^text-s004-b002

Bases cannot pass through walls, standing enemies or gaps they cannot fit through.

^text-s004-b003

Declare targets, dice allocations and choices before rolling.

^text-s004-b004

If an action is interrupted, its declared actions and consumables remain spent.

^text-s004-b005

Identical effects do not stack. Reroll a die only once.

^text-s004-b000

Ordinary reactions are limited to once per round and cannot trigger reactions.

^text-s004-b006

Specific skill wording may override its stated limits.

^text-s004-b007
