# Measuring and shared conventions

## Summary

Distances and contact; Legal movement and measuring; Friendly and device definitions; Rounding and minimum pool; Reroll limit; Effect priority and stacking; Declarations and spent resources; Displaced models and bad dice

## Rule text

### Distances and contact

- **Within:** the shortest distance between bases is no greater than the stated distance. Base contact means touching; floors must also match unless a rule says otherwise. Engagement is within 1 inch on a reachable surface.

^text-s004-b001

### Legal movement and measuring

- Measure movement along the path actually travelled, using the same point on the base throughout. You may premeasure. A unit cannot move through a solid wall, another enemy standing unit's base, or a gap its base cannot fit through.

^text-s004-b002

### Friendly and device definitions

- **Friendly** includes the acting fighter unless the rule says “another.” **Enemy** means the opposing crew. A **fighter** is a human model. A **device** is equipment, not a fighter.

^text-s004-b003

### Rounding and minimum pool

- Fractions are rounded down unless the rule specifies otherwise. Never reduce an ordinary attack pool below one die. Reroll a die at most once and keep the new result.

^text-s004-b004

### Effect priority and stacking

- Identically named effects do not stack. Specific printed exceptions override the general procedure, but never the limits of one reaction per fighter per round, one reacting model per action, or the prohibition on reactions triggering reactions.

^text-s004-b005

### Declarations and spent resources

- **Round:** both crews complete their available activations, then resolve the End Phase. **Turn:** one player activates one fighter, or voluntarily ends their crew's activations for the round. **Activation:** that fighter resolves its Move and Core Actions in either order before the opponent activates a fighter. Resolve each action and its reactions fully before beginning the next action.

^text-s004-b000

- Declare choices, targets, weapon allocation, and any conditional bonuses before rolling. If an action becomes impossible after a reaction, its slots and declared consumables are still spent.

^text-s004-b006

### Displaced models and bad dice

- A displaced model returns to its agreed position. Reroll a cocked or off-table die. Agree the intended legal position before moving a model onto unstable scenery, then mark that position safely.

^text-s004-b007
