# Battle records

## Summary

Track profiles/loadouts, value, skills, objectives, score, round, Activated/actions, READY/Reacted, states, timers, revival and consumables.

## Rule text

Record the agreed limit, scenario, six-round counter, score, and primary and secondary objectives. For each fighter record:

^text-s100-b000

Beside each entry, leave boxes for Activated, Move, Core, READY, Reacted, Stress, morale, Pinned, Hidden, Down round, revival used, consumables, and once-per-battle skills. You need only the weapon, skill, and device rules actually fielded; copy their short entries onto the roster or cards.

^text-s100-b001
