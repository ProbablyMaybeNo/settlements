| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-324 - Ambush\|Ambush]] | Charge modifier | If you begin a Charge Hidden, test AGI before moving. Capture still requires Secure and extraction. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-326 - Ghost\|Ghost]] | Once per round | Shooting a one-die weapon while in cover does not itself remove your Hidden. Other reveal triggers still apply. Cannot use with Blast or Automatic weapons. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-327 - Acrobatic\|Acrobatic]] | Passive | +2 to AGI for climbing or jumping. Climbing still costs double vertical distance and unsupported jumps remain limited to 3 inches. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-328 - Dodge\|Dodge]] | Reaction modifier | Reroll a failed Dive test. You still need READY, still become Pinned, and move no farther than 3 inches. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-329 - Quick\|Quick]] | Passive | Increase MOVE by 1, to a maximum of 7 before armour and injuries. Does not alter Charge's d6 or the jump limit. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-330 - Sneak\|Sneak]] | Passive | Hidden no longer halves your normal Move. Other causes of half-speed movement still apply. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-331 - Sprinter\|Sprinter]] | Both | Dash up to twice MOVE plus 2 inches. It still costs both slots and grants no attack. |
