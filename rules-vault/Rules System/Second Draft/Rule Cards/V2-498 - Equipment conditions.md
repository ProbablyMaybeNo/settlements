# Equipment conditions

## Summary

On Fire escalates on failed d6 checks; Poisoned drains MOVE; Stunned skips an activation; Addicted causes one battle of withdrawal.

## Rule text

### On Fire

Immediately move 6 inches in a random direction. If water is visible in any direction, move toward it instead. Contact with water removes On Fire without causing Pinned. While On Fire, the unit cannot take ordinary actions. Use the normal forced-movement and falling rules.

Make one fire check each End Phase, starting this round. Roll an unmodified d6. On 4+, remove On Fire and retain any Pinned or Down state. Each failed check of 1–3 escalates the effect:

1. Move another 6 inches as above, become Pinned and miss the next activation.
2. Become Down.
3. Become Unconscious.

The missed activation remains lost even if the fire goes out first. Removing On Fire does not clear an unrelated Pinned condition. Use the shared casualty rules.

^condition-on-fire-rule

### Stunned

Shocked and Stunned are the same condition. Become Pinned and miss your next activation, then remove Stunned. Pinned remains until removed normally. Reapplying Stunned does not create extra missed activations.

^condition-stunned-rule

### Poisoned

Immediately lose 2 MOVE. At each End Phase after the application round, lose another 2 MOVE while Poisoned. When cumulative poison loss reaches natural MOVE, become Unconscious; a MOVE bonus does not delay this.

An antidote immediately removes Poisoned and restores all MOVE lost to it. It does not revive an Unconscious unit.

^condition-poisoned-rule

### Addicted

Using any chem in each of two consecutive battles makes the unit Addicted for the crew’s next battle. The chem types need not match. Count the recipient’s use, not the unit administering it.

While Addicted, reduce STR, DEX, AGI, INT, NRV and MOVE by 2. Wounds are unchanged. Using any chem suppresses all withdrawal penalties for the rest of that battle; its +4 bonus applies normally.

Remove Addicted after that battle, even if the unit sat it out. Continued chem use can trigger it again. A battle without chem use breaks the consecutive-battle sequence.

^condition-addicted-rule

## Design notes

Source: [[Settlements/Design Workshop/Battle Catalogue Review/Equipment Category Review]]. The original feedback is preserved there.

**Essential unresolved timing — recommendations only:**

- Fire: use one check per End Phase, starting in the application round. After a failed first check, the next activation is lost even if fire goes out before it. Water cancels fire-driven Pinned; it does not clear an unrelated Pinned condition. Use ordinary forced-movement and falling rules for the 6-inch moves. Confirm whether all ordinary actions are barred while burning, or only the stated missed activation.
- Poison: take the initial −2 immediately, then another −2 at each later round’s End Phase. Measure cumulative loss against natural MOVE so a stim cannot delay Unconscious. Recommended: an antidote restores only MOVE lost to Poisoned. The notes do not yet settle the tick or restoration.

These timing recommendations are not adopted. Keep this card Partial. There is no Blinded rule, generic toxin attack, extra Injury roll or addiction subsystem.

### Follow-up decision — 2026-11-09

Ross approved the proposed fire and poison timing and confirmed d6 failures of 1–3 escalate to Pinned, Down and Unconscious. Fire checks begin at the current End Phase; poison’s later ticks begin after its application round. He approved restoring poison-lost MOVE and ending Poisoned with antidote. He added the one-battle Addicted rule and clarified its trigger as any chem in each of two consecutive battles. Earlier unresolved timing notes and addiction deferral are superseded; the retained historical feedback is not the current rule. Review status is not promoted to Confirmed or Locked.
