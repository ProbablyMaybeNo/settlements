# Forced movement

## Summary

Move a pushed or pulled unit in the stated direction. Stop at a solid obstruction or standing base. Lose any remaining distance and never overlap bases.

## Rule text

Move a pushed or pulled unit in the stated direction. Stop at a solid obstruction or standing base. Lose any remaining distance and never overlap bases.

^text-s027-b000

A unit pushed into scenery becomes Pinned. If it collides with another model, both become Pinned. Crossing a ledge causes a fall.

When scenery moves into a unit, use the feature's Displace or Crush rule.
