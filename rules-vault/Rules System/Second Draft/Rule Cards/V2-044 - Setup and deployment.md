# Setup and deployment

## Summary

Scenario instructions override these defaults. Follow the steps in order.

## Rule text

Scenario instructions override these defaults. Follow the steps in order.

^text-s014-b000

1. **Choose a scenario.** Read its objectives, scoring, duration and special requirements.

2. **Agree the Crew Rating limit.**

3. **Prepare the battlefield.** Unless the scenario says otherwise, use a 48 × 48-inch board, opposite 6-inch deployment zones and six rounds.

4. **Choose and place terrain.** Follow the scenario's terrain selection and layout instructions. Use the Battlefield and terrain agreement guidance where the scenario leaves choices to the players. Include the chosen interactive features.

5. **Agree terrain properties and starting states.** Identify cover, terrain that blocks line of sight, movement restrictions and hazards. Mark controls, terminal links and permitted positions using the terrain rules already explained.

6. **Place objectives.** Follow the scenario's placement instructions.

7. **Select and reveal crews.** Show fighters, weapons, equipment, skills, devices and any explicitly permitted reserves.

8. **Determine deployment.** Roll a d20 each, rerolling ties. The higher roller chooses a deployment zone; the other player deploys first.

9. **Deploy crews.** Alternate placing one fighter within its deployment zone. Units may start off-board only when a rule permits it.

10. **Begin round one.** Prepare the round counter and required markers. Units begin with Move and Core available; apply starting conditions, then roll for initiative and begin activations.
