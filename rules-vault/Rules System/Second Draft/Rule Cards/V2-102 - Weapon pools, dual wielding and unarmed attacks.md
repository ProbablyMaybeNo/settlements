# Weapon pools, dual wielding and unarmed attacks

## Summary

Dual-wielding; Three-die ceiling; Unarmed and melee Sidearms

## Rule text

### Three-die ceiling

Using two one-handed weapons gives the dominant weapon's full dice and **one off-hand die**. Identify that die separately and use its own range, Damage, and traits. Both weapons must be eligible for the attack. A shooting pair must contain two firearms; a melee pair may contain melee weapons or Sidearms. Skill exceptions never raise a combined attack pool above **three dice**, including a Charge die. Remove excess dice of your choice before rolling.

^text-s030-b000

### Unarmed and melee Sidearms

A fighter with no eligible held melee weapon may always use one unarmed die at Damage 0. A fighter with only its included knife makes one melee die at Damage 1. A Sidearm may contribute one melee die, using STR to hit and its printed Damage. It does not use DEX in melee. Sidearm shots in melee still require a loaded weapon and use Reload normally; a two-handed gun without Close Combat cannot contribute its shooting pool.

^text-s030-b001
