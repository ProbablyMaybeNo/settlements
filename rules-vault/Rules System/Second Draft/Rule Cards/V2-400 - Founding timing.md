# Founding timing

## Summary

Purchased founding buildings work immediately; allocate Power before first battle; no founding production or rewards.

## Rule text

Founding is the one exception to construction timing: all structures bought during founding are operational for the first battle. Generate and allocate Power once before that battle. You receive no founding production or battle rewards. You may keep resources unspent. Buildings need no scale model; a named box on a settlement sheet is enough.

^text-s085-b001

The founding package can buy a six-person 750-Credit Battle Roster, two basic workers, a Workshop, and a Clinic. The worked example in [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Settlements Rules System Version 2#22 Worked examples|worked examples]] shows the exact accounts. Persistent development does not require a campaign organiser, map, third player, or regular group.
