| Name | Use | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-308 - Grapple\|Grapple]] | Core; enemy within 1 inch | Spend Core and make an opposed STR test against one enemy within 1 inch. Failure spends Core without effect. Success makes you Grappling and the enemy Grappled. The captive cannot take actions or react and must remain within 1 inch as the holder moves along a legal route. At the start of each activation, the captive gets a free opposed STR escape test: success ends the hold and permits its normal activation; failure leaves it unable to act. While Grappling, move at half speed and use only one-handed weapons; Hauler supplies its stated exceptions. These weapon permissions allow shooting outside the held engagement. Enemy ranged attacks targeting you resolve against the captive instead. If an enemy ranged attack at another friendly passes through either model, roll one d6 for the whole attack before hit dice: 4+ keeps the intended target; 1–3 redirects it to the captive. Resolve the normal attack against the actual target, using its protection. For this interception only, the two held models do not themselves make the intended shot illegal; solid terrain and other models still block LOS normally. Area effects still affect every model in their final area. During your activation you may release the captive freely, moving it directly away to a position 3 inches from you. Stop on collision: another unit Pins both; a structure Pins the released unit. Resolve falls normally. The hold also ends if you become Pinned, Down, Unconscious or gain a morale condition; an automatic ending leaves both models in place. Throw can release a captive by its own 6-inch procedure instead. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-309 - Strong Arm\|Hauler]] | Passive | Ignore the movement reduction from Grappling or Carrying a unit or structure. While Grappling, you may also use non-Heavy two-handed weapons. This does not grant that two-handed permission while Carrying, ignore Unwieldy, add hands or waive other carrying restrictions. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-310 - Throw\|Throw]] | Core; Carrying or Grappling | While Carrying a structure or unit, or Grappling an enemy, spend Core and test STR. Failure spends Core and retains the load or hold. Success ends Carrying or the hold and moves the thrown model 6 inches in a straight line away from you; do not also apply the ordinary 3-inch release. Thrown unit: stop on another unit and Pin both. Collision with Heavy terrain causes one Damage 4 Injury roll. Resolve falls normally. Thrown structure: each unit it contacts moves with it for the remaining distance and suffers one Damage 4 Injury roll. Resolve any resulting falls. Use normal forced-movement stops at immovable terrain and legal non-overlapping final positions; the structure’s stated pushing of contacted units overrides the ordinary stop-on-a-unit rule. Use normal Injury and casualty rules for these automatic collision hits; falls are separate hazards. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-311 - Brace\|Brace]] | Core; in heavy cover | Ready and brace in place. Until you next move or take another Core Action, reduce Stress gained from each incoming attack by 1, minimum 0. Hits still Pin normally and may remove READY. This is not armour or injury resistance. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-312 - Bull\|Bull]] | Charge modifier | You may replace the Charge's melee exchange with Shove, gaining +2 to that opposed STR test. There is no separate charge attack or charge die. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-313 - Rage\|Rage]] | Melee modifier | Before rolling an own-turn Fight or Charge exchange, accept 1 Stress after the exchange to gain +2 Damage for its Injury rolls. You must have room for that Stress when declaring. No bonus in defensive exchanges or reaction attacks. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-314 - Tough\|Tough]] | Passive | Gain +1 Wound, up to the unit’s Wound limit. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-315 - Big Fella\|Big Fella]] | Passive | An Unwieldy weapon suffers −1 instead of −2 after your movement. It still occupies its normal hands and slots. |
