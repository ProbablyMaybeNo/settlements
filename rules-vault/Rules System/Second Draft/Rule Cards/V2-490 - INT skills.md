| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-332 - Terminal Training\|Terminal Training]] | Passive | +2 INT to Hack while your own fighter is physically in contact with the terminal. Does not apply through a drone or to portable device hacking. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-333 - Trojan\|Trojan]] | Core | With a hacking kit, test INT against one visible electronic enemy device within 8 inches. Success performs one legal operation with it, then control returns to its owner. A Single-use device is consumed if triggered. A turret needs unspent READY and cannot exceed its attack limit. A drone may move up to 4 inches but cannot perform an objective action. Failure changes nothing. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-334 - Interrupt\|Interrupt]] | Reaction | While touching a terminal, spend READY when an enemy within 12 inches uses a terminal on the same network. This reaction needs the terminal link, not LOS to the hacker. Oppose INT. Success cancels that Hack and Freezes its terminal through this End Phase; failure permits the original Hack to succeed without another roll. A tied test favours the active hacker. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-335 - Technician\|Technician]] | Passive | +2 INT when Deploying or arming a device. Does not improve its firing stat or create equipment. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-336 - Stabilize\|Stabilize]] | Treatment modifier | +2 INT to Treat another fighter. On success set the patient's Stress to 0 instead of 2. It is still Pinned and has used its one revival. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-337 - Doc\|Doc]] | Passive | Carry two medkits in one equipment slot. Pay for both. When Treating another fighter, you may spend its carried medkit instead of yours. Treatment still requires contact and Core. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-338 - Pack Rat\|Pack Rat]] | Passive | Gain one equipment slot, maximum three. Does not add hands or weapon slots. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-339 - Smart\|Smart]] | Interact modifier; once per round | Reroll one failed test for a direct physical Interact, including a mission Work action. The action still costs Core. Does not apply to Hack, Treat, Capture, Deploy, attacks, or skill actions. Automatic interactions remain automatic. |
