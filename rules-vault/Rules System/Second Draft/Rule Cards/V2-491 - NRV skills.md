# NRV skills

## Summary

NRV skills: complete table with links to individual skill cards.

## Rule text

| Name | Use | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-340 - Inspire\|Inspire]] | Core | Test NRV, applying your Stress. Success removes up to 2 Stress from you and from one friendly within 6 inches and LOS. Does not remove Pinned or a morale condition. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-341 - Rally Training\|Rally Training]] | Core | Make the normal Stress-free Rally test for up to two friendlies within 6 inches and LOS instead of one within 3. One test determines both outcomes. You may include yourself. While Broken, you may use this skill only to Rally yourself, not to assist others. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-342 - Motivate\|Motivate]] | Core | Test NRV. Success lets one other conscious, unpinned friendly within 6 inches and LOS travel 2 inches farther during its next normal Move this round. It must spend its own unused Move; no slot is restored. Does not extend Dash, Charge, Disengage, forced flight, or standing up. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-343 - Share the Burden\|Share the Burden]] | Core | Transfer up to 2 Stress in total from friendlies within 3 inches and LOS to yourself, limited by your remaining capacity. No test. Does not remove their morale conditions. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-344 - Hold Fast\|Hold Fast]] | Help Recover modifier | When your Help Recover action helps another fighter in cover, also remove 1 of its Stress. It must actually have cover from at least one visible enemy. No injury-prevention aura is created. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-345 - On Your Feet\|On Your Feet]] | Core | Recover up to two friendlies within 6 inches and LOS instead of one within 3. No test. Neither may be Down or Unconscious; morale conditions remain. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-346 - Commanding Presence\|Commanding Presence]] | Once per round | One friendly within 6 inches and LOS may use your NRV for its Break or Rally test. You must be conscious, unpinned, and without a morale condition. Other modifiers belong to the fighter taking the test. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-347 - Unflinching\|Unflinching]] | Once per battle | After an attack hits you but causes no Wound, ignore Pinned from that attack and retain READY if you had it. Gain all Stress normally. This does not remove pre-existing Pinned or a morale condition. |
