# Reactions and interruption

## Summary

A reaction lets a unit act during an enemy activation. Spend ALERT to perform one eligible Move or Core action. An action that uses both Move and Core cannot be a reaction.

## Rule text

A reaction lets a unit act during an enemy activation. Spend ALERT to perform one eligible Move or Core action. An action that uses both Move and Core cannot be a reaction.

^text-s021-b000

Multiple units may react to the same enemy. Each unit may normally react once per round. Reactions cannot normally trigger other reactions; specific skills and profiles provide their stated exceptions.

^text-s021-b002

**Normal reactions.** React when an enemy starts or ends its activation within your forward line of sight. Shooting requires normal weapon range. You cannot shoot partway through the enemy's movement.

^text-s021-b003

**Against a Charge.** The charged unit may Shoot before the charger moves. The charger must be within the defender's forward line of sight and weapon range.

^text-s021-b004

**Return Fire.** If an enemy shoots you, declare Return Fire before any dice are rolled. The attacker must be within your forward line of sight and weapon range. Both units make independent normal shooting attacks against 13+. Complete both attacks before applying either result. There is no extra reaction penalty.

^text-s021-b005

**Dive.** Before an incoming shot or Charge, test AGI. On success, move up to 3 inches. On failure, stay in place. Become Pinned either way.

^text-s021-b006

Pinning or Downing a unit cancels its unfinished action without refunding the cost. Completed actions are not undone. A simultaneous Return Fire exchange still finishes.

^text-s021-b008

The Interrupt skill uses its stated network exception.
