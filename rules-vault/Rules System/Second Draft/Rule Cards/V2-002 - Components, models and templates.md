# Components, models and templates

## Summary

Standard battle; Miniature equipment; Bases and facing mark; Dice and markers; Template sizes — reconcile

## Rule text

### Standard battle

The standard game has two players. Each player needs 5–10 human miniatures for a starting crew, a roster, several d20 dice, a d6, a d10, measuring tape, and several unique markers. For a standard battle you will need 36 × 36-inch board, terrain, and a round counter numbered 1–6. Larger crews are legal by agreement but increase play time.

^text-s003-b000

Settlements is a WYSIWYG wargame and when possible players should make a legitimate attempt at accuratley equipping your miniatues so their gear matches the unit on paper.

^text-s003-b001

Tokens are an important part of settlements. You will need a good variety of tokens and an solid over all amount. Feel free to create your own tokens out of cardboard, paper, change, or bottle caps, whatever you have access to. We will go over the exact tokens that you may need later in the book.

^text-s003-b003

***Everything mentioned above will be explained later in the rulebook.***

^text-s003-b005

### Bases and facing mark

***Miniatures should be based on 28mm bases with a mark on the front half of each base to signify the center of the units 180 degree vision Arc.*** Agree proxies and unusual sizes before deployment. A tall pose, banner, or weapon barrel does not make a fighter easier to see. Paper footprints can represent every interactive feature in this book.

^text-s003-b002

### Template sizes — reconcile

Finally, you may want to create or buy some templates: a **3-inch-diameter circle**, a **5-inch-diameter circle**, and an **8-inch flame template**. The weapon specifies which template applies. Owning physical templates is optional. A scatter dice or direction randomize might be helpful as well.

^text-s003-b004

## Design notes

### V2-006 — Template sizes — reconcile
Confirmed by Ross on 2026-10-22: the template set and weapon-specific selection above replace the earlier 6-inch blast suggestion and universal 2-inch-radius Blast template.

Profile assignments remain unresolved: Frag grenade (V2-259), Fire bottle initial Blast (V2-260), Grenade launcher (V2-300), Rocket launcher (V2-302), and Flamethrower (V2-301). No default size is assigned. The Flamethrower currently lists Blast and 8-inch range, but does not specify a template; flame shape/width and a flame attack procedure remain unresolved. Smoke and persistent fire zones retain their existing 3-inch diameter.

The optional scatter-die/direction-randomizer suggestion remains for review: the Blast procedure explicitly has no scatter roll. This suggestion grants no scatter mechanic. See [[design-011 Weapon-specific templates]] for the remaining profile and hazard questions.
