## Recorded rulings

- Ordinary shooting/tests: d20 + stat + modifiers vs 15+. Natural 1 fails, natural 20 succeeds. Injury: d20 + weapon Damage - Armour + modifiers vs 15+, one die per hit. Each success removes one Wound; each failure adds one Stress. No one-Wound-per-attack cap. Hit survivors become Pinned; at zero Wounds become Downed. Pinned adds no Stress; surplus successful Injury dice do not become Stress.
- Melee and experimental Return Fire use highest-opposing-roll pools, not 15+ hit thresholds or one-for-one cancellation. Equal maxima cause no hits. Unanswered permits only the initiator to inflict hits. No automatic additional attack-back. Preserve each die's weapon identity.
- Dominant weapon contributes full dice; off-hand contributes one unless a skill overrides it. Successful Charge adds one dominant-weapon Attack Die. Goliath alone does not provide full off-hand dice.
- One Move and one Core Action. Dash = 2x MOVE, costing both actions unless overridden. Charge = MOVE + 1d6; Human Bullet = MOVE + 2d6. Failed Charge moves up to MOVE directly toward the target by a legal route, stops outside engagement range and ends the activation.
- Pinned permits only half-speed MOVE and Core-Action RECOVER. Recover removes Pinned, not Stress or a break condition. Recover then move normally, or move at half speed then Recover. Pinned prevents shooting, fighting, Ready, Dash and skill actions unless explicitly overridden. Permitted movement reactions use half speed. Retaining READY alone does not bypass these restrictions.
- Stress caps at 5, or 7 with Implode. Apply the rolling unit's Stress once to all its rolls, including Injury, except Break/Rally or explicit exceptions. No extra flat Shaken penalty. Transfers cannot exceed capacity; excess stays at its source.
- End-Phase Break at 2+ Stress: d20 + NRV + non-Stress modifiers vs 15+, without subtracting Stress. Pass clears Stress; fail retains it and assigns BROKEN at 1–2 Stress, BOLTING at 3–4, BUGGING OUT at 5+. Severity uses Stress, not the die total. Apply behaviour next activation.
- Broken applies Pinned when its activation starts. Bolt spends both actions Dashing safely toward the nearest reachable heavy cover outside every enemy's LOS. Bugging Out spends both actions Dashing to the nearest edge by the shortest route, including hazards and their required tests. Exiting is not Out of Action. Forced flight overrides Pinned's action and speed restrictions.
- Existing break conditions Rally instead: the same Stress-free NRV test. Pass clears the condition and Stress; failure retains both and repeats the existing behaviour, without selecting a different condition. One regular morale test per End Phase; a new Break failure never grants an immediate Rally. Explicit skill-forced tests are exceptions.
- Caps remain skills ±4, weapons ±3, stats ±6, armour ±6. Cap positive and negative contributions separately per source, then combine. Printed Damage is outside the weapon cap; skill-granted Damage remains skill-source. Caps affect roll adjustments, not dice/inches/Wounds. Stress and terrain are separate sources. No global total cap.
- Hidden -4 stacks with cover. Hidden plus -2 cover gives -6; targeting restrictions still apply. Use the starting Hidden state for the entire attack pool, then reveal on any hit.
- READY: maximum one token; spend on reaction declaration. Start-trigger reactions resolve first; pinning/downing cancels the declared action and spends its cost. End-trigger reactions follow the action. Do not retroactively cancel participation in a simultaneous exchange. Reactions cannot trigger reactions without explicit permission.
- Rage Stress occurs after the attack resolves. Implode replaces an eligible End-Phase Break test, clears Stress and supplies up to +4 melee hit/Injury during the next activation. It cannot replace Rally. Beta Blocker halves penalties (round up), not Stress points or severity.

## Approved test variant: Return Fire
The READY target of a ranged attack may spend READY for a legal shot back. Resolve both opposing DEX attack pools against the other side's highest total. Each exceeding die hits; equal maxima cause no hits. Resolve each shot only once. Reaction shooting against other actions stays at 15+. Compare with ordinary reactions firing first. Cover Me extending this to protect an ally is only a suggestion.

## Working rulings — explicit choices for this pass

1. **Frequency:** 'once per turn' and 'end of turn' mean per round/end of round unless explicitly tied to an activation. Reset at round start; bonus actions/reactions do not refresh limits. Implode persists into the next activation despite the intervening round boundary.
2. **End Phase:** snapshot units already carrying break conditions. Each non-Downed, on-board unit gets at most one regular Break/Rally/Implode resolution. Skill-forced Break at zero Stress uses Broken on failure. If already carrying a condition, a forced Break failure retains it; success clears Stress but Rally is still required to clear the condition.
3. **Rally and recovery:** Rally success also removes Pinned. Stress removal alone never clears a break condition; Recover and On Your Feet clear Pinned only. Broken reapplies Pinned at the next activation unless rallied. A break condition blocks voluntary reactions/bonus actions; Bolt/Bugging Out consume the activation, including any granted extras.
4. **Friendly help:** Core Action, friendly within 3 inches and LOS. Recover removes that unit's Pinned without a test. Rally tests helper's NRV vs 15+ with non-Stress modifiers; subtract neither unit's Stress. Skills override range/count; Rally skill uses one Stress-free test for up to three targets carrying any break condition. Other NRV activation tests still suffer the user's Stress.
5. **Bolt fallback:** seek safe heavy cover outside LOS, otherwise reachable ground outside LOS, otherwise nearest reachable heavy cover. If none exists, stay and spend both actions. Stop when the destination is reached. Bugging Out can risk hazards but cannot cross impassable walls. Tied routes use shortest distance, then a stable declared tie-break. Pinned remains until recovered/rallied; forced flight overrides it rather than erasing it. Test flight from unengaged positions until disengagement rules are supplied.
6. **Stress on distance:** subtract once from a Charge-distance total, not once per d6; floor at zero. Fixed MOVE/Dash distances are not rolls. Apply Stress to unit terrain tests, not shared scenario randomness. This literal reading of 'all rolls' is a working choice to review.
7. **Opposed naturals:** natural 1s neither hit nor defend. Natural 20s outrank non-20s; both sides having a 20 ties the exchange. If only one side rolls 20s, its 20s hit; its other dice still compare modified totals against the opposing maximum. Against an empty/all-1 pool, every non-1 die hits. Ordinary single opposed tests give ties to the defender. Report a modified-totals-only alternative separately if this choice drives a finding.
8. **Pool snapshots:** declare weapons, target/dice allocations and optional bonuses before rolling. Snapshot modifiers for the complete attack, including Injury; apply its outcomes together afterward. New bonus attacks take fresh snapshots. Choose Rage before hits, gain its Stress even on a miss, and limit the chosen amount to remaining Stress capacity.
9. **Trigger order:** determine/apply the original attack outcomes, then Rage Stress, then after-attack/Down effects such as Blowing Off Steam. Last Laugh occurs immediately before its owner's Downing, cannot undo the original pool, and explicitly bypasses that pending Down/Pinned prohibition. Defensive counterattacks precede the original attacker's movement-and-attack follow-ups. Same-owner effects use a declared order; opposing same-priority effects use active-player first.
10. **Finite chains:** Parry requires zero incoming hits and an eligible unit; once per round, never from another Parry or Last Laugh. Its kill can trigger Blade Fury. Blade Fury allows its two printed follow-ups at most per round. Rampage follows only the original Charge attack, not itself; its follow-up gets no new charge die. Choose one movement/follow-up ability when two share a kill. These are explicit working limits, not claims about unchanged printed wording.
11. **Multiple reactions:** declare reactors/order and commit READY before rolling, then resolve sequentially and recheck legality. A committed reaction with no remaining legal target is lost. Isolate Return Fire tests to the targeted reactor. Reaction Charge spends READY alone and includes its free exchange; evaluate it separately from ordinary shooting reactions.
12. **Terrain fixtures:** open 0, light cover -1, heavy cover -2 to hit; do not combine light/heavy. Hidden adds -4. Default MOVE6 is a test profile, not a universal stat. Charge requires LOS but no separate 12-inch declaration ceiling for this pass. Use legal non-hazardous movement paths unless the terrain test is fully defined. Hidden/Ambush tests may start in a declared legal Hidden state; that does not test Hide acquisition or climbing.
13. **Marked and auras:** use selected Mark Target text: +2 shooting and extended Hidden targeting, without removing Hidden. Identical auras do not stack; distinct effects combine subject to caps. Friendly auras include their source unless they say 'other'; no LOS requirement; measure base-to-base. Big Aura allows two distinct auras, otherwise one. Failed activation keeps existing auras; Downed removes them. Passive auras persist during break conditions, but no new activation is allowed. Keep printed costs/tests; alternative costs are separate variants.
14. **Come On Then:** lasts until the source's next activation; restricts attacks only when the source is a legal target. It does not compel an enemy to attack. Record opportunities and AI choices separately from effectiveness.

## Boundaries
