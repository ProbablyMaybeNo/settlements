# Draft 2 — Simulation Assumptions

These are not automatically final rules. No simulations are run by this drafting migration, and no external test-agent status is asserted.

## Approved test variant: Return Fire
The READY target of a ranged attack may spend READY for a legal shot back. Resolve both opposing DEX attack pools against the other side's highest total. Each exceeding die hits; equal maxima cause no hits. Resolve each shot only once. Reaction shooting against other actions stays at 15+. Compare with ordinary reactions firing first. Cover Me extending this to protect an ally is only a suggestion.

## Provisional implementation choices from the handoff
- Opposed natural 1s cannot hit/defend; natural 20s beat non-20s; both sides rolling 20 ties the exchange. Test sensitivity; this hierarchy is not locked.
- Printed Damage separate from weapon modifier caps; cap positive and negative source contributions separately. Caps apply to roll modifiers, not dice/inches/Wounds.
- Once per turn means once per round for tests.
- For short test sequences, Pinned prevents movement/reactions until a Core Action recovers; do not retroactively cancel the exchange that inflicted it.
- Explicit skill chains retain their printed limits. No invented unlimited recursion.
- Fixed loadouts/stat profiles can be analytical fixtures, not newly created catalogue entries. Test off-hand weapons with more than one die for Gunslinger/Ambidextrous.

## Deliberately outside the first suite
Campaign, acquisition, final prices and full-battle AI. Unsupported skill mechanics are reported as not evaluated, not zero value. Old d10 or cancellation-engine results do not establish current balance.
