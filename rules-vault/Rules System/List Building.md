# 05 · List Building
> **S3 Battle Layer** · status **Drafted** · build order **15**

**Depends on:** [[Unit Design]]
**Feeds into:** [[Factions]]
**Raw dependency (from Notion):** Unit Design

## Focus
How players assemble a legal crew — roster rules, costs, and force composition.

The Rules column should nail down:
- Roster structure: 1 leader + elite fighters + the non-elites those choices unlock; min/max crew size.
- The costing method (points? threat rating? scenario-defined?) and what gets costed (units, weapons, gear).
- Faction selection at list level and how each faction's buffs AND nerfs apply.
- How a persistent campaign roster differs from a one-off pickup list.
- Restrictions/keywords that keep lists legal and on-theme.

## Working rules / decisions

### Points budget
A crew is built to a **points budget** set by the scenario. Cost prices every power axis, not just stats:

> **Unit cost = Rank base + stat points + wargear/weapons above the free basic loadout + extra skills.**

### Fielding caps (honour "no heroes")
- **1 Leader** and up to **2 Specialists** per crew; the rest are Recruits ([[Unit Design]]).
- No single model may exceed roughly **⅓ of the budget** (anti-hero cap).
- Optional: a **minimum crew size**, so you can't field three monsters.

> [!question] First-pass costing — the exact values (rank base cost, cost per stat point, weapon/skill costs, the anti-hero %) are all playtest dials. Lock the **caps** first, then tune the **numbers** against real games.
