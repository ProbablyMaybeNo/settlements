## 🧠 Unsorted (type freely here)

- Include a fear/terror mechanism where units can "break" and become frozen in place unable to move or act until a crew member "snaps them out of it" or the controlling player rolls a certain hard to hit dice roll. "Break" is different to "Flee" or "bolt" where a unit will try to run off the board. "Break" occurs when a unit witnesses enough trauma in a single turn. Trauma can include witnessing a friendly get shot. They get shot from a out of LOS enemy. An enemy with the "Fear" trait finished a turn in their LOS or they get targeted by an enemy. Something like that. 
  
  I'd like to include more of an emphasis on morale and mental health in the game. We could include a set of "fear/terror" related affects, Break, Bolt, Freak, Fight.
  
  Break - Become frozen in the spot, must pass a specific test or base 2 base with friendly to "Snap out of it"
  
  Bolt - Heads for the nearest board edge, rolls each turn to see if they rally.
  
  Freak - Loses control and starts to attack the nearest enemy/friendly unit either in melee/shooting depending on what weapon they have.
  
  Fight - Pushed too far the unit becomes enraged and begins to move directly toward the nearest enemy in LOS ignoring cover they take the quickest most direct route no matter what.
  
  ---
  
  It would be cool to include hacking mechanics which allow players to hack into terminals and take control of the terrain, attacking opponent with turrets, setting off alarms, triggering traps, smoke, closing/locking doors. Players determine what they want to do before hacking, they select a terrain feature to trigger, then they measure the distance from their terminal, 3 12" range bands, closer is easier/farther is harder. Then they roll + skill - Difficulty. If they succeed they can trigger 1 feature in the chosen terrain.
  
  If the enemy has a hacker who is also at a terminal and within the 3 range bands they can "react" to the hack by trying to divert the hack to a different terrain, lock the hacker out by freezing their terminal for a turn, or "overload" and destroy the opponents terminal while causing the enemy to suffer a negative condition

Lets make hacking and using terrain a bigger mechanic. Maybe we say that in order to take control of the terrain a player has to hack into it. Each board set up has a pre-determined number of terminals. A player has to "hack" a terminal in order to use it to take control of a piece of terrain. BUILDER type units can build additional hacking terminals and they can build connections from an existing terminal to other pieces of terrain which then allow the players to "Control" that terrain without needing a hacker. 

This is just a thought, it's a big mechanic but it could be fun and a great way to differentiate the game.

Having a lower percentage chance to hit is fine due to the units being civilians not special forces. The average basic unit starts with 1 Dexterity which = 10% chance of hitting a unit with heavy armor in heavy cover...if my calculations are correct a super skilled shooting unit with dexterity 5 can hit a heavily armored dude in heavy cover on a 50% chance...that seems about right to me. We'll obviously include other ways to modifier a units dexterity positively turn an average joes chance of hitting a super protexted unit to 20, 30, even 40% and a unit with great dex already up to 80%. This seems like a fair and sensible stats system for a game that suggest it is "realistic, bloody, and brutal"
