# Pinned movement and hit modifiers

## Confirmed by Ross

Each fighter has one Move Action for movement and one Core Action for another task. Ordinary self-Recover spends Move to stand up instead of travelling; an unused Core remains available for shooting or another legal action. Against a Pinned target, ranged hit rolls suffer −2 and melee hit rolls gain +2.

Players choose when to use their fighters. Recovery should be assessed with that choice in mind: wait while other fighters act, exhaust nearby enemy actions/reactions, change LOS, or exploit an exposed target. Remaining Pinned can be useful under fire while making the fighter more vulnerable in melee.

## Integration in the working manuscript

[[Settlements Rules System Version 2]] applies the target modifier to hit rolls only. Melee adds +2 to the pool totals before comparing the opposing maximum. Ranged Blast hit rolls take −2 against Pinned; automatic hazards have no hit roll. Applicable cover/rear modifiers still apply. Standing ends both Pinned modifiers and counts as movement for Aim/Unwieldy. Neither Core nor spent Move is refreshed. Half-speed crawling remains an alternative Move that leaves the fighter Pinned. Existing friendly Help Recover, Rally and printed assistance effects remain distinct procedures.

Individual actions still alternate. Standing and a later Core shot are separate chosen turns under that timing, with ordinary reaction windows. This revision grants no immunity after standing.

## Collateral proposals, not additional user rulings

| Item | Revised proposal | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aim | Core; +2 next Shoot through end of next round, lost on movement/standing, Pinned or engagement | Preserve preparation without spending Move on a non-movement task; permit a later shot to benefit. |
| Mark Target | Core, same test and round-long mark | Marking is a Core task. |
| Speed Loader | Core Reload readies up to two held Reload weapons | Improve Reload without converting Move. |
| Smart | Once per round reroll a failed physical Interact/Work test; Core still spent | Improve interaction without another Core. |
| Motivate | Core NRV; nearby ally travels +2 inches on its next normal Move this round | Support an ally's movement without creating another Core or refreshing actions. |
| Drone movement | Operator Core moves drone; operating through it needs a separate Core | Operator's Move remains for its own movement. |
| Armour Loot / improvised tasks | Armour uses Core Loot; Both improvised actions include the fighter's movement | Remove non-movement tasks charged to Move. |

These replacement effects require review and playtesting, particularly Aim's preparation cost, Reload usefulness, the revised skills and drone travel/operation cost. Prices are unchanged pending evidence. No tabletop or simulation validation is implied.
