# Equipment consolidation for battle testing

Ross authorised consolidating his completed equipment design notes into live owners, preserving feedback and approval records, then mirroring the vault. Source: [[Settlements/Design Workshop/Battle Catalogue Review/Equipment Category Review]].

Implemented the explicit tool refinements, repair-pack replacement, automatic teammate medkit revival, smoke Scatter, Molotov On Fire replacement and Unconscious model removal. Removed the superseded safety harness, trauma dressings, portable ladder and field supply case from active catalogue membership. Held the tentatively removed extinguisher outside the first test. Shields belong to Equipment; Armour remains separate. The folding barricade is ordinary Equipment, not a skill-gated deployable.

Added the supported utility vest, breaching charge, flashbang, tear-gas grenade and Juice profiles. Recorded the harness, shields and unanswered additions without approving their alternatives. The shared equipment-conditions owner consolidates On Fire, Poisoned and Shocked/Stunned; fire/poison timing remains Partial. No Blinded effect, poison source, weapon redesign, new campaign subsystem or price is invented.

The original component approval records, IDs and anchors remain. Existing item approvals are not promoted. Stimulant and Anti-anxiety dose retain their effects and semantic dates. Catalogue rows are regenerated using Settlements Rule Workspace’s existing renderer from item Rule text.

See [[Settlements/Design Workshop/Battle Catalogue Review/Equipment Consolidation - Battle Test]] for current owning profiles and the five essential decisions. This is drafting and source reconciliation, not balance or playtest validation.
