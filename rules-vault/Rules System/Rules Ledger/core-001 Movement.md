# core-001 · Movement
> **core** · status **draft** · v0.1

**Parent phase:** [[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Archived/Movement]]

## Text
- **Move slot:** up to **MOV"** (baseline 6"). Cannot split around the Action.
- **Sprint** (both slots): up to **2× MOV"** — nothing else.
- **Charge** (both slots): declare a target in LOS; roll **MOVE + 1d6** for charge distance. Reach within 1 inch for a free melee exchange with **+1 Attack Die on the dominant weapon**. See [[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Archived/Melee]] for skills and resolution.
- **Low leap:** obstacle under **2"** tall — no test, costs **2"** Move.
- **Athletic traversal** (climb, gap/jump, vault ≥2", swim): Move-slot **AGI** test vs **7+**. Fail = stop / fall short / hazard (see [[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Archived/Movement#Terrain movement]]).
- **Difficult** ground costs double Move ([[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Archived/Terrain]]). **Impassable** blocks entry unless Climbable / Breached.
- **Disengage** (Move slot): move up to MOV"; you **keep your Action** (no Charge); cannot end within 1" of any enemy; each previous Engager gets a free swing at **−2**.
- Measuring: pre-measure allowed; base edge to base edge; round down ([[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Archived/Rules Engine]]).
