# Rulebook outline and objective direction

Ross supplied a thirteen-chapter outline and then clarified its learning sequence and scope. [[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Second Draft/Settlements Rulebook Outline - Review and Proposed Structure]] contains the updated hierarchy and source mapping. These directions supersede the earlier outline recommendations where they differ.

## Confirmed reading structure

- Introduction: creator's brief why/what/how; setting/lore/backstory; what players need; hints at persistence, regional map and app; finish with pared-down tenets and the intended experience.
- Core Mechanics: unit card, tests, tokens, terminology and the basics.
- Playing the Game: one concise overview showing how every aspect fits together before detailed rules.
- Fighting Battles begins with Battle Overview. Its subheadings house the shared activation/action, READY/reaction, visibility/cover/stealth and ordinary interaction/objective groundwork.
- Detailed interactive terrain follows that groundwork. Injuries/Resolving Combat and End Phase cover immediate outcomes and scheduled checks respectively.

## Confirmed objective direction and scope

- No more than six objectives on the board. One to three objectives each use a six-inch diameter; four to six each use a three-inch diameter.
- The diameter is the complete control area. No additional control distance extends beyond it.
- Battles use primary and secondary objectives. Ross will add their specifics; leave placeholders rather than expanding mission types, generator tables, scoring, rewards or objective edge cases.
- Earlier mission examples were brainstorming. The resource-hunting idea remains available for Ross to develop; do not assign it to primary or secondary objectives as a settled decision.

## Battle aids

Ross confirms a battle cheat sheet plus the companion app. The book teaches and fully explains; the sheet supports running a battle; the app provides rules lookup and crew/settlement records as planned. Keep terms and procedures synchronized. Audit the existing battle reference after restructuring; do not claim the future app or revised cheat sheet is implemented by this note.

## Integration status

The directions are now integrated into 484 individual cards, thirteen chapter views and the reference. The pre-migration manuscript and annotated table are archived; all original IDs and Notes are retained. Eighteen earlier objective/scenario cards are marked Deferred and excluded from the assembled book. See [[design-010 Individual rule cards and section views]]. No new objective designs, reward values, faction benefits, app implementation or playtest results have been added.

The original outline's placement of initiative and fleeing has not been clarified as an explicit timing change. Keep that editorial distinction visible during migration; it does not block the agreed learning structure.
