# Rulebook outline and objective direction

Ross supplied a thirteen-chapter rulebook outline and asked for an assessment before fleshing it out. The supplied outline takes precedence over the current manuscript where it gives a definite direction. The structural proposal and source mapping are in [[Settlements Rulebook Outline - Review and Proposed Structure]].

## Confirmed user directions

- Present the book as section headers, subheaders and rule/mechanic text. Assess rearrangements and contradictions before the full drafting pass.
- No more than six objectives may be on the board. With one to three objectives, each has a six-inch diameter; with four to six, each has a three-inch diameter.
- The listed objective examples were brainstorming, not an adopted objective catalogue.
- Retain the idea of crews venturing out to hunt for something their settlement specifically needs. Whether these are primary or secondary objectives remains open.

## Recommendations awaiting review

Keep the thirteen-chapter order, add homes for omitted shared procedures, and assemble coherent mechanic notes into full-text section/book views. Use settlement needs mainly as crew secondary objectives, with resource-hunt primaries when appropriate. Use the objective discs as control areas with explicit physical interaction points; keep the chosen diameters fixed through the battle. The counting treatment of secondary sites and support terrain needs to be stated during integration.

The outline's End Phase mentions initiative and fleeing. These may describe intended timing changes or simply topics to cover. The review recommends retaining Start Phase initiative and start-of-own-turn voluntary withdrawal unless Ross intended otherwise. No timing change has been applied.

## Integration status

The objective cap/diameters are recorded user direction but are not yet integrated into the playing manuscript, quick reference, generator or annotated review table. Existing Control/Claim/Retrieve/Work wording needs the area/contact interpretation resolved. The outline review is complete; full rules migration has not started. No new reward values, faction benefits, app functions, simulation results or playtest claims have been added.
