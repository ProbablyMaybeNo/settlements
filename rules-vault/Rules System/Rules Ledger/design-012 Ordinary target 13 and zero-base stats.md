# Ordinary target 13 and zero-base stats

## Confirmed direction and rationale

Ross accepted **13+** as the ordinary success/hit target, with **zero as each stat's base before existing recruitment point allocation**. On an unmodified stat-0 test at Stress 0, rolls 13–20 succeed: **8/20 = 40%**. Target 14 would give 35%; the previous target 15 gave 30%. A total bonus of +10 at Stress 0 gives 90% against 13+, which Ross considered reasonable; this does not authorize a change to bonus limits.

[[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-015 - Reading a fighter profile|V2-015 Five stats]] keeps its 0–6 range. [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-029 - Recruitment and training grades|V2-029 Recruitment allocation]] already starts all five stats at zero before the existing allocation. Neither needs a semantic edit or a new rule-update date.

## Integration

Actual rule owners changed and dated **2026-10-22 (America/Los_Angeles)**:

- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-017 - Ordinary tests and modifiers|V2-017 Ordinary test]]: ordinary total 13+ succeeds.
- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-065 - Reactions and interruption|V2-068 Return Fire]]: independent simultaneous normal 13+ shooting tests; timing and both outcomes remain unchanged.
- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-097 - Shooting and target allocation|V2-097 Shoot]]: each ordinary ranged attack total 13+ hits.
- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-139 - Stress, Break and forced flight|V2-142 End Phase Break]]: ordinary NRV test at 13+, still ignoring Stress and keeping existing trigger/outcome bands. Rally and other normal stat tests inherit the shared ordinary target without inventing a new exception.

Summaries and corresponding review/index descriptions/dates follow changed owners. Opposed explanations and their summary labels use number-free wording as housekeeping, without advancing dates. Opposed single tests and melee still compare highest totals using their existing natural-result and tie rules; no ordinary success threshold is added. Direct quick-reference formulas and the shooting boundary example follow target 13. Historical design audits, earlier dated decisions and simulation contracts remain intact and are superseded only for the ordinary target.

## Preserved scope and unresolved threshold

[[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-114 - Injury and attack outcomes|V2-114 Injury roll]] independently specifies **d20 + Damage − Armour + injury modifiers vs 15+**; V2-021 explicitly gives Injury its own formula. That threshold and its worked example remain unchanged. No separate Injury change was confirmed; flag the question for Ross rather than infer it from an ordinary stat-test decision. Injury outcomes/bands, Wounds, Damage and Armour are unchanged.

Keep natural 1 failure/natural 20 success, Stress penalties and exemptions, source modifier caps/stacking, recruitment budgets/starting caps, prices, distances, table results and all unrelated numbers unchanged. No engine implementation, simulation, playtest or claim of proven balance. The confirmed template revision and separate target-only counterfire test variant are preserved.
