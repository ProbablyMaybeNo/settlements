# core-002 · Shooting
> **core** · status **draft** · v0.1

**Parent phase:** [[Shooting]]

## Text
1. Declare a target in **range** and **line of sight**.
2. **Attack roll:** `1d10 + DEX + mods` vs **7+**.
3. On a hit → **Injury roll** ([[core-007 Casualties]] / [[Damage]]).
4. Hit *or* miss → target gains **+1 Stress** ([[Morale]]).

**Modifiers:** Cover Light −1 · Heavy −2 · Hidden −3. Out of range = illegal. Weapons rarely add to hit ([[Weapons]]).

**Engaged:** most ranged weapons cannot fire in base contact. **Sidearm** may fire at the Engaged enemy only.

*Graduated from [[Shooting]]. See [[Rules System MOC]].*
