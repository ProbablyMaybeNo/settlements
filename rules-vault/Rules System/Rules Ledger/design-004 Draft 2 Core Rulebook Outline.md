# Draft 2 Core Rulebook Outline

[[Archive/Settlements/2026-10-24 - Retired organization/Rules System/Second Draft/Settlements Core Rulebook Outline]] organises the proposed core book into getting started, crew and equipment, battle procedures, settlements and campaigns, and reference material with a reserved drone module.

Each chapter and subsection has a short content description, keywords and an editorial status. Only active Draft 2 supports gameplay statuses. Research books guide presentation; historical rules do not fill gaps. The outline introduces no adopted mechanics and does not confirm complete playability or balance.

Review and source-conflict follow-ups are recorded in [[Settlements/Rules System/Rules Ledger/Draft 2 - Development decisions and history]].
