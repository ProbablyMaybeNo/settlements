# Weapon-specific templates

## Confirmed direction

Ross confirmed a **3-inch-diameter circle**, a **5-inch-diameter circle**, and an **8-inch flame template**. Which template applies is **based on the weapon**. Physical template ownership, making or buying remains optional.

Canonical edits belong to [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-006 - Template sizes — reconcile|V2-006 Template sizes — reconcile]] and [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-248 - Blast attack|V2-248 Blast attack]], dated 2026-10-22 in Pacific time. Stable IDs, anchors, embeds and user Notes are retained. Index descriptions/dates, the quick reference and current editorial discrepancy notices follow those owners.

## Unresolved assignments and adjacent contradictions

- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-259 - Frag grenade|Frag grenade]]: Blast profile has no circle-size assignment.
- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-260 - Fire bottle|Fire bottle]]: initial Blast has no circle-size assignment. Its persistent fire zone remains 3 inches in diameter under V2-252; that does not assign the initial attack's template.
- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-300 - Grenade launcher|Grenade launcher]] and [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-302 - Rocket launcher|Rocket launcher]]: Blast profiles have no circle-size assignment.
- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-301 - Flamethrower|Flamethrower]]: currently lists Blast and 8-inch range, not a template assignment. Range alone does not establish use of the flame template. Template assignment, flame width/shape and any distinct flame attack procedure remain unresolved.
- Custom Blast construction has no template-size assignment rule. No default circle, choice permission or new pricing rule is supplied.
- [[Settlements/Rules System/Second Draft/Rule Cards/V2-212 - Blast mine|Blast mine]] explicitly uses a separate 2-inch-radius Crush hazard, not the shared ranged Blast procedure. Its 2-inch trigger, hazard footprint and AGI/Damage 3 resolution remain unchanged. Whether that footprint should reconcile with the physical template set is unresolved; no 3-inch or 5-inch assignment is inferred.
- The optional scatter-die/direction-randomizer suggestion remains in Components. It is not reconciled as a scatter mechanic: V2-248 still explicitly has no scatter roll.

## Preserved scope

No change to Dice and markers, smoke's 3-inch diameter, persistent fire's 3-inch diameter, LOS, vertical limits, walls/floors, cover, modifiers, hit/Injury/device procedure or no-scatter resolution. Profiles are inspected and recorded above, not assigned or redated. The earlier October 18 manuscript-check record remains historical. No balance test or tabletop acceptance is claimed.
