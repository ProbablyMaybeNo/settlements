# Chems and equipment follow-up

Ross approved the harness ignoring Unwieldy and the movement reduction when carrying. Other carrying requirements remain.

Fire: unmodified d6 each End Phase, beginning in the application round. Failed checks of 1–3 escalate to Pinned, Down and Unconscious; success extinguishes the fire. Preserve the earlier movement and missed-activation effects. Poison: −2 MOVE immediately, then each End Phase after its application round. Antidote removes Poisoned and restores MOVE lost to it.

Ross replaced +2/next-test chems with six +4, battle-long chem types for STR, DEX, AGI, INT, NRV and MOVE. Wounds are excluded. The extra Pinned/Stress Stimulant is superseded; Anti-anxiety dose supplies NRV.

Any chem used in each of two consecutive battles makes the unit Addicted for the crew’s next battle. Withdrawal is −2 to the six non-Wound stats. Any chem suppresses that penalty for the rest of the battle and applies normally. Addicted expires after exactly that battle, including when the unit sits out. Continued qualifying use can trigger it again. This later explicit request overrides the previous addiction deferral. Using stat penalties preserves the intended MOVE penalty without changing Injury rolls or flat d6 fire checks.

Shields remain undecided. Ross asked for advice; omission from the first battle test is a recommendation, not approval to remove them or create a new effect.
