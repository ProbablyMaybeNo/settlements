# Injury and attack outcomes

## Summary

Each hit gives the target 1 Stress before Injury is resolved.

## Rule text

Each hit gives the target 1 Stress before Injury is resolved.

^text-s035-b000

For each hit, roll **1d20 + Damage − Armour + applicable Injury modifiers**. A total of **13+ removes one Wound**. A natural 1 fails. A natural 20 succeeds. Stress does not modify Injury, and Armour gives no separate saving roll.

^text-s035-b001

Resolve the attack's Injury dice together. A unit that loses a Wound becomes Pinned if it has Wounds remaining, or Down at zero. A hit causing no Wound adds Stress only, unless a specific rule says otherwise.

^text-s035-b002

Injury rolls add no further Stress. Extra successes do not execute a casualty or cause extra lasting injuries. Keep Stress on Down and Unconscious units, but ignore it while they are in those states.
