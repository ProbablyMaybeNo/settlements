Transfer Stress inflicted on other friendlies within it to you, up to your capacity; excess stays with its original recipient. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-346 - Commanding Presence\|Commanding Presence]] | General | Passive | Once per round, a friendly within 8 inches and line of sight may use your NRV for a Break test. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-540 - Beta Blocker\|Beta Blocker]] | General | Passive | Halve Stress penalties, rounding up. Break tests still use full Stress. |
