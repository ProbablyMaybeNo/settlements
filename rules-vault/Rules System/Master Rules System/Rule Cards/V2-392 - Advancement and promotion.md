# Advancement and promotion

## Summary

During the Settlement Phase, spend 10 shared XP to give a chosen unit +1 Stat within its rank limit. You may buy several advances. Record the total XP spent advancing each unit.

## Rule text

During the Settlement Phase, spend 10 shared XP to give a chosen unit +1 Stat within its rank limit. You may buy several advances. Record the total XP spent advancing each unit.

^text-s083-b000

When a Stat reaches 2, 4 or 6, choose one associated skill without paying more XP. Count each Stat separately.

^text-s083-b011

Every 30 XP spent advancing a unit promotes it one rank: Recruit → Fighter → Specialist → Veteran. Promotion costs no extra XP and grants no free Stat points. Veteran is the highest rank.

^text-s083-b012

The individual Stat maximum is 3 for a Recruit, 4 for a Fighter, 5 for a Specialist and 6 for a Veteran. MOVE normally remains 6. Normal Wound limits remain 3, or 4 for a crew leader; promotion does not itself grant Wounds.

^text-s084-b000
