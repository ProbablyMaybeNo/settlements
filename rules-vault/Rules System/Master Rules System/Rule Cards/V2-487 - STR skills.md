| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-308 - Grapple\|Grapple]] | Grappler | Core | Spend Core and make an opposed STR test against an enemy within 1 inch. Success makes it Grappled: it cannot act or react, stays within 1 inch, and gets a free opposed STR escape test at activation start. While Grappling, move at half speed and use one-handed weapons. Ranged attacks targeting you hit the captive instead. For an enemy attack through either model at another friendly, roll one d6 for the whole attack: 1–3 redirects it to the captive; 4+ keeps its target. During your activation, freely release the captive 3 inches directly away. Stop at collision: another unit Pins both; a structure Pins the captive. Resolve falls normally. Pinned, Down, Unconscious or a morale condition ends your hold in place. Throw uses its own release procedure. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-309 - Hauler\|Hauler]] | Grappler | Passive | Ignore movement reductions while Grappling or Carrying a unit or structure. While Grappling, you may use non-Heavy two-handed weapons. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-310 - Throw\|Throw]] | Grappler | Core | While Carrying, or using a Grappled enemy, spend Core and test STR. Failure retains the load or hold. Success ends it and throws the model 6 inches in a straight line away from you. A thrown unit stops on another unit and Pins both; hitting Heavy terrain causes a Damage 4 Injury roll. A thrown structure pushes contacted units for its remaining distance and causes a Damage 4 Injury roll against each. Resolve falls and normal solid-terrain stops. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-311 - Brace\|Brace]] | Tank | Core | Spend Core to Brace until the start of your next activation. Incoming attacks suffer −4 to hit. While Bracing, you cannot move, act or react. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-516 - Come On Then\|Come On Then]] | Tank | Core | Spend Core and test STR. On success, enemies within 6 inches must target you with their Attack actions until the start of your next activation. Normal targeting requirements still apply. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-517 - Tough as Nails\|Tough as Nails]] | Tank | Triggered | Once per round, when attacked in melee without being wounded, ignore the hit’s effects. Subsequent attacks against you suffer −4 to Injury rolls until the start of your next activation. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-518 - Rampage\|Rampage]] | Charger | Triggered | After Downing an enemy with a Charge attack, move the remaining Charge distance. If you reach another enemy within 1 inch, make a free melee attack against it. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-312 - Bull\|Bull]] | Charger | Charge modifier | When declaring a Charge, test STR. Success lets you pass through non-LOS-blocking scatter, obstacles and barricades. Contacted enemies test AGI; on failure, push them 2 inches in your chosen direction and Pin them. Reinforced doors, buildings and LOS-blocking structures still stop you. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-519 - Human Bullet\|Human Bullet]] | Charger | Passive | Use MOVE + 2d6 for Charge distance instead of MOVE + d6. Declare a target within MOVE + 12 inches. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-520 - Goliath\|Goliath]] | General | Passive | Wield two two-handed melee weapons together as one-handed weapons. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-314 - Tough\|Tough]] | General | Passive | Gain +1 Wound, up to your Wound limit: 3, or 4 for a crew leader. |
| [[Settlements/Rules System/Master Rules System/Item Cards/Skill Cards/V2-315 - Big Fella\|Big Fella]] | General | Passive | Ignore Unwieldy on your weapons. |
