# Battle rewards

## Summary

The winner receives 250 Credits. The loser receives 125 Credits. In a draw, each crew receives 125 Credits.

## Rule text

The winner receives 250 Credits. The loser receives 125 Credits. In a draw, each crew receives 125 Credits.

^text-s081-b000

There is no generic Materials reward. Keep legitimately earned loot, captives and battle rewards. Credits may be used to buy Materials under the eventual campaign purchasing rules.

^text-s081-b001

Withdrawal counts as a loss, retains earned XP and loot, and grants no scenario or mission XP unless the scenario says otherwise. Use Ending a battle for the physical withdrawal procedure.
