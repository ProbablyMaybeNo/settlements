# Ventilation fans

## Summary

Declare the outlet and its smoke or irritant-gas mode at setup. It may clear one linked smoke/gas template immediately or switch off. A smoke outlet maintains a 3-inch-diameter smoke zone while on. An irritant-gas outlet maintains a 3-inch-diameter Difficult zone: entering or starting an action inside gives a human 1 Stress, once per round. Respirators prevent that Stress. Turning the outlet off or using its valve clears the zone. Fans cannot clear fire.
