# Grapple

## Summary

Spend Core and make an opposed STR test against an enemy within 1 inch. Success makes it Grappled: it cannot act or react, stays within 1 inch, and gets a free opposed STR escape test at activation start. While Grappling, move at half speed and use one-handed weapons. Ranged attacks targeting you hit the captive instead. For an enemy attack through either model at another friendly, roll one d6 for the whole attack: 1–3 redirects it to the captive; 4+ keeps its target. During your activation, freely release the captive 3 inches directly away. Stop at collision: another unit Pins both; a structure Pins the captive. Resolve falls normally. Pinned, Down, Unconscious or a morale condition ends your hold in place. Throw uses its own release procedure.
