# Bomber drone

## Summary

Before battle, equip two payloads in any combination of Frag, Smoke, Flashbang or Tear-gas grenades. Core Jettison: release one payload at a point within 1 inch, rolling d20 +3 against 13+. On failure scatter it 1 inch randomly; otherwise place it at the chosen point. Resolve that grenade’s effects. When enemy shooting destroys the drone, it may automatically release one remaining payload directly beneath itself before removal. Losing control does not trigger emergency release.
