# Cloaking field

## Summary

Create a 5-inch-diameter field around its marker. Friendlies moving through it cannot be targeted by ALERT reactions, including sentry turrets and seeker mines. It lasts until destroyed, disarmed or replaced by another field deployable from the same unit.
