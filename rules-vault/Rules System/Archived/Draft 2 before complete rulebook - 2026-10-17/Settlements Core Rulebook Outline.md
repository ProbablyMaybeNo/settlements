# Settlements Core Rulebook Outline

An outline for a single book containing the two-player battle rules, crew creation, settlements and campaign play. The sequence introduces the terms players need, walks through a battle, then explains what carries forward between games. The undeveloped drone module is reserved separately at the end.

The headings below are proposed rulebook headings. Descriptions outline the content to include; they do not adopt new mechanics. **Keywords** identify terms to explain and index, including terms awaiting a definition. Status labels are editorial annotations to remove from the published book.

## Status key

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | The active draft records the described rule or selected direction. This does not mean balanced, tested at the table, or complete in every interaction. |
| **Drafted** | Usable text or a selected framework exists, but the section still needs completion, reconciliation or review. |
| **Pending** | No active rules exist for this section, or its scope still needs choosing. The description identifies what needs writing. Historical material is not a substitute. |
| **Blocked** | A specific unresolved decision, conflict or missing prerequisite prevents completing the described section. The description names it. |
| **Canceled** | A rule or approach has been explicitly removed or superseded. These entries appear in the editorial exclusions, not the proposed contents. |

Each chapter has an overall status; its subsections give the more precise assessment. A chapter marked Drafted can contain Confirmed procedures and Pending gaps.

**Authority:** [[Full Rules System v2]], [[Draft 2 - Skills]], [[Draft 2 - Basic Weapon System]], [[Draft 2 - Keywords]] and recorded decisions. [[Draft 2 - Simulation Assumptions]] distinguishes recorded rulings from working choices for tests. Neither old satellite notes nor simulation fixtures supply missing rules.

**Status: Drafted.** Give players a setup procedure that finishes with deployed crews ready to play.

### 7.1 Choose a Scenario and Battle Limit

**Status: Confirmed.** Explain that the scenario provides battlefield dimensions, deployment, objectives and special setup/end conditions. Establish the Crew Rating limit before selecting crews. Use the standard board and round count unless the scenario changes them.

**Keywords:** Scenario, Crew Rating, Battlefield, Deployment.

### 7.2 Place and Describe Terrain

**Status: Drafted.** Use scenario-guided key terrain, then player placement for the remainder. Describe movement, visibility and interaction properties in clear terrain profiles. Write the placement sequence, density and selection rules; the old nine-to-twelve-feature requirement is not adopted.

**Keywords:** Terrain, Obstacle, Structure, Cover, Line of Sight.

### 7.3 Place Interactive Features

**Status: Drafted.** Include additional meaningful interactive features beyond ordinary doors and windows. Explain token/template substitutes. Decide the mandatory number, selection procedure and feature states before publishing a universal setup checklist.

**Keywords:** Interactive Feature, Terminal, Crane, Elevator, Bridge, Pit.

### 7.4 Deploy Crews and Resolve Setup Abilities

**Status: Pending.** Define deployment order, zones, reserves and the timing of setup skills. Integrate Tunnels and Strategist with the eventual deployment and initiative procedures, including arrival timing and enemy reactions.

## 8 Rounds Activations and Actions

**Status: Drafted.** Establish the turn sequence and ordinary action choices.

### 8.1 The Round Sequence and Initiative

**Status: Pending.** Define how the first player is chosen, the order of phases, refreshes and what happens when one crew has no eligible activations left. Selected alternating activations and End-Phase morale must fit into that sequence; the old Priority die and underdog bonus are not defaults.

**Keywords:** Round, Initiative, Active Player, End Phase, Refresh.

### 8.2 Activating a Unit

**Status: Confirmed.** Players alternate unit activations. An activation supplies one Move and one Core Action; Dash and Charge use both unless an explicit ability changes that cost. A reaction is outside activation and does not refresh it.

**Keywords:** Activation, Move, Core Action, Dash, Charge, Reaction.

### 8.3 The Action Reference

**Status: Drafted.** Give each ordinary action a short entry identifying its cost, requirements and destination chapter: Move, Dash, Charge, Shoot, Fight, Ready, Recover, Rally, Interact, Hack, Deploy, Hide and Stabilize. Several names are already used, but not all have a complete shared procedure.

**Keywords:** Action Cost, Legal Target, Requirement, Effect.

### 8.4 Orders and Additional Actions

**Status: Pending.** Define Orders' availability, cost, range, recipients and limits, alongside skills that grant or convert actions. Distinguish a granted action from an extra activation and clarify repetition and recipient eligibility. Do not import the old rank Order counts.

**Keywords:** Order, Additional Action, Motivate, On Me, Activation Eligibility.

### 8.5 The End Phase

**Status: Drafted.** Gather Break, Rally, Implode, expiries and eventual scoring into one ordered checklist. The recorded morale outcomes exist; the complete order, simultaneous effects and turn/round terminology still need confirmation.

**Keywords:** End Phase, Break, Rally, Expiry, Scoring.

**Sources:** [[Full Rules System v2]] §§3, 10–11; [[Draft 2 - Skills]]; [[Draft 2 - Simulation Assumptions]] working rulings 1–4, 9.

## 9 Movement and Engagement

**Status: Drafted.** Explain movement on open and obstructed ground before resolving attacks.

### 9.1 Move and Dash

**Status: Confirmed.** Move uses the unit's MOVE value. Dash moves up to twice MOVE and normally spends both action slots; Sprinter is an explicit exception. Define detailed placement conventions elsewhere rather than hiding them in movement examples.

**Keywords:** MOVE, Move, Dash, Sprinter.

### 9.2 Charging an Enemy

**Status: Drafted.** Explain Charge's two-slot cost, MOVE plus d6 distance, engagement within one inch, free melee exchange and dominant-weapon charge die. Failed Charge moves toward the target up to MOVE by a legal route, stops outside engagement and ends activation. Declaration range and general path requirements remain unfinished.

**Keywords:** Charge, Charge Distance, Engagement, Failed Charge, Human Bullet.

### 9.3 Moving Through Terrain

**Status: Pending.** Define ordinary obstacles, difficult and impassable ground, doors, stairs, climbing, gaps and water. Explain any traversal tests and failed movement, while preserving explicit skill exceptions such as Acrobatic. The old movement table is historical.

**Keywords:** Obstacle, Climb, Jump, Traverse, Difficult Terrain, Acrobatic.

### 9.4 Height Falls and Forced Movement

**Status: Pending.** Define measurement between levels, safe landing and displacement, falling outcomes and forced movement into hazards or occupied spaces. Apply these common rules to Throw, Bull, Knock Back and interactive features without separate damage systems for each.

**Keywords:** Height, Fall, Forced Movement, Hazard, Collision.

### 9.5 Leaving and Moving Within Combat

**Status: Pending.** Define disengagement, movement while engaged and multi-enemy positioning. Ordinary movement cannot start engagement; explicit movement-and-fight skills can. Resolve exit attacks and placement restrictions without inheriting Draft 1's free-swing procedure.

**Keywords:** Engaged, Disengage, Separation, Blade Fury, Rampage.

### 9.6 Moving While Pinned

**Status: Drafted.** Explain half-speed Move and the choice to Recover before moving normally or move first at half speed. The keyword note adds that a moving Pinned unit can be targeted by ranged attacks or effects only when its base is visible; reconcile its scope with the general visibility rules.

**Keywords:** Pinned, Recover, Half Speed, Base Visibility.

**Sources:** [[Full Rules System v2]] §§4, 10; [[Draft 2 - Keywords]] PINNED / RECOVER; [[Draft 2 - Skills]].

## 10 Shooting

**Status: Drafted.** Present a single ranged-attack procedure with linked exceptions.

### 10.1 Choose a Target

**Status: Drafted.** Require weapon range and LOS unless explicitly overridden. Complete visibility, facing, cover qualification, shooting into combat and targeting Downed or moving Pinned units. Do not silently adopt the old 180-degree arc.

**Keywords:** Target, Range, LOS, Facing, Cover, Engaged, Downed.

### 10.2 Resolve a Ranged Attack

**Status: Confirmed.** Roll one d20 plus DEX and applicable modifiers against 15+ per Attack Die, then one Injury roll per successful hit. Explain misses, successful hits and weapon identity; natural 1 and 20 follow ordinary resolution.

**Keywords:** Shoot, DEX, Attack Dice, Hit, Injury.

### 10.3 Cover and Hidden Targets

**Status: Drafted.** Explain Hidden's recorded targeting restrictions and how its accuracy penalty stacks with applicable cover. Finalise cover values and eligibility; light −1 and heavy −2 are test fixtures rather than universally adopted terrain rules. Resolve MARKED and the new moving-Pinned base-visibility wording.

**Keywords:** Cover, Hidden, MARKED, Pinned, Visibility.

### 10.4 Dual Wielding and Split Fire

**Status: Drafted.** Use the dominant weapon's full pool and one off-hand die, retaining each weapon's traits, range and Damage. Gunslinger and split-fire skills provide their printed exceptions. Complete allocation, reroll scope and bonus-die chains.

**Keywords:** Dual Wielding, Dominant Weapon, Off Hand, Gunslinger, Split Fire.

### 10.5 Special Ranged Weapons

**Status: Blocked.** Explain thrown attacks, Blast and other delivery methods after their final profiles, affected targets and templates are defined. Cross-reference payloads and hazards rather than creating separate injury rules.

**Keywords:** Thrown, Blast, Template, Payload, Single Use.

**Sources:** [[Full Rules System v2]] §§5, 7, 9–10; [[Draft 2 - Basic Weapon System]]; [[Draft 2 - Keywords]]; [[Draft 2 - Simulation Assumptions]] working ruling 12.

## 11 Close Combat

**Status: Drafted.** Explain the shared exchange, then its skill and positioning exceptions.

### 11.1 Fighting an Engaged Enemy

**Status: Confirmed.** Already-engaged units spend a Core Action to Fight. A successful Charge grants an exchange with one extra dominant-weapon die. Ordinary movement cannot initiate engagement; explicit effects can.

**Keywords:** Fight, Engaged, Charge, Core Action.

### 11.2 The Melee Exchange

**Status: Confirmed.** Both units roll their weapon pools with STR or permitted AGI. Each die exceeding the opponent's highest modified total scores a hit; equal highest totals cause no hits. Resolve resulting Injury rolls without an automatic second attack-back. Include the master's short example.

**Keywords:** Melee Pool, Highest Total, Hit, Tie, STR, AGILE.

### 11.3 Two Weapons and Unanswered Attacks

**Status: Confirmed.** Preserve dominant/off-hand weapon identity and printed skill exceptions. UNANSWERED uses the same comparison but only the initiator may score hits; it does not grant a different dice engine.

**Keywords:** Dual Wielding, Ambidextrous, Goliath, UNANSWERED.

### 11.4 Counterattacks and Follow Up Attacks

**Status: Drafted.** Explain explicit Parry, Last Laugh, Blade Fury and Rampage triggers around the common exchange. Finalise limits, competing triggers and Ambush/Aerial Assault order; finite-chain choices in simulations still await design review.

**Keywords:** Parry, Last Laugh, Blade Fury, Rampage, Trigger Order.

### 11.5 Multiple Enemies and Grappling

**Status: Blocked.** Complete multiple-enemy engagement and the Grapple package: holding, release, separation, escape, dragging and Meat Shield targeting. Recorded skill fragments exist, but they do not establish a complete shared control procedure.

**Keywords:** Multiple Combat, GRAPPLED, Escape, Meat Shield, Throw.

### 11.6 Natural Results in Melee

**Status: Blocked.** Confirm natural 1/20 priority, all-failing and empty defence pools before publishing their outcomes. The test contract supplies explicit choices for simulation, not final general melee rules.

**Keywords:** Natural 1, Natural 20, Defence Pool, Tie.

**Sources:** [[Full Rules System v2]] §§4, 8; [[Draft 2 - Keywords]]; [[Draft 2 - Skills]]; [[Draft 2 - Simulation Assumptions]] working rulings 7–10.

## 12 Injury Conditions and Recovery

**Status: Drafted.** Put the shared consequences of ranged and melee hits in one chapter.

### 12.1 Injury Rolls and Wounds

**Status: Confirmed.** Roll d20 plus the hit's weapon Damage minus target Armour and applicable modifiers against 15+. Each success removes one Wound; each failure adds one Stress. Resolve the pool together; successful dice are not capped to one Wound per attack.

**Keywords:** Injury Roll, Damage, Armour, Wound, Stress.

### 12.2 Pinned and Recover

**Status: Confirmed.** A hit survivor becomes Pinned without extra Stress. Pinned allows only half-speed Move and Core-Action Recover unless explicitly overridden. Recover clears Pinned, not Stress or a break condition; forced flight has its recorded exception. Put the additional visibility wording under targeting and movement.

**Keywords:** Pinned, Recover, Core Action, Forced Flight.

### 12.3 Downed and Out of Action

**Status: Drafted.** At zero Wounds, a target becomes Downed in melee or shooting. Write its remaining activity, persistence, removal and the distinction from Out of Action. Surplus successes in the original attack do not automatically finish it.

**Keywords:** Downed, Out of Action, Wounds, Removal.

### 12.4 Stabilize and Treatment

**Status: Blocked.** Define the ordinary treatment action, requirements, restored Wounds/state and bleed-out deadline before completing Stabilize's skill exception. The active draft deliberately leaves these unresolved; old med-kit modifiers and deadlines do not fill the gap.

**Keywords:** Stabilize, Treatment, Recovery, Bleed Out, Med Kit.

### 12.5 Other Conditions and Device States

**Status: Drafted.** Provide concise entries for selected effects such as STUNNED, GRAPPLED, HIDDEN and MARKED, with application, expiry and recovery. Finish the condition list and repeated-application rules. Keep FREEZE and objective/device states distinct from conditions on fighters; do not import Fire/Poison tables wholesale.

**Keywords:** STUNNED, GRAPPLED, HIDDEN, MARKED, FREEZE, Duration.

**Sources:** [[Full Rules System v2]] §§9–10; [[Draft 2 - Keywords]]; [[Draft 2 - Skills]]; [[Draft 2 - Progress and Decisions]].

## 13 Stress Break and Rally

**Status: Drafted.** Explain battlefield pressure without introducing a parallel fear track.

### 13.1 Gaining and Applying Stress

**Status: Confirmed.** Ordinary failed Injury rolls add Stress. Stress normally caps at five and applies −1 per point to the rolling unit's rolls, including Injury; explicit skills supply exceptions. Break and Rally ignore Stress penalties. Stress transfers cannot erase overflow.

**Keywords:** Stress, Stress Cap, Shaken, Stress Transfer, Beta Blocker.

### 13.2 Break Tests

**Status: Confirmed.** At the end of the round, eligible units with two or more Stress test NRV against 15+ without a Stress penalty. Passing clears Stress; failing retains it and assigns severity by current Stress, not the die total.

**Keywords:** Break, NRV, End Phase, Severity.

### 13.3 Broken Bolting and Bugging Out

**Status: Confirmed.** Describe the recorded bands: Broken at one–two Stress, Bolting at three–four, Bugging Out at five or more. Explain next-activation pinning or forced flight and that leaving through Bugging Out is not Out of Action. Routing details belong in the shared movement procedure.

**Keywords:** Broken, Bolting, Bugging Out, Heavy Cover, Board Edge.

### 13.4 Rallying a Unit

**Status: Drafted.** Units already carrying a break condition Rally instead of Break; success clears the condition and Stress, and failure keeps both. A new Break failure does not grant an immediate Rally. Complete ordinary friendly assistance, ranges and Pinned interaction; those details currently include test-only choices.

**Keywords:** Rally, Friendly Assistance, Break Condition, Recover.

### 13.5 Stress Skills and Other Triggers

**Status: Drafted.** Integrate Implode, Rage, Inspire and other printed exceptions. Complete any environmental or campaign Stress triggers and Downed persistence. Review the identified Implode/Blowing Off Steam interaction without treating a test finding as a rule change.

**Keywords:** Implode, Rage, Inspire, Blowing Off Steam, Stress Trigger.

**Sources:** [[Full Rules System v2]] §§10–11; [[Draft 2 - Keywords]]; [[Draft 2 - Skills]]; [[Draft 2 - Progress and Decisions]].

## 14 Ready and Reactions

**Status: Drafted.** Give reaction timing its own clear procedure and diagram.

### 14.1 Gaining and Spending READY

**Status: Confirmed.** Explain gaining READY through actions or explicit grants, the one-token maximum, persistence between rounds and cancellation by movement, another regular action or a hit. Spending READY gives the permitted reaction; retaining it does not bypass Pinned restrictions.

**Keywords:** READY, Ready Action, Token, Cancellation, Reaction.

### 14.2 Reaction Triggers and Timing

**Status: Confirmed.** Use eligible enemy action start/end triggers in LOS and range. Start-trigger reactions precede the action; end-trigger reactions follow it. A pre-action attack that Pins or Downs the actor cancels the declared action and spends its cost. Reactions do not normally trigger reactions.

**Keywords:** Start Trigger, End Trigger, LOS, Declared Action, Interrupt.

### 14.3 Permitted Reactions

**Status: Drafted.** List attacks, Charge, Interact, Hack, Deploy, Skill, Move, Hide and Dodge where legal. Complete the underlying action definitions, core Dodge and exceptional costs before presenting every entry as playable.

**Keywords:** Reaction Attack, Charge, Move, Hide, Dodge, Skill Reaction.
