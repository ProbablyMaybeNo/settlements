## Attack Dice

### 1.5 · Attack Dice — the multi-shot axis

Attack Dice are defined in the greater rules system under [[Full Rules System v1#15 · Weapons — Basic Weapon System]]. Every weapon defaults to one Attack Die. A weapon with additional Attack Dice rolls one attack per die for one Action; the burst inflicts no more than one Wound, and surplus hits become Stress under the current combat rules.

## Armour

### 3 · Armor

Armour is defined with the weapon and roster equipment rules in [[Full Rules System v1#15 · Weapons — Basic Weapon System]]. Armour modifies the Injury roll, not the attack roll.

| Armour | Injury modifier | Credits |
|---|---:|---:|
| None / Thick clothing | 0 | **0** |
| Light | −1 | **10** |
| Heavy | −2 | **20** |
