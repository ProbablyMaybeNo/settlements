# Structural Collapse

As an Action, pass a STR test to destroy up to a 4"-wide section of adjacent Breachable terrain. On success, every fighter within 2" becomes Pinned and gains the normal 1 Stress from Pinned; on failure, the terrain remains and you become Pinned. *(Breacher · Area controller)*
