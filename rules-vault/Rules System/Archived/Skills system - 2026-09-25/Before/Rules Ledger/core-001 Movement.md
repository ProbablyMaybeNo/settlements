# core-001 · Movement
> **core** · status **draft** · v0.1

**Parent phase:** [[Settlements/Rules System/Archived/Movement]]

## Text
- **Move slot:** up to **MOV"** (baseline 6"). Cannot split around the Action.
- **Sprint** (both slots): up to **2× MOV"** — nothing else.
- **Charge** (both slots): up to **2× MOV"** into Engagement, then free melee at **+1** (see [[Settlements/Rules System/Archived/Melee]] / [[core-003 Melee]]).
- **Low leap:** obstacle under **2"** tall — no test, costs **2"** Move.
- **Athletic traversal** (climb, gap/jump, vault ≥2", swim): Move-slot **AGI** test vs **7+**. Fail = stop / fall short / hazard (see [[Settlements/Rules System/Archived/Movement#Terrain movement]]).
- **Difficult** ground costs double Move ([[Settlements/Rules System/Archived/Terrain]]). **Impassable** blocks entry unless Climbable / Breached.
- **Disengage** (Move slot): move up to MOV"; you **keep your Action** (no Charge); cannot end within 1" of any enemy; each previous Engager gets a free swing at **−2**.
- Measuring: pre-measure allowed; base edge to base edge; round down ([[Settlements/Rules System/Archived/Rules Engine]]).

*Graduated from [[Settlements/Rules System/Archived/Movement]]. See [[Settlements/Rules System/Archived/Rules System MOC]].*
