# Trap Relay

As an Action, pass an INT test to link up to three armed electronic traps within 6". Until your next activation, when one linked trap triggers normally, trigger one other linked trap whose normal conditions are met. A trap triggered by Trap Relay cannot trigger this skill, and each linked trap can trigger at most once. *(Trapper · Area control)*
