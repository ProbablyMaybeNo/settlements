## Working rules / decisions

### Advances
Surviving units earn **Advances** after a battle — from kills, glorious deeds, surviving, and objectives held. Spend an Advance on:
- **+1 stat** (up to the campaign cap), or
- **+1 skill** (up to your rank's slot cap — see [[Skill Paths]]), or
- **heal a lasting scar** (see [[Campaign]]).

### Caps (anti-snowball)
- Campaign cap is **+6** (Tier 3). Start caps (+2 / +4) lift as a unit earns Advances — most plateau around +4–5, a rare few reach +6.
- **WND and MOV never rise from Advances** — only from the specific [[Skill Paths|skills]] that grant them.
- Skills stay driven by stat points (every 2 = 1 skill) — growth makes a unit *better*, not higher-*ranked*.

### Promotion
- **Rank never rises automatically.** You may **promote** a veteran into an **open** rank slot between battles (e.g. a fallen Leader's seat), gaining that rank's Orders + skill slot.
- Multiple veterans can be *leader-calibre* — a deep bench for a brutal game — but [[List Building]] caps decide how many hold rank at once. A promoted Leader still **fights**.
