
## TESTING PACKET

(The games Narrative will be revealed closer to it's release and is kept deliberately sparse during testing)

Firstly, if you are reading this you have very likely offered your time to help test and finalize the rules system for a brand new miniature skirmish game called Settlements. Your input in any shape or form is an absolutely necessity to get this game made, so thank you, I genuinely appreciate it. 
global

Settlements is a table top miniature skirmish game set in the near future during the aftermath of a cataclysmic global event. Battles are fought between everyday citizens, not super heroes, struggling to survive in a world torn apart.

Mission: Realistic, bloody battles fought, on boards filled with dense interactive terrain, between crews of everyday citizens struggling to survive in a post-apocalyptic reality. Battles are multi-faceted and nuanced offering players a variety of objectives, each with different risks and rewards, creating a genuine tension by forcing players to choose what is most important to them while constantly assessing the evolving situation to determine if the risk is truly worth the reward. In the Settlements universe survival is the exception and death the rule. 

Tenents:

- Citizens not Super Heroes - Unskilled fighters, low wound counts, and swingy dice rolls promote a sense of realim in which crews are forced to think outside the box
