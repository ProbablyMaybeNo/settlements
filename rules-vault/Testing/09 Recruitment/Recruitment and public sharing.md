# Recruitment and public sharing

## Summary

Recruit a useful mix of testers and share a small, versioned ask in an appropriate community.

^testing-summary

## Recruit for the question

Use local miniature clubs, game stores, friends-of-friends, design meetups and reciprocal online groups. Designer friends help diagnose early systems; fresh skirmish players test learnability; experienced competitive players search for exploitable strategies. Include occasional/returning hobbyists and different reading/access needs where they fit the intended audience. Keep experience context rather than assuming everyone should like the same game.

Current routes checked: [Weekly Online Playtesting](https://www.breakmygame.com/weekly-online-playtesting) coordinates online events through Discord, with its own roles and posting rules. [FAQ](https://protospiel.online/faq/) uses reciprocal testing and has its own supported-platform policy. Recheck schedules/eligibility at booking time; this workspace has not joined, booked or posted anywhere.

## Reddit / Discord: useful, but no universal permission

Public beta rules and community feedback have precedent in miniatures development ([Firefight Playtest: New rules and Asterians now available](https://www.manticgames.com/news/firefight-playtest-new-rules-and-asterians-now-available/)). That is evidence of a method, not permission to post in every group. A transparent, relevant, specific invitation is preferable to repeated promotional links. Official Reddit policy prohibits mass/repetitive unsolicited engagement: [Spam](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam).

The particular subreddit rule pages did not reveal their rules to the research tool. Treat r/tabletopgamedesign, r/BoardgameDesign and r/miniatureskirmishes as **candidates to inspect**, not approved posting destinations. Read pinned threads, flair, self-promotion and AI-content rules; use the appropriate feedback channel or ask moderators if uncertain. Discord communities also differ; do not send unsolicited recruitment DMs.

## What to share

- A one-sentence pitch, current development stage and the exact question.
- A screenshot or clear board image, player count, required models/materials and the expected session window.
- A versioned, readable PDF with fixed crews and all the rules that pack needs.
- Direct access with minimal friction where possible, plus one clear feedback/booking route.
- A frank note that prices and broad balance are still being tested.

Start with a small guided/unguided wave, not a request that strangers test every rule and catalogue. A public download is not a completed test; track enquiries, bookings, played sessions and returned reports separately. Do not require a marketing signup to provide criticism.

Fill placeholders and verify the link before posting. Choose wording appropriate to the community, offer reciprocal testing and respond to criticism as research, not a sales objection.
