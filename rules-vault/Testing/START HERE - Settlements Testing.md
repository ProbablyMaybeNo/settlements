# Settlements Testing

## Summary

Plan, run, record, analyse and retest Settlements battles without changing approved rules.

^testing-summary

> [!important] Research and testing workspace
> These are research notes, proposed protocols and test resources. They are not additional game rules. No human sessions have been recorded here and no balance conclusion is implied. Approved rules remain in the Master Rules System.

## Start with one question

Use **focused tests plus full battles**. Short fixtures isolate a problem; complete battles reveal interaction, pacing and objectives. Later, fresh players test the packet without the designer rescuing them. Human play and simulation answer different questions.

1. Read [[Settlements/Testing/01 Research/Research findings|Research findings]] and [[Settlements/Testing/02 Plan/Testing programme|Testing programme]].
2. Pick at most two questions from [[Settlements/Testing/03 Modules/Modules and priorities|Modules and priorities]].
3. Use [[Settlements/Testing/02 Plan/Host protocol|Host protocol]] and [[Settlements/Testing/07 Printables/Print and share manifest|Print and share manifest]].
4. Collect one five-question player sheet; the host records the detailed data.
5. Transcribe to [[Settlements/Testing/05 Data/Data dictionary and recording|Data dictionary and recording]]; keep narrative notes in Sessions.
6. Follow [[Settlements/Testing/06 Analysis/Feedback to decision to retest|Feedback to decision to retest]]; only an explicit decision changes approved rules.
