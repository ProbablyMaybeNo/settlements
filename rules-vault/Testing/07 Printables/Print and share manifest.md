## Versions and status

The starter packet is the November 11 working test fixture. Hold the Block is a packet-specific scenario, not an adopted universal mission. Fixed crews have identical capabilities but are not priced rosters. New worksheet/aids text is operational reference, not an amendment to approved rules.

File hashes and copied-source provenance are in `asset-manifest.json`. Preserve each distributed revision. If a current owner changes, regenerate affected aids/packet and issue a new revision; do not silently replace what testers already used without recording it.

## Print check

- [ ] Black on white, readable at the actual print size.
- [ ] 1-inch calibration line measures 25.4 mm.
- [ ] Blast / smoke are 3 / 5 inches in **diameter**.
- [ ] Objective template is 6 inches across; use three copies or mark a 3-inch radius from each centre.
- [ ] Paper unit markers measure 28 mm and have facing arrows; agree normal standing body volume for LOS.
- [ ] Token labels remain distinct in monochrome; do not rely on red/blue ink to identify crews.

## Distribution gate

Keep as working/pilot material until a dry run and one fresh unguided group can set up, play and score without a missing essential answer. Complete [[Settlements/Testing/02 Plan/Packet and blind testing|Packet and blind testing]] before a broad public wave. These resources were prepared; no test has been scheduled or reported.
