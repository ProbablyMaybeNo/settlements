Our printable forms are original Settlements tools. Early play does not need purchase-intent questions, demographic questionnaires or every mechanic rated after every game. Do not turn a playtest into a market survey.

## How to use the new forms

Players receive **only the Player Exit Sheet** after play. The host chooses the relevant prep, round or incident sheet. The focused-mechanic tally sheet is for an observer in a dedicated test; omit it during an ordinary first game if it would distract the players.

The blind/unguided checklist belongs to the observer, not the learner: showing it first can teach the answers we want to measure.
