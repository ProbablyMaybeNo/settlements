## Reviewed sources

All access checks below are dated **11 November 2026**. Historical development accounts are not current recruitment invitations. Summaries are paraphrases.

### R01 - Choose the right playtest method

- **Owner:** Steve Bromley / Games User Research
- **Type:** Research practitioner guidance
- **Publication date:** 2023-09-25
- **Link:** [Choose the right playtest method](https://gamesuserresearch.com/choose-the-right-playtest-method/)
- **Use and limits:** Select methods from a concrete research question. Behaviour and opinion answer different questions; observations, interviews, analytics and surveys have different strengths. This informs the split between host observations and player impressions. It is games UX guidance, not a miniature-wargame balance standard.

^source-r01

### R02 - Find usability issues in games with playtests

- **Owner:** Steve Bromley / Games User Research
- **Type:** Research practitioner guidance
- **Publication date:** 2022-05-24
- **Link:** [Find usability issues in games with playtests](https://gamesuserresearch.com/find-usability-issues-in-games-with-playtests/)
- **Use and limits:** Establish design intent, observe unexpected behaviour and ask neutral follow-ups. Teaching or intervention can change what a test measures. Usability problems and dislike are distinct. Applied here to setup, rule lookup and legal-action comprehension; severity categories below are Settlements planning choices.

^source-r02

### R03 - Creating a Playtest Feedback Form for Your Board Game

- **Owner:** Amber Humphrey / BackerKit
- **Type:** Tabletop industry guidance
- **Publication date:** 2021-03-25
- **Link:** [Creating a Playtest Feedback Form for Your Board Game](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/playtest-feedback-form/)
- **Use and limits:** Describes internal, guided and blind playtesting; recommends concise, consistent, non-leading feedback questions. Does not prescribe a universal test-game count. Its pandemic-era platform discussion is historical; current community policies were checked separately.

^source-r03

### R04 - The Conception of Viticulture: February 2012

- **Owner:** Jamey Stegmaier / Stonemaier Games
- **Type:** First-party development account
- **Publication date:** 2012-08-29
- **Link:** [The Conception of Viticulture: February 2012](https://stonemaiergames.wordpress.com/2012/08/29/the-conception-of-viticulture-february-2012/)
- **Use and limits:** An early large blind-test shipment became inefficient when initial feedback immediately required revisions. Supports starting the unguided stage with one group, fixing blocking issues, then expanding. This is one designer's experience, not a controlled study.

^source-r04

### R05 - Firefight Playtest Designer Notes: Part One

- **Owner:** Andy Sharp / Mantic Games
- **Type:** First-party miniature-wargame development account
- **Publication date:** 2021-04-19
- **Link:** [Firefight Playtest Designer Notes: Part One](https://www.manticgames.com/news/firefight-playtest-designer-notes-part-one/)
- **Use and limits:** Describes a small alpha group, public beta, version-controlled changes and remote play using Universal Battle. Importantly, online alpha play under-exercised climbing and elevated shooting. This is a concrete warning to retest 3D terrain physically; the 2021 invitation is historical.

^source-r05

### R06 - Firefight Playtest: New rules and Asterians now available

- **Owner:** Mantic Games
- **Type:** First-party public-beta account
- **Publication date:** 2021-05-10
- **Link:** [Firefight Playtest: New rules and Asterians now available](https://www.manticgames.com/news/firefight-playtest-new-rules-and-asterians-now-available/)
- **Use and limits:** Shows public rules distribution, a dedicated feedback community and updated beta rules incorporating clarifications and adjustments. It demonstrates a workable publishing pattern; it does not establish that every community accepts unsolicited PDF links.

^source-r06

### R07 - Providing Feedback on Prototypes: The WINQ

- **Owner:** Kathleen Mercury
- **Type:** Designer / educator resource directory
- **Publication date:** Undated
- **Link:** [Providing Feedback on Prototypes: The WINQ](https://www.kathleenmercury.com/providing-feedback-on-prototypes-the-winq.html)
- **Use and limits:** Provides an original feedback framework and links to several other creators' evaluation forms. Useful for choosing a form style. Her page explicitly credits the original form authors; linked forms are reference material, not a licence to reproduce them here.

^source-r07

### R08 - Game Salute Playtesting Feedback Form

- **Owner:** Game Salute; hosted by Kathleen Mercury
- **Type:** Existing two-page PDF worksheet; inspected
- **Publication date:** Undated
- **Link:** [Game Salute Playtesting Feedback Form](https://www.kathleenmercury.com/uploads/2/2/7/4/22743302/game_salute_playtest_form_-_fillable-4.pdf)
- **Use and limits:** Includes first/repeat-play context, memorable moments, strategy and rule-confusion prompts. It also asks commercial and demographic questions unnecessary for this early battle pass. Linked as an existing example; the new Settlements forms are original and shorter.

^source-r08

### R09 - Capstone playtesting report template

- **Owner:** Educational resource hosted by Brian Schrank; credited excerpts from design texts
- **Type:** Existing three-page educational worksheet; inspected
- **Publication date:** 2012
- **Link:** [Capstone playtesting report template](https://brianschrank.com/capstone/resources/Playtesting_reports_template.pdf)
- **Use and limits:** Sets out observation and structured reporting as part of a playtest. Useful as a host/report reference, with an educational rather than miniature-wargame context. Do not mistake its academic framing for an industry certification checklist.

^source-r09

### R11 - Weekly Online Playtesting

- **Owner:** Break My Game

- **Owner:** Protospiel Online
- **Type:** First-party community policies
- **Publication date:** Undated; live page checked
- **Link:** [FAQ](https://protospiel.online/faq/)
- **Use and limits:** Describes reciprocal playtesting, Discord coordination and supported virtual platforms. Its current FAQ explicitly excludes Tabletop Simulator. Use this to choose a compatible community; no endorsement of the FAQ's wider claims about a software vendor is inferred.

^source-r12

### R13 - Steam Workshop

- **Owner:** Tabletop Simulator Knowledge Base / Berserk Games
- **Type:** Official platform documentation
- **Publication date:** Undated; live page checked
- **Link:** [Steam Workshop](https://kb.tabletopsimulator.com/custom-content/steam-workshop/)
- **Use and limits:** Documents custom mod distribution and public, friends-only and hidden visibility. Multiplayer assets must be available to other players. This supports a staged remote test build; a Workshop upload alone does not recruit testers or validate the rules.

^source-r13

### R14 - Spam

- **Owner:** Reddit Help
- **Type:** Official platform policy
- **Publication date:** Updated 2026-10-30
- **Link:** [Spam](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- **Use and limits:** Prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and directs users to community-specific rules. A relevant, transparent test invitation is different from mass promotion, but permission varies by subreddit. The specific subreddit rule pages did not expose their full rules to this research tool.

^source-r14

### R15 - Confidence intervals for proportions

- **Owner:** NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook
- **Type:** Statistical reference
- **Publication date:** Undated
- **Link:** [Confidence intervals for proportions](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm)
- **Use and limits:** Provides the Wilson interval for a binary proportion. Used only for the explicitly illustrative independent win/loss calculation; repeated players, paired games, draws and multiple versions need different treatment or qualification. It does not specify how many game playtests are enough.

^source-r15
