# Research findings

## Summary

What the reviewed guidance supports, where sources differ, and how it applies to Settlements.

^testing-summary

## Findings

| Question | Research-backed direction | Settlements application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One mechanic or whole game? | Select the method from the question; observations and measurements complement each other. [Choose the right playtest method](https://gamesuserresearch.com/choose-the-right-playtest-method/) | Use a short fixture for an interaction, then a full objective battle to see its consequences. |
| What should we observe? | Compare intended behaviour with actual behaviour, not just opinions. [Find usability issues in games with playtests](https://gamesuserresearch.com/find-usability-issues-in-games-with-playtests/) | Record missed reaction windows, uncertainty, pauses, choices and recovery; ask what the player expected. |
| Should strangers learn alone? | Unguided tests can expose packet failures that a live designer hides. [The Conception of Viticulture: February 2012](https://stonemaiergames.wordpress.com/2012/08/29/the-conception-of-viticulture-february-2012/) | Start with one fresh group; fix blockers before sending the same packet to many groups. |
| Is online testing legitimate? | A commercial miniatures alpha used remote play, but missed some vertical-terrain concerns. [Firefight Playtest Designer Notes: Part One](https://www.manticgames.com/news/firefight-playtest-designer-notes-part-one/) | Remote tests help recruitment; verify real measuring, model handling and 3D LOS physically too. |
| Can public rules be shared? | Public beta PDFs and dedicated feedback groups have first-party precedent. [Firefight Playtest: New rules and Asterians now available](https://www.manticgames.com/news/firefight-playtest-new-rules-and-asterians-now-available/) | Publish a versioned test pack when it works unaided, with a specific ask and return route. |
| What forms already exist? | Designer/educator directories and printable forms are available. [Providing Feedback on Prototypes: The WINQ](https://www.kathleenmercury.com/providing-feedback-on-prototypes-the-winq.html), [Game Salute Playtesting Feedback Form](https://www.kathleenmercury.com/uploads/2/2/7/4/22743302/game_salute_playtest_form_-_fillable-4.pdf), [Capstone playtesting report template](https://brianschrank.com/capstone/resources/Playtesting_reports_template.pdf) | Use original short player forms and separate detailed host logs, rather than importing a long generic survey. |
| How many tests? | No universal count is supplied by this reviewed guidance. [Creating a Playtest Feedback Form for Your Board Game](https://www.backerkit.com/blog/playtest-feedback-form/) | Set learning and coverage gates. A session budget is planning, not proof of balance. |

## What is not a consensus

The reviewed sources are practitioner guidance, educational material and development accounts, not a representative survey of all game designers. They support a mixed, iterative approach; they do not prove a universal workflow, game-count threshold or preferred virtual tabletop. Recommendations in the Plan folder are our synthesis for this particular skirmish game.

Examples of public test requests show that sharing occurs, not that any named subreddit currently permits our proposed post. Current community rules still matter.

## Important miniature-wargame implication

Keep **terrain, scenario, experience, list strength and rules clarity** separate in analysis. A long shooting lane can make a short-range crew appear weak; a hard-to-read rule can make a strong tool seem unused; a digital board can conceal physical problems. An observation is a reason to investigate, not an immediate stat adjustment.

## Research limitations

Some current Stonemaier pages, the Playtest UK download endpoint and a GDC slide deck could not be fetched. The accessible first-party Stonemaier development account and the Playtest UK listing are identified honestly. Unread resources are not used as evidence. No full third-party worksheets are reproduced.
