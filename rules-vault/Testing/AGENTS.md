# Settlements Testing workspace

This is a research, planning, resource and evidence workspace, not an owning rules system. Use the current Master Rule Cards and Item Cards for game text. Preserve IDs, anchors and source hashes. Research recommendations, proposed experiments and tester suggestions do not amend approved rules.

No actual tests exist until real observations are entered. Keep templates, simulation results, human results and proposed decisions distinct. Raw measurement CSVs are authoritative for structured data; session notes hold narrative. Do not publish, recruit, message or upload externally without explicit authorisation. Recheck changing community policies. Edit the live vault and mirror with scripts/sync-rules.ps1. Do not hand-edit rules-vault or overwrite tester data when refreshing resources.
