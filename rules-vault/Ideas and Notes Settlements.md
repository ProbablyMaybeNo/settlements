## KEYWORDS

Impale - On a successful hit it moves through the target hitting any other unit positioned directly behind the target. Roll to hit as normak against the second target. Travels full range before stopping. 

Grappling Hook - Spend a shoot action targeting a ledge within 18" on a successful hit place an access token below the ledge. Any unit can now use the access token to move onto the ledge as if it were a ladder.

## DEPLOYABLES

### DRONES

Units with the required skill can deploy a drone during battle by spending a CORE ACTION "DEPLOY" and succeeding at a INT test. Drones can be represented on the board by a model or token using the base size found on the drone's profile. Once deployed Drones act as a "sub-unit" with their own move and core action point. Each drone type has a range in inches representing the distance the drone can move away from it's pilot or operator. If a drone is forced to go beyond that range (if their pilot is pushed or moved further away) then the drone immediately powers down causing aerial drones to go out of action immediately. Ground drones will remain in place and can be powered back up by a unit with the required skill succeeding a INT test while in base contact with the drone. In some cases units can use a TERMINAL to regain control of a powered down ground drone. A unit can only ever control one drone at a time. Drones must be purchased by it's pilot prior to the battle. Drones are persistent and will return to their pilot/operator if it is still functional at the end of a battle. Unless stated otherwise drones cannot capture, hold, or contest, an objective.

*Aerial Drones* - Aerial drones all have the FLY keyword and an 8" movement allowing them to ignore terrain while moving across the board. Units with the fly keyword can't be targeted by melee attacks unless they "hover." Hitting an aerial drone with a ranged attack suffers a -2 modifier. Aerial drones must stay within 18" of their pilot. They have no armor. If they are successfully hit by an enemies shot they go down and are removed from the battle. HOVER - The drone lowers its altitude and moves across that board like a regular unit capable of taking advantage of LOS blocking terrain. Switching from fly to hover takes up half of the drones movement

Base Stats - move 8" / w 1 / FLY, DRONE, RANGE, HOVER, FRAGILE

FLY - Ignore terrain when moving across the board.
DRONE - an uncrewed aircraft, vessel, or vehicle guided by remote control or onboard computers. 
RANGE - Distance that the drone can travel away from it's pilot or a friendly hacked terminal.
HOVER - Defensive capability allowing the Drone to decrease it's altitude. Drone acts like a regular ground unit regarding cover, LOS, etc. Can be targeted by melee attacks and removes it's -2 to hit modifier.
FRAGILE - Successful hits cause this unit to go down. No injury roll needed.

Unlike aerial drones ground drones are built to withstand enemy while protecting their vital components because of this drone operators can often repair ground drones if they go down.

*FIELD MECHANICS*

### DEPLOYABLES

Deployable are mines, traps, turrets, and any other equipment placed by a unit with the FIELD MECHANICS skill. They always require a CORE ACTION "DEPLOY" and a successful INT test. Deployables remain active until destroyed, disarmed, or the same unit deploys another deployable. A unit can never have more than one active deployable. Deployables are bought from traders and can be equipped on any unit but only used by units with the FIELD MECHANIC skill. Deplorables still in use at the end of a battle are automatically collected unless the crew withdraws in which case they are lost forever and must be re-bought. Deployables cannot be repaired if destroyed but they can be disarmed.

**Seeker Mines** - Spend a DEPLOY CORE ACTION to place a seeker mine within 3." Once deployed the seeker mine will activate at the end of the units turn. Once activated any enemy unit that enters the seeker mines 6" perimeter will become it's target. The seeker mine will respond immediatley by rapidly moving toward the target and detonating when within 1". Seeker mines do not need LOS and will take the fastest route possible to reach their target. A seeker mine will travel up to 12" to reach it's target and can scale walls, move through open windows, fall from any height. If it cannot reach it's target via the shortest path possible without exceeding 12" of movement it will detonate move as close to the target as possible and detonate. 

Seeker mines detonate with a 3" blast at DMG 2

**Sentry Turret** - A stationary assault rifle deployed by a unit after succeeding an INT test. The sentry turret using the same profile as an assault rifle. It does not have actions like a regular unit. Instead it counts as having a persistent ALERT token allowing it to resolve multiple reaction per round. In order to deploy sentry turrets they must be light weight and cannot have a large power source causing the turret to power down at the start of the units next activation. Once powered down they can be reactivated with a successeful INT test by a unit with the required skills. WARNING: Enemy hackers can take control of powered down sentry turrets using a terminal within range. 

Assault rifle profile / 1 w / 2 armor / 180 degree firing arc. 

**Cloaking Field** - Deploy with a successful INT test. While active creates a 5" area of effect around the token. Friendly units moving through the field cannot be targeted by ALERT reactions. Including seeker mines and sentry turrets. Remains active until destroyed, disarmed, or another deployable is set but the same unit. 

**Smoke Emitter** - Deploy on a successful INT test. Place a 3" smoke token anywhere within 2" of the unit. Unlike smoke grenades a smoke emitter remains active until it is destroyed, disarmed, or the same unit uses another deploy action.
