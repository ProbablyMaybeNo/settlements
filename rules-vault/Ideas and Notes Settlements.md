
## SKILLS

SELF HEAL - Allows this unit to REVIVE itself with an equipped med kit. 

HOUDINI - Can hide even when within LOS of an enemy as long as they are 6"+ away from the unit. Must still be in cover. 

AGILE - Create a skill that allows non-agile weapons to have the agile keyword

## KEYWORDS

Impale - On a successful hit it moves through the target hitting any other unit positioned directly behind the target. Roll to hit as normak against the second target. Travels full range before stopping. 

Grappling Hook - Spend a shoot action targeting a ledge within 18" on a successful hit place an access token below the ledge. Any unit can now use the access token to move onto the ledge as if it were a ladder. 
## SPECIAL WEAPONS

*MELEE*

Grapple Gauntlet - 1h melee weapon, 1 ATK 3 DMG allows the wielder to use the Grapple skill
Breach Hammer - Use sledge hammer rules, +4 to breach tests.
Retractable Whip - 1 ATK 2 DMG Range Melee to 8"

*RANGE*

Harpoon Cannon - 1 ATK DMG 3 Range 18" (Impale, Grappling Hook)



## EQUIPMENT

Climbing Gear - Create a an access point that spans 1 level.
Med-kit - +4 to revival tests
Utility Vest - Add an additional Equipment slot
Lift Harness - Gives the unit the ability to carry at full speed
Stims - +2 Move for 1 activation
