
## SKILLS

SELF HEAL - Allows this unit to REVIVE itself with an equipped med kit. 

HOUDINI - Can hide even when within LOS of an enemy as long as they are 6"+ away from the unit. Must still be in cover. 

AGILE - Create a skill that allows non-agile weapons to have the agile keyword

Impale - On a successful hit it moves through the target hitting any other unit positioned directly behind the target. Roll to hit as normak against the second target.
