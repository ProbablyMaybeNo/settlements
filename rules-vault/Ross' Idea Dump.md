GREATER GOALS

One of the main goals of settlements is to create a new system that will
allow players to experience campaign play without the requirement of
finding a group of players to meet consistently over a 6 month or however long
period of time. 

Instead players will all use an app to build and grow their settlements and crews
individually within a larger community setting.

Players will join a faction, submit thier battle reports, update their settlement and
crew growth aftyer each battle, and participate in a community wide battle for control
of a region displayed on a live updating interactive map.

Players can fight one off battles against a random opponent and still use the battles
outcome, items looted, etc, to grow their own crew and settlement. 

Balance will be regulated by player set crew ratings for each battle meaning that
each player will bring the same amount of points regarding their units, equipment, etc,
to their battles the only difference is that a veteran player might have some more "fun"
unique, rare, equipment, weapons, etc, that they are using but the game should still remain balanced.

In order to achieve this we must prioritize creating a well thought out, tested, and balanced,
rating/points system which we can use to appropriatley price every aspect of a players crew
so that battles are truly balanced even if one player has a lot more games under their belt
than their opponent. 

Approaching Settlements this way solves the major problem I believe many narrative skirmish games face.
1. Narrative games are often based on campaign play. Campiagn play usually requires a group of people to comit
a certain amount of time on a regular scheduled basis consistently for months...this often doesn't happen and groups fall apart before the campaign reaches it's half way point. 
2. Campaigns require a GM or arbitrator to set up, organize, and manage the campaign over several months.
3. Campaigns require a pre-existing network or community of people who are all willing to invest time into learning the rules and building/painting a crew...the settlement system allows players to play against anyone the only moat is that they live close enough to play...everything else will be taken care of by the Settlements system and the accompanying app.  ^oQCqWNbV
