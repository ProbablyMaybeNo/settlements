# AMBUSH

After a successful CHARGE begun HIDDEN, test AGI. Pass: one melee weapon makes an UNANSWERED attack with +4 to hit and injure; the second weapon attacks normally. Fail: no bonuses; the target may spend an unused ACTION to make an UNANSWERED attack first, followed by your normal attack if still able.
