# Stand Your Ground

Once per game, when an ally within 3" fails a Break test, it takes no Break state (it stays merely Shaken) and you gain 3 Stress. *(Anchor · Rescuer)*

*Record. These properties are the source of truth — `records.py` regenerates the table in [[Skill Paths]] from them.*
