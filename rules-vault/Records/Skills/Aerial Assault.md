# AERIAL ASSAULT

From a structure at least 3 inches high, select an enemy within charge range. Roll to charge, then test AGI. If both succeed, move into engagement and make an UNANSWERED attack with +1 to hit and injure per full 2 inches of starting height above ground, maximum +4/+4.
