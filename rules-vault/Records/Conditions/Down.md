# Down

prone and out of the fight; no normal actions. Only **ranged / hazard** hits leave a unit Down — a **melee** kill goes straight to **Out of Action**. **Heavy cover vs ranged unless in the open**; a **melee / engaged** attack **auto-hits** to finish it (Injury roll still made, a pass = Out), but **ranged attacks resolve normally**. Stabilize by the end of its next activation or it bleeds out — full rules in [[Damage]]. **Keeps its Stress** and takes **no Break tests while Down**, resuming them on recovery ([[Morale#Stress and Down — persistence]]).
