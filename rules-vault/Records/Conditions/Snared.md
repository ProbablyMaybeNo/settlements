# Snared

caught by a clamp, wire or pit (a [[Deployables]] trap). **Cannot Move, Charge, Sprint or Disengage.** On its activation it may spend its **Action** on a **STR test (7+)** to break free (nat 1 fails, it stays). A device-sourced negative condition, so its first application gives **+1 Stress**. Persists until it breaks free or the device is cleared. *(Kin to **Grappled**, but no grappler holds it — the terrain does.)*
