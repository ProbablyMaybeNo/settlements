# Prone

knocked flat by a fall, slip, or being forced off a ledge (*not* an injury). **Heavy cover vs ranged unless in the open**; cannot Shoot, Charge or Sprint. **Standing up costs the whole activation** (Move + Action). Not bleeding and not auto-hit — that line is what separates Prone from Down.
