# Modifier cap.

However many conditions a unit carries, the total modifier on any single roll never exceeds **−3** (or +3). Conditions past the cap still matter — they still restrict actions and still have to be cleared.

*Record. These properties are the source of truth — `records.py` regenerates the table in [[Conditions]] from them.*
