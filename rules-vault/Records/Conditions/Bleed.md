# Bleed

each End Phase, the unit loses **1 WND** unless treated. Treating = an Action + **INT test (7+)** by the unit or an adjacent friendly, **−2 without a Med-Kit**; a **Medic** ([[Skill Paths]]) treats automatically. **At WND 1 — i.e. almost everyone — Bleed is a two-round death clock:** it drops you Down at the next End Phase, and Down + Bleed bleeds out. It is the harshest condition in the game by a wide margin, which is why **Bleeding** is the priciest weapon payload and why a **Med-Kit** earns its points.
