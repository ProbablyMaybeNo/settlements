# Fight

*skill-induced only* (e.g. Fanatic, [[Skill Paths]]): instead of cracking, the unit must move by the shortest route toward the nearest visible enemy on its next activation and attack it if able. It cannot Hide, detour for cover, or move away from that enemy.
