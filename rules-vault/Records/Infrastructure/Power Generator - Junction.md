# Power Generator / Junction

*Utilities · enabler*. The optional macro-toggle: while off, all infrastructure in its zone is **Powered Down**. Operating the Generator (hack or manual) powers the zone on or off. It can't be destroyed — deny it by **Overloading** its terminal (an interrupt).
