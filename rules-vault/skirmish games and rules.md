

## Mad Dogs with Guns
Mad Dogs with Guns is a Prohibition era gangster skirmish game, along the lines of stuff like Necromunda, Mordheim or any other of the dozens of warband campaign games that exist out there. The core system is decent and basic, but what makes it sing are the campaign rules.

It has rules for owning and fighting over different kinds of turf ( speakeasies, gambling dens, brothels) each type having several basic ones that give different bonuses, and then a single unique, named one that gives that bonus plus extras. That too is neat, but not unknown.

Where it gets devilishly fun is that the gangs can also use their income to begin bribing the press, police, judges, and politicians to subvert the levers of society in the city to their ends. If you have key figures in your gang's pocket, you get different sorts of considerations. To make it even more fun, as different gangs compete in their attempts to bribe these authority figures, the crooked SOBs get greedier! The system includes rules for the price of bribes going up over the course of the campaign! Who says corruption doesn't pay?
