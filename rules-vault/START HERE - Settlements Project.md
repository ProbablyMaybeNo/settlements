## Editing

Procedure text belongs to its Rule Card; an item’s profile belongs to its Item Card. Master and chapter notes embed those owners. Edit the source, not generated catalogue rows. Preserve IDs, anchors, feedback and semantic dates.

**DRAFTED** means Ross has confirmed the rule sufficiently for testing. It does not mean balanced, playtested or ready for publication. The catalogue prices remain deferred for the pricing pass.

## Parents and families

The live workspace uses Section → Mechanic → Rule. Parent notes and the canvas retain their stable identities and summary anchors. Their progress is derived from the active rules.

## Archive

The archive is preserved for recovery and design history, not an alternative playable rule set. Unapproved ideas must be reviewed before they return to the active rules.
