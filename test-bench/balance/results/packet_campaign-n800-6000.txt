Both crews built by the same rule; the weaker one simply has less Rating to
spend. This is the curve the campaign model needs and it is MEASURED, not assumed.

  deficit       A rating  B rating    A win%
       0%           1000      1000     49.3%
      10%           1000       900     59.2%
      20%           1000       800     49.1%
      30%           1000       700     89.2%
      40%           1000       600     79.7%
      50%           1000       500     91.8%

====================================================================================================
STAGE 2 - T11: DOES CUTTING PER-HEAD UPKEEP REMOVE THE ONLY CONTINUOUS BRAKE?
(6000 campaigns x 20 battles each)
====================================================================================================
'Rating gap' = how much more Crew Rating the stronger settlement can field at
season end. The Crew Rating cap is 1000, so a gap of ~0 means the cap is doing
the braking on its own and no upkeep is needed. 'Idle wealth' is unspendable
surplus — the symptom of an economy with no sink.

  configuration                             rating gap   p90 gap  roster gap  idle wealth  under-cap
  packet as written (no upkeep)                     63       150        -0.0         2199       6.51
  + per-head upkeep 10/head                         69       140        -0.1         2115       4.66
  + per-head upkeep 25/head                         91       255         0.7         2124      12.50
  + per-head upkeep 50/head                        122       315         0.6         2141      18.95
  no upkeep, tighter lot (6 structures)             68       150        -0.1         2081       6.44
  no upkeep, bigger lot (15 structures)             63       150        -0.1         1759       6.50
