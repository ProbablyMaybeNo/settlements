The burst

A burst rolls every die at once, the attacker applies one Injury result, and inflicts at most 1 WND however many pass. Every other die that hit converts to +1 Stress — ruled 2026-08-29.

Every system, this build

The same fighter and the same target, resolved seven ways. Down is what changes the board; Pin is what feeds the Stress economy.

Floor and ceiling

The worst shot in the game against the best-protected target, and the best shot against the worst — the two ends every system has to place. Ratio is how much being good at shooting is worth.

Saturation ladder

The hit roll has to tell 13 net states apart — stat +0…+6 crossed with modifiers −3…+3. Any two states sharing a probability are mechanically the same state, and no amount of tuning separates them. Collapsed rows are marked in rust.

Firefight — Monte Carlo

Two symmetric squads, everyone shoots once a round, then an End Phase runs Break tests. Fixed geometry — no movement, terrain, reactions or Stabilize — so only the delta between systems is meaningful, never the absolute numbers.

A second injury bar

If the injury roll carried a severity tier — a higher bar above the wound bar — how often would it fire? What a severe result does is not ruled, so this reports frequency only.

Engine — nat 1 always fails, nat 20 (or nat 10 on d10) always succeeds · modifier cap ±3 · a hit that fails to wound becomes Pinned +1 Stress · a burst inflicts at most 1 WND, surplus hits convert to Stress
Method — outcome probabilities are exact full enumeration over the die's faces, never sampled. Only the firefight tab is Monte Carlo, and it is fixed-geometry.
Not modelled — movement, terrain, reactions, Dodge, payloads, Stabilize, bleed-out, Fate. Costs are unchanged: every price in the catalogue was measured under 1d10 vs 7+.
